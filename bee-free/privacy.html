Bee Free — Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Bee Free: Hive Escape Puzzle is a puzzle game made by Noqyris. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and who else is involved.

What Bee Free itself collects

The app has no account and no login. Your progress, stars, honey, power-ups, daily quests and settings are stored only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app. If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled on your device by iOS; no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere for them.

Since version 1.3 the game can send us game statistics about how it is played, but only after it knows your age, never if you are under 16, and in Europe only if you allow it. The section "Game statistics (Noqyris)" below says exactly what they are.

Advertising

Bee Free is free and shows ads served through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource), which runs an auction between ad networks — Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange, both run by Unity Technologies. To show ads, tailor them, measure them and prevent fraud, Unity and the ad networks it works with may collect and process, as independent controllers:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA — only if you allow tracking)
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • approximate location, such as the country, derived from your IP address
  • ad data, such as which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them
  • diagnostic data about how the ad software is running

This data is handled under Unity's privacy policy.

Before any ads: your age, then your answer

No advertising software starts before you have answered. The first time you open the game, after a short welcome that links these terms and this policy, Bee Free asks how old you are; under 16 there are no ads at all (see "Children"). What comes next depends on where your phone is, read from its time zone:

  • In the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Bee Free asks its own question about ads. It says who shows the ads, what data Unity receives and why, and has two equal answers, Accept and Decline. If you accept, Bee Free then asks for tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown, but without your personal data: Unity is told that you did not consent, your advertising identifier is not used, Apple's prompt is not shown, and Unity is also told not to sell or share your data.
  • Everywhere else (Serbia included), Bee Free asks no question of its own and shows no screen of its own before Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Ads are shown whichever you choose; only if you allow tracking may they use your advertising identifier and be tailored to you, and otherwise Unity is also told not to sell or share your data.

If you declined ads in an earlier version of Bee Free, that answer stays: the ads you now see do not use your personal data, and the game tells you so once.

You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & data → Privacy & ads: the "Personalised ads" switch everywhere (off, ads are shown without your personal data), and in Europe "Change my answer"; elsewhere it also leads to iOS Settings → Bee Free → Allow Tracking.

Purchases

Bee Free offers optional in-app purchases: Remove Ads, honey packs, power-up packs, a limited-time Welcome Pack and the Honey Pot. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the app never sees your payment details. What you bought is recorded on your device and read back from Apple, so Restore Purchases works on a new device.

Remove Ads stops banner and between-level ads for good. Optional reward videos — the ones you start yourself, such as "Watch ad for +3 moves" — stay available, so the advertising software may still run after the purchase if you agreed to ads, and Unity LevelPlay processes data as described above whenever it does.

Reminders

Daily reminders are off until you turn them on (the game asks iOS for permission only when you tap "Remind me" or the Settings switch). They are local notifications about your daily gift and quests, at most one a day for about a week after you last played. Turn them off at any time in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

The game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; they are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

When they are sent

  • Nothing is stored or sent until the game knows your age (the same question it asks before any ads, when you first open the game). Until then, what happens in the game waits in memory only and is dropped if you close the game.
  • Under 16: never. The game sends no statistics at all on that device, and the Settings switch for them is not shown.
  • 16 or over, in the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: only after you tap "Allow" on the game's own "Game statistics" card, which has two equal answers and comes after the question about ads. Before "Allow" nothing is stored or sent.
  • 16 or over, elsewhere: on, until you turn them off in Settings.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier, not a device ID, and it is never given to anyone, including our ad network (Unity LevelPlay).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, levels and attempts, tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier, your age or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game asks you before anything is sent and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow".
  • In other countries: our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings → Privacy & data, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

Children

Bee Free is for players of all ages, children included. Since version 1.2, before anything to do with ads, the game asks once how old you are. If you are under 16, the advertising SDK never starts on that device: there is no consent question, no tracking prompt and no ads, and no advertising data leaves the device. Since version 1.3 the same answer also keeps the game statistics off: under 16 the game sends no statistics at all. Your answer is kept only on your device and is never sent. Purchases go through Apple, with its Ask to Buy and parental controls.

Your choices

  • Change your ad choice in the game: Settings → Privacy & data → Privacy & ads ("Personalised ads", and in Europe "Change my answer").
  • Turn the game statistics off, or delete them: Settings → Privacy & data → Game statistics and Delete my data.
  • Report an ad that is inappropriate or not suitable for your age: Settings → Privacy & data → Report an ad opens an email to us, with the game's version filled in.
  • Turn off tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop banner and between-level ads.
  • Delete the app to erase everything it has stored on your device.
