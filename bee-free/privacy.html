Bee Free — Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Bee Free: Hexa Escape Puzzle is a puzzle game made by Noqyris. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and who else is involved.

What Bee Free itself collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no login and no server of its own. Your progress, stars, honey, power-ups, daily quests and settings are stored only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app. If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled on your device by iOS; no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Advertising

Bee Free is free and shows ads served through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource), which runs an auction between ad networks, including Unity Ads. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Unity and the ad networks it works with may collect and process, as independent controllers:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA — only if you allow tracking)
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • approximate location, such as the country, derived from your IP address
  • ad data, such as which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them
  • diagnostic data about how the ad software is running

This data is handled under Unity's privacy policy.

Your consent comes first

Before any advertising software starts, Bee Free asks for your permission. If you decline, the advertising software is never started: every level still plays, but there are no ads at all and no "watch an ad" offers. You can change your answer at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.

On iOS, Bee Free also asks for tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared; ads you agreed to may still be shown, but they are less relevant to you.

Purchases

Bee Free offers optional in-app purchases: Remove Ads, honey packs, power-up packs, a limited-time Welcome Pack and the Honey Pot. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the app never sees your payment details. What you bought is recorded on your device and read back from Apple, so Restore Purchases works on a new device.

Remove Ads stops banner and between-level ads for good. Optional reward videos — the ones you start yourself, such as "Watch ad for +3 moves" — stay available, so the advertising software may still run after the purchase if you agreed to ads, and Unity LevelPlay processes data as described above whenever it does.

Reminders

Daily reminders are off until you turn them on (the game asks iOS for permission only when you tap "Remind me" or the Settings switch). They are local notifications about your daily gift and quests, at most one a day for about a week after you last played. Turn them off at any time in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Children

Bee Free is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Change your ad consent in the game: Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Turn off tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop banner and between-level ads.
  • Delete the app to erase everything it has stored on your device.
