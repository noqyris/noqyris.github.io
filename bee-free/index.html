Hive Escape Puzzle — support and questions.

About the game

The hive is sealed: bees leave only through its doors on the rim. Tap a bee to turn it, drag it to aim it, hold it to fly it straight the way it faces. With a clear path to a door it escapes; if something is in the way it bumps back, and the flight still costs a move.

The catch is honey: every cell a bee flies over turns to honey, and a bee that flies into honey stops in it — and collects it. So the order decides everything: who goes first, which way, and where. In later chapters the doors have colours, and a bee only fits the door of its own colour. 300 levels, each checked by a solver: there is always a way out.

Questions

How do I remove the ads?

Buy Remove Ads in the game's shop. One purchase removes the banner and the ads between levels for good. Reward videos you start yourself (extra moves, double honey) stay available, always optional.

I paid, and I still see ads.

Tap Restore Purchases in the game, on the same Apple Account you bought with. If that does not help, write to us.

I'm stuck.

Use a Hint: it shows the next move of a winning line. Honey Cleaner wipes the honey off a bee's path, Undo takes back your last move, and the restart button starts the level again. If the stars above the board say the board can no longer be won, restart: every level has a solution.

How do I get three stars?

Free every bee with the level's minimum number of moves — no bumps and no needless stops in honey.

Personalised ads, tracking and game statistics

Version 1.3: Settings → Privacy & data. "Privacy & ads" has the "Personalised ads" switch (off, the ads you see are not personalised); "Game statistics" can be turned off, and "Delete my data" deletes them. Version 1.2: Settings → Privacy & ads opens the question about ads again; 1.2 has no game statistics. Your version is shown in the game's Settings. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

What does a player under 16 get?

The game asks your age once, before anything to do with ads. Under 16, version 1.3 shows ads that are not personalised and are marked for a child, with no question about ads, no tracking prompt and no game statistics; version 1.2 shows no ads at all. The Privacy Policy says exactly what is sent in each version.

Do I need the internet?

Not to play — every level is in the app and the game works offline. The internet is needed only for ads and purchases, and in version 1.3 for game statistics, if they are on.

Do I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Progress is saved only on your device. Remove Ads comes back with Restore Purchases after a reinstall.
