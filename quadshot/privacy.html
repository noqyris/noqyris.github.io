Privacy Policy — Quadshot

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · applies to Quadshot 1.5.3 and 1.5.4

Quadshot (“the app”) is a single-player arcade game published by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). This policy explains what data leaves your device, who receives it, and what you can turn off.

Two versions, one policy. Version 1.5.3 is the one on the App Store until the 1.5.4 update reaches you, and the two handle ads and data differently. Where they differ, this policy says which version each part is about; everything without a version applies to both. iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage (iPad Storage on an iPad) → Quadshot shows the version you have. In short: version 1.5.3 sends nothing to us, and there a Decline means no ads at all; version 1.5.4 sends game statistics to us (in the consent zone, defined below, only if you allow it), and there a Decline means that LevelPlay is told you do not consent to personalised ads.
What version 1.5.4 changes: the first time you open it, Quadshot shows a short welcome screen, right after the splash, asking you to accept the Terms of Use and to read this Privacy Policy. The consent zone is the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, decided from your phone’s time zone; a time zone that names no place (such as UTC) or cannot be read counts as inside it. There Quadshot then asks its own ads question, with equal Accept and Decline buttons, unless it already has your answer or you own Remove Ads; Decline no longer turns ads off as it did in 1.5.3; LevelPlay is told instead that you do not consent and that your data must not be sold or shared. Outside the consent zone Quadshot asks no question of its own (1.5.3 asked it in every country). Apple’s tracking prompt can follow, with no screen of ours in front of it: outside the consent zone, and inside it only after Accept — unless iOS already has your answer, iOS lets no apps ask, you declined Quadshot’s ads question (in an earlier version, or in the consent zone), or you own Remove Ads. iOS may then ask about notifications, for the optional reminders. Version 1.5.4 also sends game statistics to us (Noqyris), so we can see how the game is played and fix what does not work (see Game statistics (Noqyris) below); in the consent zone nothing is sent until you allow it, on a card that comes later, on the main menu. Settings → PRIVACY & ADS opens a page with a Personalised ads switch that works in every country, and Settings has two further rows, GAME STATISTICS and DELETE MY DATA.
What changed in v1.5.1 (through 1.5.3): Quadshot no longer asked about ads when you opened it. The question came after your first finished game, or earlier if you tapped an offer to watch an ad for a reward, and Apple’s tracking prompt appeared only if you accepted ads. Settings has a REPORT AN AD row since then.
What changed in v1.4.0: Quadshot no longer contains any Google ad software. Ads are now served through Unity LevelPlay, and LevelPlay’s auction may include other ad companies and ad exchanges (see below). From 1.4.0 to 1.5.3 the ad software started only after you said yes to a privacy prompt. Versions 1.0 and earlier had no ads and collected nothing.

What we collect ourselves

Version 1.5.3: nothing. It sends no statistics and nothing else to us.

Version 1.5.4: only the game statistics described in the next section: a random install number the game creates and how the game is played, sent to our own server at Cloudflare. In the consent zone nothing is sent until you allow it, and everywhere you can turn it off in Settings → GAME STATISTICS. Quadshot has no accounts, and the game sends us neither your name, your advertising identifier, your payment details nor a record of what you buy. We see your e-mail address only if you write to us.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

This section is about version 1.5.4. Version 1.5.3 has no game statistics.

The game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work, and how often ads and offers are shown. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; the game never gives them to Unity LevelPlay, and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier and not a device ID, and the app never gives it to Unity LevelPlay.
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, levels and attempts, tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, errors in the game and loading time. In Quadshot also: when the game asks its ads question and gets a result while it waits, the answer that stands after it (or that the question was dismissed); Apple’s tracking answer when its prompt was really on screen (never the advertising identifier itself); requests for a rating; and whether the game was opened from a link to its leaderboards.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the consent zone: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game sends nothing until you choose "Allow". What happens in the game before you answer waits on your phone and is sent only if you choose "Allow"; "Don't allow" discards it.
  • Outside the consent zone: our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Which applies is decided by your phone’s time zone each time the game starts; a time zone that names no place or cannot be read counts as inside the consent zone. There, once the first-launch screens below are done — never on top of them — Quadshot asks with its own Game statistics card on the main menu, with two equal buttons, Allow and Don't allow, and nothing chosen in advance. It asks only until you answer, on the card or in Settings → GAME STATISTICS. Nothing in the game depends on your answer.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game’s Settings (the gear in the top-right corner of the main menu), turn off "GAME STATISTICS": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "DELETE MY DATA", the row below it, turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install’s data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16, turn statistics on only with a parent’s consent.

Our advertising partner: Unity LevelPlay

Quadshot shows ads using Unity LevelPlay, the ad mediation platform of Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). LevelPlay chooses which ad to show from the ad networks and ad exchanges that take part in its auction, including Unity Ads. When the ad software runs, Unity and those networks collect the following data from your device and process it as independent controllers, under their own privacy policies:

DataWhy
Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only while tracking is allowed for Quadshot in iOS (in Apple’s prompt, or later in iOS Settings)Personalised ads, ad measurement
Other device identifiers (such as an app-installation ID)Ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Device and system information (such as model, iOS version, language and connection type)Ad delivery and compatibility
IP address, used to estimate your approximate location (country or region)Region-appropriate ads, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (ads requested, shown, watched to the end, tapped)Ad delivery, measurement, billing advertisers
Diagnostic and performance data about the ad softwareReliability, ad quality

Unity describes what it does with this data, and how to exercise your rights with Unity, in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Quadshot does not use precise location, contacts, photos, the microphone, the camera or your health data. It has no user accounts and no user-generated content.

Your choices

In version 1.5.4

When you open version 1.5.4, right after the splash screen and before you play, Quadshot goes through these steps in this order and skips each step that already has an answer: (1) a welcome screen; (2), only in the consent zone, Quadshot’s own ads question; (3) Apple’s tracking prompt; (4) iOS’s own prompt about notifications, for the optional reminders below. Steps (2) and (3) are skipped too if you own Remove Ads, and (3) after a Decline of Quadshot’s question, or, for that launch, when the game cannot read whether you declined it. Quadshot shows no screen of its own in front of Apple’s prompt. The welcome screen appears once (again only if the game could not record that you accepted it). A question you leave without an answer comes again at the next launch (or after a finished Classic game, or when you tap an offer to watch an ad).

1. Welcome. One button, Accept Terms of Use: “Welcome to Quadshot! Please accept our Terms of Use and read our Privacy Policy,” with links to the Terms of Use and to this policy. Nothing else happens on this screen.

2. In the consent zone (the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland by your phone’s time zone, and a time zone that names no place or cannot be read): Quadshot’s own ads question. Two equal buttons, Accept and Decline, nothing chosen in advance. It says who shows the ads, what data Unity receives and why, and what each answer means. Until it has an answer, no ad software starts — unless the ad software is already running from earlier in the same session, outside the consent zone: then it keeps running under the rules for outside the zone until the game reads your time zone again (when you come back to it from another app or the home screen, when it starts again, or when a question or the Personalised ads switch is used), and from then on no ads are shown until you answer. Unity receives data about your device, the app and the ads you see on either answer; the answer, together with the Personalised ads switch, decides whether it may also use it to personalise ads.

  • Accept: Apple’s tracking prompt (3) follows, unless iOS already has your answer. Ads may then be personalised to you, and the usual rewarded offers remain available (unless you own Remove Ads): continue a run, double the shards a run earned, or double the daily gift. Accept also turns the Personalised ads switch (below) on — unless the game could not read your earlier answer just before asking; then the switch stays as it was.
  • Decline: Apple’s tracking prompt is not shown. Declining does not turn ads off (unless you bought Remove Ads, you still see ads): LevelPlay is told you do not consent (GDPR consent “no”) and that your data must not be sold or shared, before its software starts, or at once if it is already running (an ad already loaded before your Decline can still be shown). Decline also turns the Personalised ads switch (below) off, so this holds outside the consent zone too. Your advertising identifier is available to the ad software only while tracking is allowed for Quadshot in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (it is allowed there if you chose Allow in Apple’s prompt, or switched it on there yourself); declining here does not turn it off. The rewarded offers are unaffected.

Change your answer any time: Settings → PRIVACY & ADS → Change my answer (shown only in the consent zone; once you own Remove Ads, the row is gone from the next launch, since there are no ads to ask about). A changed answer is told to LevelPlay straight away, in the session you are playing (an ad already loaded before can still be shown), and sets the Personalised ads switch as above.

Outside the consent zone: no question of ours. Unless you own Remove Ads, ads can be shown once Apple’s tracking prompt below has an answer, whichever it is (and without the prompt if you declined Quadshot’s question before, in an earlier version or in the consent zone, or, for that launch, if the game cannot read whether you did); the answer decides whether the ad software gets your advertising identifier.

3. Apple’s tracking prompt. iOS shows it at most once per install: “Allow Quadshot to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites?” Outside the consent zone it comes when you open the game, before you play; inside the zone only after you Accept Quadshot’s question above. It does not come if iOS already has your answer, if iOS lets no apps ask, or if you declined Quadshot’s question (in an earlier version, or in the consent zone) and have not turned Personalised ads on since; once you own Remove Ads, Quadshot does not bring it up by itself. Otherwise, if it is not shown when you open the game (for example because the game could not read your earlier answer), it comes later: after a finished Classic game, when you tap an offer to watch an ad, or at a later launch. There is no screen of ours in front of it.

  • “Allow”: the advertising identifier may be used to personalise and measure ads.
  • “Ask App Not to Track” (or no answer): iOS gives the app and the ad software no advertising identifier (it reads as zeros). Outside the consent zone LevelPlay is also told your data must not be sold or shared. Inside it, your Accept to Quadshot’s question still stands: while the Personalised ads switch is on, LevelPlay is told you consent, and is not told that your data must not be sold or shared.

If “Allow Apps to Request to Track” is off in iOS Settings, iOS shows no prompt and answers “Ask App Not to Track” for you. Change it any time: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or iOS Settings → Quadshot → Allow Tracking; outside the consent zone, Settings → PRIVACY & ADS also has a button that opens it.

Personalised ads, in every country. Settings → PRIVACY & ADS → Personalised ads is a switch everywhere — outside the consent zone too, and for players who were never asked a question of ours. Your answer to Quadshot’s question sets it (Decline: off; Accept: on, as above), and while a Decline of that question is stored, it reads off. Off: LevelPlay is told you do not consent and that your data must not be sold or shared — at once if its software is running (an ad already loaded before can still be shown), and before it starts otherwise. On: in the consent zone it needs your Accept (turning it on there shows Quadshot’s question if you have not accepted); outside the zone it forgets an earlier Decline of Quadshot’s question, a “no” it told LevelPlay earlier is taken back, and whether your data may be sold follows whether tracking is allowed for Quadshot in iOS, in Apple’s prompt or later in iOS Settings, as the game last learned it: from Apple’s prompt when it answers, otherwise when the game started or came back to the foreground (while the game cannot read that, LevelPlay is told not to sell or share your data); if Apple’s prompt has never been answered and cannot be shown, or the game cannot read or forget that Decline, the switch reads off and you can try again. With that Decline forgotten, Quadshot’s question is asked again in the consent zone, unless you own Remove Ads by then. In the consent zone, the same screen also has Change my answer (above), which shows the ads question again. Once you own Remove Ads the row is gone from the next launch, since there are no ads.

If you declined Quadshot’s own ads question in an earlier version (1.4.0 to 1.5.3, where a Decline meant no ads at all): your “no” to personalisation stands, and you are not asked again unless you change it. From the 1.5.4 update, unless you own Remove Ads, you see ads, LevelPlay is told you do not consent to personalised ads, and the game shows a one-time note: “Quadshot now shows ads. Because you said no before, personalised ads are off. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & ads.”

In version 1.5.3

Version 1.5.3 has no welcome screen and no Personalised ads switch. It asks Quadshot’s own ads question in every country, never when you open the app: after your first finished Classic game, when you leave its results, or earlier, if you tap an offer to watch an ad for a reward yourself. The question says who shows the ads, what data Unity receives and why, what a no means and where to change your answer. The ad software is not started until you accept: until you answer, and if you decline, nothing is sent to any ad partner.

  • Accept: Apple’s tracking prompt appears once (“Allow”: the advertising identifier may be used to personalise and measure ads; “Ask App Not to Track”: iOS does not give it to the app or its ad partners), then LevelPlay starts, and ads may be shown — an ad between some runs, and optional ads you can choose to watch for a named reward.
  • Decline: the ad software is never started, so no data is sent to any ad partner. No ads are shown, there are no rewards for watching them (shards still come from playing), and Apple’s tracking prompt never appears.

If you leave without answering, the question comes again after a finished game in a later session. After you decline, Quadshot asks again only when you tap an ad offer yourself, and not sooner than 180 days later; until then those offers are not shown. Change your answer any time in Settings → PRIVACY & ADS (once you own Remove Ads, the row is gone from the next launch). If you withdraw consent, it takes effect straight away: Quadshot stops loading and showing ads and tells LevelPlay that you do not consent and that your data must not be sold or shared; from the next launch the ad software is not started at all.

Purchases and Remove Ads

Quadshot sells one-time purchases, all processed entirely by Apple: “Remove Ads”, cosmetic packs (new looks for the pads, shots, hit effects and backgrounds — they never change gameplay) and bundles of them. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The in-game currency (shards) is earned in the game only (by playing, and by optional ads that double a reward); it is never sold.

After buying Remove Ads you see no ads at all — neither the ad between runs nor any watch-an-ad reward: the continue becomes free and every run's shards are doubled automatically. In both versions Quadshot then brings up neither its own ads question nor Apple’s tracking prompt by itself, and from the next launch it does not start the ad software. The app stores the list of your purchases on your device. After reinstalling Quadshot, or on a new device, open Settings (or the Shop) and tap RESTORE PURCHASES once — until then the app cannot know what you own.

Daily reminders (optional)

Quadshot can remind you when a new Daily Challenge is ready — only while reminders are on in the game, and only while notifications are allowed for Quadshot in iOS (in iOS’s own prompt, or later in iOS Settings). In version 1.5.4 iOS asks when you open the game (step 4 above), unless it already has your answer, and a yes there also turns the game’s reminders on; in version 1.5.3 iOS asks when you first turn reminders on in the game. Either way you can allow them later in iOS Settings. Reminders are local notifications: the game schedules them on your device and iOS delivers them; the reminders are not sent to us or to any server. There is at most one a day, timed to the hour you usually start playing (that hour is worked out on your device and never leaves it). Turn them off any time in Settings → REMINDERS or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Leaderboards (Apple Game Center, optional)

The RANKS screen shows Apple’s Game Center leaderboards. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best Classic and Daily Challenge scores are sent to Apple together with your Game Center nickname, so they can be ranked, and the game shows other players’ nicknames and scores. Apple runs Game Center under Apple’s privacy policy; Noqyris receives nothing from Game Center. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent: your best scores are kept on your device and sent only if you sign in later. You can sign out, hide your profile or stop sharing in iOS Settings → Game Center.

Reporting an ad

If you see an ad that is inappropriate, or not suitable for your age, use Settings → REPORT AN AD. It opens your own mail app with a pre-written message that contains the app version and room to describe the ad. Nothing is sent until you send the e-mail yourself. We then see your e-mail address and what you wrote, and use them only to check the ad, report it to Unity so it can be blocked, and answer you.

Test builds

Pre-release builds handed to testers through Apple’s TestFlight carry a small label in the top-left corner of the screen: ADS OFF (the ad software is never started) or FAKE ADS + IAP (placeholder ads, a stand-in for the ads question in the consent zone and a pretend store, all drawn by the game itself; the welcome screen and Apple’s tracking prompt are the real ones, but the ad software never starts, so no answer and nothing else is sent to any ad partner, and nothing is charged). Test builds send the game statistics described above like the App Store version, marked as coming from a test build, so they are not counted with players’. A build without such a label is the App Store version and works exactly as this policy describes.

Data stored on your device

  • Your best score and local high-score table
  • Your play record (games played, symbols destroyed, best combo, highest phase), shown on the STATS tab of the PROGRESS screen
  • Your progress: shards, level, daily missions, Daily Challenge results, play streak, awards and the looks you own or have equipped
  • Settings (sound, music, haptics, reminders), your purchases, your answer to Quadshot’s ads question (and, from version 1.5.3, when that question was last asked); in version 1.5.4 also whether you have accepted the welcome screen, the Personalised ads switch, whether the one-time note for an earlier Decline was shown, and Apple’s tracking answer as the game last read it
  • The hours you recently started playing, used only to time reminders
  • Your best leaderboard scores (sent to Game Center only while you are signed in)
  • In version 1.5.4: your answer about game statistics and, while they are on, the random install number and what the statistics need to send it (see Game statistics (Noqyris))

Apart from what LevelPlay is told about your ads answers and the switch (see Your choices), the leaderboard scores sent to Game Center while you are signed in, and, in version 1.5.4, the game statistics described above, this stays on your device, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Quadshot is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game’s content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "DELETE MY DATA" button in the game’s Settings on the child’s phone deletes them.

Quadshot is not in the App Store’s Kids Category.

Your rights

Game statistics (version 1.5.4). We do not know whose install number is whose, so your statistics can be found only from the game itself: Settings → DELETE MY DATA deletes them (see How to turn it off and delete above), and Settings → GAME STATISTICS turns them off at any time. We cannot find or delete them by e-mail.

E-mails you send us (an ad report, or any other message) we can find by your address: write to us and we will delete them.

Ads. For data Unity and its ad partners hold, see Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which explains how to make a request to Unity. In version 1.5.4, unless you own Remove Ads (then the row is gone from the next launch, since there are no ads), you can change your answer in the consent zone and turn off personalised ads everywhere, in Settings → PRIVACY & ADS; neither stops ads. In version 1.5.3 you can decline or withdraw consent in Settings → PRIVACY & ADS, and then no ads are shown. Everywhere you can also limit tracking in iOS Settings.
