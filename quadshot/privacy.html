Privacy Policy — Quadshot

Last updated: 16 September 2026 · applies to Quadshot v1.4.0 and later

Quadshot (“the app”) is a single-player arcade game published by bySubotic (Djordje Subotic). This policy explains exactly what data leaves your device, who receives it, and how to stop it.

What changed in v1.4.0: Quadshot no longer contains any Google ad software. Ads are now served through Unity LevelPlay instead of Google AdMob, and LevelPlay’s auction may include other ad companies and ad exchanges (see below). The ad software now starts only after you have said yes to a privacy prompt. Versions 1.0 and earlier had no ads and collected nothing.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. bySubotic operates no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We never receive your data.

Our advertising partner: Unity LevelPlay

Quadshot shows ads using Unity LevelPlay, the ad mediation platform of Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). LevelPlay chooses which ad to show from the ad networks and ad exchanges that take part in its auction, including Unity Ads. When the ad software runs, Unity and those networks collect the following data from your device and process it as independent controllers, under their own privacy policies:

DataWhy
Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow trackingPersonalised ads, ad measurement
Other device identifiers (such as an app-installation ID)Ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Device and system information (such as model, iOS version, language and connection type)Ad delivery and compatibility
IP address, used to estimate your approximate location (country or region)Region-appropriate ads, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (ads requested, shown, watched to the end, tapped)Ad delivery, measurement, billing advertisers
Diagnostic and performance data about the ad softwareReliability, ad quality

Unity describes what it does with this data, and how to exercise your rights with Unity, in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Quadshot does not use precise location, contacts, photos, the microphone, the camera or your health data. It has no user accounts and no user-generated content.

Your choices

1. Tracking (Apple’s prompt). On first launch iOS asks: “Allow Quadshot to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites?”

  • “Ask App Not to Track” (or no answer): iOS does not give your advertising identifier to the app or its ad partners. Ads you see are not based on tracking across other apps. The game works exactly the same.
  • “Allow”: the advertising identifier may be used to personalise and measure ads.

Change it any time: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

2. Ads consent (Quadshot’s own prompt). Straight after, on first launch, Quadshot asks whether you accept ads from Unity LevelPlay and its partners. The ad software is not started until you accept.

  • Accept: LevelPlay starts, and ads may be shown — an ad between some runs, and an optional ad you can watch to continue a run.
  • Decline: the ad software is never started, so no data is sent to any ad partner. You get no ads at all — and no watch-an-ad continue. Everything else in the game works the same.

Change your answer any time in the game: Settings → PRIVACY CHOICES (not shown once you own Remove Ads — the ad software is then never started, so there is nothing to change). If you withdraw consent, it takes effect straight away, in the session you are playing: Quadshot stops loading and showing ads, and tells LevelPlay that you do not consent and that your data must not be sold or shared. From the next launch the ad software is not started at all.

Purchases and Remove Ads

“Remove Ads” is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account.

After buying it you see no ads at all — neither the ad between runs nor the watch-an-ad continue: the continue becomes free. From the next launch Quadshot no longer asks the two questions above and does not start the ad software. The app stores one flag on your device (adsRemoved). After reinstalling Quadshot, or on a new device, open Settings and tap Restore purchases once — until then the app cannot know that you own Remove Ads.

Test builds

Pre-release builds handed to testers through Apple’s TestFlight carry a small label in the top-left corner of the screen: ADS OFF (the ad software is never started) or FAKE ADS (placeholder ads drawn by the game itself; nothing is sent to anyone). A build without such a label is the App Store version and works exactly as this policy describes.

Data stored on your device only

  • Your best score and local high-score table
  • Game statistics (games played, best combo, highest phase)
  • Settings (sound, haptics), the “ads removed” flag, and your ads-consent answer

This never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Quadshot is rated 4+ but is not directed to children and is not in the Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data Unity and its ad partners hold, see Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which explains how to make a request to Unity. You can also decline or withdraw consent in Settings → PRIVACY CHOICES (while ads can still show), and limit tracking in iOS Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the “last updated” date above changes and the new version is published here before the app update ships.
