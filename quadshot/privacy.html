Privacy Policy — Quadshot

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · applies to Quadshot v1.5.4 and later

Quadshot (“the app”) is a single-player arcade game published by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). This policy explains exactly what data leaves your device, who receives it, and how to stop it.

What changed in v1.5.4: Quadshot now opens with a short welcome screen, right after the splash and before your first game, asking you to accept these Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy. In the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (“the consent zone”, decided from your phone’s time zone) it then asks its own ads question, with equal Accept and Decline buttons; Decline no longer turns ads off — ads still show, just not personalised to you. Apple’s tracking prompt follows, with no screen of ours in front of it: always outside the consent zone, only after Accept inside it. iOS may then ask about notifications, for the optional reminders. Quadshot also sends game statistics to us (Noqyris), so we can see how the game is played and fix what does not work (see Game statistics (Noqyris) below); in the consent zone nothing is sent until you allow it, on a card that comes later, on the main menu. Settings → PRIVACY & ADS now opens a page with a Personalised ads switch that works in every country, and Settings has two further rows, GAME STATISTICS and DELETE MY DATA.
What changed in v1.5.1: Quadshot no longer asks about ads when you open it. The question comes after your first finished game, or earlier if you tap an offer to watch an ad for a reward. Apple’s tracking prompt now appears only if you accept ads. Settings has a new REPORT AN AD row.
What changed in v1.4.0: Quadshot no longer contains any Google ad software. Ads are now served through Unity LevelPlay instead of Google AdMob, and LevelPlay’s auction may include other ad companies and ad exchanges (see below). The ad software now starts only after you have said yes to a privacy prompt. Versions 1.0 and earlier had no ads and collected nothing.

What we collect ourselves

Only the game statistics described in the next section: a random install number the game creates and how the game is played, sent to our own server at Cloudflare. In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland (“the consent zone”) nothing is sent until you allow it, and everywhere you can turn it off in Settings → GAME STATISTICS. Quadshot has no accounts, and we never receive your name, your advertising identifier, your payment details or a record of what you buy. We see your e-mail address only if you write to us.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

The game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; they are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier, not a device ID, and it is never given to anyone, including our ad network (Unity LevelPlay).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, levels and attempts, tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, errors in the game and loading time. In Quadshot also: your answers to the ads question and to Apple’s tracking prompt (never the advertising identifier itself), requests for a rating, and whether the game was opened from a link to its leaderboards.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the consent zone (the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland): your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game asks you before anything is sent and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow".
  • In other countries: our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Which applies is decided by your phone’s time zone; a time zone the game cannot read counts as inside the consent zone. There, once the first-launch screens below are done — never on top of them — Quadshot asks with its own Game statistics card on the main menu, with two equal buttons, Allow and Don't allow, and nothing chosen in advance. It asks only until you answer. Nothing in the game depends on your answer.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game’s Settings (the gear in the top-right corner of the main menu), turn off "GAME STATISTICS": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "DELETE MY DATA", the row below it, turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install’s data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16, turn statistics on only with a parent’s consent.

Our advertising partner: Unity LevelPlay

Quadshot shows ads using Unity LevelPlay, the ad mediation platform of Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). LevelPlay chooses which ad to show from the ad networks and ad exchanges that take part in its auction, including Unity Ads. When the ad software runs, Unity and those networks collect the following data from your device and process it as independent controllers, under their own privacy policies:

Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow trackingPersonalised ads, ad measurement

Unity describes what it does with this data, and how to exercise your rights with Unity, in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Quadshot does not use precise location, contacts, photos, the microphone, the camera or your health data. It has no user accounts and no user-generated content.

Your choices

On first launch, right after the splash screen and before your first game, Quadshot runs through its onboarding screens in this order: (1) a welcome screen; (2), only in the consent zone, Quadshot’s own ads question; (3) Apple’s tracking prompt; (4) iOS’s own prompt about notifications, for the optional reminders below. Quadshot shows no screen of its own in front of Apple’s prompt. A player who has played before sees only whichever of these they have not yet answered; the welcome screen appears at most once.

1. Welcome. One button, Accept: “Welcome to Quadshot! Please accept our Terms of Use and read our Privacy Policy,” with links to the Terms of Use and to this policy. Nothing else happens on this screen.

2. In the consent zone — the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (“the consent zone”, read from your phone’s time zone; a time zone the game cannot read counts as inside it) — Quadshot’s own ads question. Two equal buttons, Accept and Decline, nothing chosen in advance. It says who shows the ads, what data Unity receives and why, and what each answer means. No ad software starts until you answer.

  • Accept: Apple’s tracking prompt (3) follows. Ads may then be personalised to you, and the usual rewarded offers remain available: continue a run, double the shards a run earned, or double the daily gift.
  • Decline: Apple’s tracking prompt is not shown. Ads still show — declining does not turn ads off — but they are not personalised: LevelPlay is told you do not consent (GDPR consent “no”) before its software starts, and your advertising identifier is not used. The rewarded offers are unaffected.

Change your answer any time: Settings → PRIVACY & ADS → Change my answer (shown only in the consent zone, and not once you own Remove Ads, since there are no ads to ask about). A changed answer takes effect straight away, in the session you are playing.

3. Outside the consent zone: no question of ours. Ads are shown to every player on either answer to Apple’s tracking prompt below; what differs is only whether they may be personalised.

4. Apple’s tracking prompt. iOS asks once: “Allow Quadshot to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites?” Outside the consent zone it appears at first launch, before your first game; inside the zone it appears only after you Accept ads above. There is no screen of ours in front of it.

  • “Allow”: the advertising identifier may be used to personalise and measure ads.
  • “Ask App Not to Track” (or no answer): the advertising identifier stays at zero; ads are not personalised, and LevelPlay is told your data must not be sold or shared.

If “Allow Apps to Request to Track” is off in iOS Settings, iOS shows no prompt and answers “Ask App Not to Track” for you. Change it any time: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or iOS Settings → Quadshot → Allow Tracking; Settings → PRIVACY & ADS also has a button that opens it.

Personalised ads, in every country. Settings → PRIVACY & ADS → Personalised ads is a switch everywhere — outside the consent zone too, and for players who were never asked a question of ours. Off: LevelPlay is told you do not consent and that your data must not be sold or shared, so ads stop being personalised; ads keep showing either way. In the consent zone, the same screen also has Change my answer (above), which shows the ads question again. The row is not shown once you own Remove Ads, since there are no ads to ask about.

If you declined Quadshot’s own ads question in an earlier version (1.4.0 to 1.5.3, where a Decline meant no ads at all): your “no” to personalisation stands, and you are not asked again. From the 1.5.4 update you see ads — simply not personalised ones — and the game shows a one-time note: “Ads keep Quadshot free. They don’t track you.”

Purchases and Remove Ads

Quadshot sells one-time purchases, all processed entirely by Apple: “Remove Ads”, cosmetic packs (new looks for the pads, shots, hit effects and backgrounds — they never change gameplay) and bundles of them. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The in-game currency (shards) is earned by playing only; it is never sold.

After buying Remove Ads you see no ads at all — neither the ad between runs nor any watch-an-ad reward: the continue becomes free and every run's shards are doubled automatically. Quadshot does not ask its own ads question or show Apple’s tracking prompt, and from the next launch it does not start the ad software. The app stores the list of your purchases on your device. After reinstalling Quadshot, or on a new device, open Settings (or the Shop) and tap RESTORE PURCHASES once — until then the app cannot know what you own.

Daily reminders (optional)

Quadshot can remind you when a new Daily Challenge is ready — only if you turn reminders on in the game, and only if you allowed notifications in iOS’s own prompt (at first launch, step 4 above, or later in iOS Settings). Reminders are local notifications: the game schedules them on your device and iOS delivers them. Nothing is sent to us or to any server. There is at most one a day, timed to the hour you usually start playing (that hour is worked out on your device and never leaves it). Turn them off any time in Settings → REMINDERS or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Leaderboards (Apple Game Center, optional)

The RANKS screen shows Apple’s Game Center leaderboards. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best Classic and Daily Challenge scores are sent to Apple together with your Game Center nickname, so they can be ranked, and the game shows other players’ nicknames and scores. Apple runs Game Center under Apple’s privacy policy; Noqyris receives nothing from Game Center. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent: your best scores are kept on your device and sent only if you sign in later. You can sign out, hide your profile or stop sharing in iOS Settings → Game Center.

Reporting an ad

If you see an ad that is inappropriate, or not suitable for your age, use Settings → REPORT AN AD. It opens your own mail app with a pre-written message that contains the app version and room to describe the ad. Nothing is sent until you send the e-mail yourself. We then see your e-mail address and what you wrote, and use them only to check the ad, report it to Unity so it can be blocked, and answer you.

Test builds

Pre-release builds handed to testers through Apple’s TestFlight carry a small label in the top-left corner of the screen: ADS OFF (the ad software is never started) or FAKE ADS + IAP (placeholder ads, a placeholder of the welcome screen, the ads question in the consent zone, and Apple’s tracking prompt, and a pretend store, all drawn by the game itself; nothing is sent to any ad partner and nothing is charged). Test builds send the game statistics described above like the App Store version, marked as coming from a test build, so they are not counted with players’. A build without such a label is the App Store version and works exactly as this policy describes.

Data stored on your device only

  • Your best score and local high-score table
  • Your play record (games played, symbols destroyed, best combo, highest phase), shown on the STATS tab of the PROGRESS screen
  • Your progress: shards, level, daily missions, Daily Challenge results, play streak, awards and the looks you own or have equipped
  • Settings (sound, music, haptics, reminders), your purchases, whether you have accepted the welcome screen, your ads-consent and personalised-ads answers and when that question was last asked, and Apple’s tracking answer as the game last read it
  • The hours you recently started playing, used only to time reminders
  • Your best leaderboard scores (sent to Game Center only while you are signed in)
  • Your answer about game statistics and, while they are on, the random install number and what the statistics need to send it (see Game statistics (Noqyris))

Apart from the leaderboard scores sent to Game Center while you are signed in, and the game statistics described above, this never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Quadshot is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game’s content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "DELETE MY DATA" button in the game’s Settings on the child’s phone deletes them.

Quadshot is not in the App Store’s Kids Category.

Your rights

Game statistics. We do not know whose install number is whose, so your statistics can be found only from the game itself: Settings → DELETE MY DATA deletes them (see How to turn it off and delete above), and Settings → GAME STATISTICS turns them off at any time. We cannot find or delete them by e-mail.

E-mails you send us (an ad report, or any other message) we can find by your address: write to us and we will delete them.

Ads. For data Unity and its ad partners hold, see Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which explains how to make a request to Unity. In the consent zone you can change your answer, and everywhere you can turn off personalised ads, in Settings → PRIVACY & ADS; ads keep showing either way. Everywhere you can also limit tracking in iOS Settings.

Changes
