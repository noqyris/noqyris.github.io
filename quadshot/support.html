Support — Quadshot

Quadshot: Reaction Time Game · for iPhone and iPad · by Noqyris

Quadshot is a one-thumb reaction game. Symbols fall from the top of the screen: slide the rack of four pads along the bottom to line up the right pad, and tap to fire straight up before each symbol crosses the line. Miss three and the run is over. Nine phases keep changing the rules (match the shape, match the color, then cross-matches where pairs trade places), and after phase 9 OVERDRIVE loops them, faster and faster. Play a Classic run whenever you like, and one Daily Challenge course a day.

Contact: questions, problems and ideas go to support@noqyris.com. Please say which iPhone or iPad you use and which version of Quadshot you have (see Which version do I have?). See also the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy.

Purchases

What can I buy?

Six one-time purchases, in the SHOP (the bottom row of the main menu, or tap your shards at the top). Each is bought once and is yours for good; the price comes from the App Store, in your currency.

STARTER BUNDLERemove Ads + the Neon Pack.
NEON PACKA rack, a trail, a burst and a background: glowing neon and a city skyline.
DEEP SPACE PACKThe same four looks: void black, stardust and a nebula.

ALL PACKSNeon, Deep Space and Chrome: 12 items.

Packs change only how the game looks, never how it plays; you put looks on in the LOCKER. Shards, the in-game currency, are earned by playing and are never sold. The SHOP stops offering what you already own, and a bundle once you own part of it.

My purchase is missing

Open Settings (the gear in the top-right corner of the main menu) and tap RESTORE PURCHASES, or tap RESTORE PURCHASES at the bottom of the SHOP, signed in to the Apple Account that bought it. All six purchases come back: Remove Ads, the three packs and both bundles. After a reinstall or on a new iPhone or iPad, do this once: until then the game cannot know what you own. If the SHOP says “Store unavailable — check your connection”, try again when you are online.

What does Remove Ads change?

With Remove Ads (or the Starter Bundle) you see no ads at all. The continue offer becomes CONTINUE — FREE, every run’s shards are doubled, and the game does not ask you anything about ads.

Ads and your privacy

Where are the ads?

An ad between some Classic runs (never during a run, and never in your first few runs), and optional ads you choose to watch for a named reward, on buttons that start with ▶ WATCH AD: three lives back to continue a run (once per run), double the shards a run earned, or double the daily gift. The Daily Challenge has no ads and no continue.

Why was I asked to accept ads, or about tracking?

On first launch, right after the splash and before your first game: a welcome screen (accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy); then, only in the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland (“the consent zone”, decided by your phone’s time zone), Quadshot’s own ads question, with equal Accept and Decline buttons; then Apple’s tracking prompt — always outside the consent zone, only after Accept inside it, with no screen of ours in front of it. Decline no longer turns ads off: ads still show, just not personalised to you. A returning player only sees whichever of these they have not yet answered. The privacy policy explains it in full.

Game statistics

Quadshot sends us statistics about how it is played (screens, runs and their results, the tutorial, ad offers and ads shown, errors and loading time) with a random install number the game creates, never your name or the advertising identifier, so we can improve the game. In the consent zone (EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland) nothing is sent until you choose Allow on the Game statistics card, which comes up on the main menu once the first-launch screens above are done (its other button is Don't allow). Everywhere, Settings → GAME STATISTICS turns them off, and DELETE MY DATA turns them off and asks our server to delete what was sent. We cannot find your statistics by e-mail: only that button can.

Daily Challenge and RANKS

When does the Daily Challenge change?

At 00:00 UTC, the same moment for every player: one course a day, the same for everyone. You get three scored attempts; after that you can keep playing it as practice, which does not count. Missions, the daily gift and your play streak turn at midnight where you are.

How do the RANKS work?

RANKS (the bottom row of the main menu) shows three Apple Game Center leaderboards: TODAY (the Daily Challenge, scored attempts only), THIS WEEK (Classic, starts again every Monday at 00:00 UTC) and ALL TIME (Classic, best ever). Classic counts your score at the first game over: what a continue adds does not count. To take part, tap SIGN IN on RANKS; if it says GAME CENTER IS OFF, sign in under iOS Settings → Game Center and come back. Game Center’s sign-in comes up only when you open RANKS or tap SIGN IN. While you are signed out, your best scores are kept on your device and sent only if you sign in later.

Reminders

Optional: switch on Settings → REMINDERS, and allow notifications when iOS asks — at first launch, or later in iOS Settings if you skipped it then. They are local notifications: the game schedules them on your device and iOS delivers them, and nothing goes to a server. There is at most one a day, about the new Daily Challenge (with your streak, missions or daily gift), timed to the hour you usually start playing. If the row says “Enable in iOS Settings”, notifications are off for Quadshot in iOS Settings → Notifications. To stop them, switch REMINDERS off, or turn them off in iOS Settings → Notifications → Quadshot.

Saves and offline play

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only: there is no account and no cloud save. Your shards, level, looks, missions, awards, streak, scores and settings stay through restarts and updates, and are deleted if you delete the game. Purchases come back with RESTORE PURCHASES; Game Center scores stay with Apple.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Classic runs and the Daily Challenge play without one. Ads, purchases and restoring them, RANKS (Game Center) and the game statistics need a connection.

Which version do I have?

The game does not show its version on screen. iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage (iPad Storage on an iPad) → Quadshot shows the installed version, and Quadshot’s App Store page lists every version under Version History. A mail from Settings → REPORT AN AD fills the version in for you.
