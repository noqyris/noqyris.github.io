Quadshot shows ads through Unity LevelPlay: a short ad between some Classic runs, and optional ads you choose to watch for a named reward (a continue, double shards, double the daily gift). The Daily Challenge has no ads. Your choices about personalised ads, and what happens on first launch, are explained in full in the Privacy Policy. The advertiser is responsible for an ad's own content; an ad appearing in Quadshot does not mean we recommend what it advertises. Report an ad that looks wrong through Settings → REPORT AN AD. Do not watch or tap ads falsely or automatically — ad networks treat that as invalid traffic.

5. Game Center leaderboards

RANKS shows three optional Apple Game Center leaderboards (the Daily Challenge, this week's Classic and all-time Classic), governed by Apple's own terms. If you sign in, other players can see your Game Center nickname next to your score. The game works normally if you never sign in.

6. Reminders

Quadshot can send one local reminder a day about the Daily Challenge, only if you turn REMINDERS on in Settings and allow notifications when iOS asks. These are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent from a server.
