Bank or Bust: Coin Sort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026. Applies to Bank or Bust version 1.0 and later.

Overview

Bank or Bust is a single-player puzzle game by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic): drop stacks of coins into a vault, let matching colours slide together and bank them before the vault fills up. There are no accounts of ours and no sign-in to us. The game sends us statistics about how it is played, under a random install number and never with your name, and in Europe only if you allow it (see Game statistics (Noqyris) below). The game also shows ads, and the ad service it uses collects some data from your device. This policy explains what is collected, why, and how you control it.

Advertising

Unless you own Remove Ads, the game can show two kinds of ads: short videos you choose to watch for a stated reward (Continue, which clears five stacks so a level can go on once after the vault fills up; Double Reward, which adds a level's gold reward again; and a free booster when you have run out of one), and an occasional full-screen ad when you leave the card at the end of a level. A full-screen ad never interrupts a level, is never shown when the app opens or closes, and is never shown in your first session; a reward video plays only when you tap to watch it. There is no banner.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange (both Unity Technologies). The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data, such as performance measurements and technical error information about how ads load and display.

What it is used for

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads, only with your tracking permission for the advertising identifier and, in Europe, your consent.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity.
  • Keeping the ad software working: finding and fixing problems with how ads load, display and perform.

Your choices

The ad software does not start when the game opens, and the game asks nothing when it opens. The first question comes when you leave the card of your fourth finished level, or of your second won level, whichever comes first (if you leave it unanswered, after a level in a later session), or earlier if you tap an offer to watch an ad for a reward. A short notice, "One question first", comes before it. If you own Remove Ads, nothing asks you after a level. Which question you see depends on where your device is, read from its time zone.

In Europe (the EU and EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Serbia, and wherever the device's time zone cannot be read), where the law asks for your consent before ad software reads your device, the game asks its own question first. It says who shows the ads (Bank or Bust, by Noqyris, through Unity LevelPlay), what Unity Technologies (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange) receives and why, and what declining means. Its two answers, Accept and Decline, look the same.

  • Accept: iOS then asks whether Bank or Bust may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency), and the ad software starts once you have answered it. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown, but your advertising identifier is not shared, so ads cannot be personalised with it.
  • Decline: the ad software is not started at all and collects nothing. No ads are shown, there are no rewards for watching ads, and iOS's tracking question is not shown. The game asks again only if you tap an ad offer at least 180 days later.

To change your answer, open Settings → Privacy & ads at any time. If you withdraw consent after ads have started, the game stops showing and loading ads and tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information. We receive your answer only as part of the game statistics (below), and in Europe only if you allow them.

Everywhere else, the only question is iOS's own (App Tracking Transparency): whether Bank or Bust may use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. It comes at the same moments, after the same notice. The ad software starts after you answer, whichever answer you give. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared and the game tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information, so ads are not personalised with it. You can change your answer at any time in the iOS Settings app (Bank or Bust → Allow Tracking); Settings → Privacy & ads in the game explains this and opens that page.

Report an ad: if an ad looks inappropriate or wrong for the game's age rating, tap Settings → Report an ad. It opens your mail app with a pre-written message to support@noqyris.com, including the game's version; you decide what to send.

If you own Remove Ads, no full-screen ad is shown or requested from the moment of the purchase, the ad software is not started at all from the next time the game opens on this device, and the rewards that others watch an ad for are yours without watching anything. After you reinstall the game or move to a new device, tap Settings → Restore Purchases so the game knows again.

Many Unity ads also show a privacy (shield) icon that you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the choices you have, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. To make a privacy request about the data Unity collects, contact Unity as described in its policy: we don't hold that data.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

The game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; they are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier, not a device ID, and it is never given to anyone, including our ad network (Unity LevelPlay).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, levels and attempts, tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, your answers to the game's ad question and to iOS's tracking and notification requests, which kinds of reminders are scheduled and when (never their words), your Daily Heist streak, when the game asks for a rating or shows the Remove Ads offer (never a purchase), errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland and Serbia: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), Serbian Personal Data Protection Act Art. 12, Electronic Communications Act Art. 160). The game asks you before anything is sent and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow".
  • In other countries: our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16 (15 in Serbia), turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

In-app purchases

The one purchase is optional: Remove Ads. It is processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase. What you own is remembered on your device, and Restore Purchases in Settings (and in the Shop) asks Apple to re-apply it, for example after you reinstall. The game sells no currency.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your progress: the level you have reached, your coins and boosters, your Take and rank, your daily-heist streak, and play statistics.
  • Your settings, such as sound, reduced motion and whether reminders are on.
  • Which purchases you own, and the purchase transactions already credited.
  • Counters used to space out ads, such as when the last full-screen ad was shown, how many levels and sessions you have played, and which rewards you have used today.
  • In Europe, your answer to the game's ad question and when it was last shown (so a refusal is not asked again for 180 days); everywhere, a note of your answer to iOS's tracking question.
  • For the game statistics: your answer to their question and, while they are on, the random install number, the statistics waiting to be sent and how many times you have tried each level.

None of it is sent to us, except the game statistics described above, and all of it is removed if you delete the app.

Reminders

If you turn them on (the game asks first, then iOS asks for permission), the game schedules a few notifications on your device: when a new Daily Heist opens, before a daily streak runs out, and a last few if you have not played for some days, never more than one a day and none at night. They are local notifications: the game plans them on your device and iOS shows them, and they never advertise anything. Their words are never sent anywhere; with game statistics on, we learn only whether you turned reminders on and which kinds were scheduled for when (see Game statistics (Noqyris)). Turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Sharing a result

When you share a result, the game hands a short line of text about it and the game's App Store link to iOS's share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice; the game sends it nowhere itself.

Network

The game itself plays fully offline. It uses the network only to load ads (after the question above), for purchases and restores through Apple, and to send the game statistics described above. Without a connection the statistics wait on your phone, and nothing in the game changes.

Children's Privacy

Bank or Bust is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13, and it tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone deletes them.
