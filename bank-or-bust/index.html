Bank or Bust: Coin Sort — Support

Bank or Bust is a coin-stacking puzzle game for iPhone by Noqyris. Drop stacks of coins into the vault, let matching colours slide together, bank every run of ten, and don't let the vault fill up. A new Daily Heist every day.

Contact

Questions, problems and ideas: support@noqyris.com. Please say which iPhone and which version of iOS you use.

Common questions

  • My purchase is missing. Open SETTINGS on the game's home screen and tap Restore Purchases (also in SHOP), signed in to the Apple Account that bought it. Remove Ads comes back.
  • How do I change my answer about ads? SETTINGS, then Privacy & ads. The Personalised ads switch is there in every country: off, you still see ads, chosen without your personal data. In the EU and EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Change my answer asks the game's question again. On iPhone, Apple's own tracking setting is in the iOS Settings app (Bank or Bust → Allow Tracking).
  • I saw an ad that should not be there. SETTINGS, then Report an ad, or write to the address above and say what the ad showed and roughly when.
  • How do I play? Drag a stack of coins onto an empty cell. Coins of one colour on top of neighbouring stacks slide together; ten or more of one colour on top of a stack are banked. Bank the level's goal before the vault fills up. HAMMER smashes a stack; SHUFFLE deals three new ones. RULES on the home screen shows it again.
  • Do I need an internet connection? No. The game plays without one. Ads, ad rewards and purchases need a connection.
  • Is my progress backed up? Progress is kept on your device and is removed if you delete the game.

Privacy

The privacy policy explains what the ad service collects and how you control it. The terms of use cover playing the game, the ads and the purchase.
