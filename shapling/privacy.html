Shapling — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026. Applies to Shapling (Shapling: My Virtual Puppy) version 1.2.3 and later. Until version 1.2.2 the ad software started only after you tapped Accept, the question came at your first tap on ▶ and never on the day you installed the game, Decline meant no ads at all, pop-up ads never came on the install day, and Serbia was one of the countries where game statistics asked first.

Shapling has no account and no sign-in: your puppy, your park and your wardrobe stay on your phone. The game shows ads, which keep it free. In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland it first asks whether ads may use your personal data; if you say no, ads still show, but they are not personalised. Everywhere else ads start without a question of their own. The game never tracks you and never uses your device's advertising identifier. It also sends us statistics about how it is played, without your name, which you can turn off and delete (see Game statistics). This policy explains what, why, and how you control it.

Advertising

The game can show two kinds of ads:

  • Ads you choose (▶). In three places — the basket, the ideas and the stall — a ▶ button offers a short video for a stated number of clovers. It plays only when you tap it, and the reward is given only when the video has been watched to the end. ▶ offers appear after a minute in the park, at most four videos a day and at least a minute apart.
  • Pop-up ads. The game may occasionally show a short full-screen ad at a natural pause: after you close a panel, or when your puppy falls asleep. A card announces it two seconds before it starts. On the day you install the game: not before 4 minutes of play in a session, and at most one per session. From the second day: not before 3 minutes of play in a session, and at most two per session. Always at least 3 minutes of play between two pop-up ads, none within a minute of coming back to the game or within 5 minutes after a ▶ video, never in the middle of caring for your puppy, and at most four a day. Only time you actually spend in the game counts. Once you have bought anything in the game — any bundle or park look, No Pop-up Ads or the Golden Collar — pop-up ads are never shown again.

There is no banner. Feeding, petting, walking and playing with your puppy never depend on ads.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from the Unity Ads network. The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect

Once the ad software has started (see Your choices for when that is), and as Unity describes it in its documentation:

  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (for example, city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data, such as performance measurements and technical error information about how ads load and display.

No tracking

Shapling does not ask for permission to track you (iOS App Tracking Transparency) and does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA): without that permission, iOS gives the app and the ad software only a blank identifier. Unity and its ad partners use the device data listed above to show and measure ads as described below — not to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

What it is used for

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears. Only where your answer allows it (see Your choices): choosing ads that suit you.
  • Measuring how ads perform.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity.
  • Keeping the ad software working: checking ad quality and finding and fixing problems with how ads load and display.

Your choices

The game decides where you are from your phone's time zone, which iOS normally sets from where the phone is. A time zone it cannot read counts as one of the countries below.

In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

The first time you open the game, right after you accept the Terms of Use, a Your privacy prompt names Unity LevelPlay and asks whether ads may be personalised for you. Its two buttons, Accept and Decline, are equal, and nothing is chosen in advance. Until you answer, the ad software is not started at all: it collects nothing and there are no ads. If you close the game without answering, it asks again the next time you open it, or when you tap a ▶ button.

  • If you tap Accept, the ad software starts and ads can be personalised for you, using the data listed above. No other permission is asked for.
  • If you tap Decline, the ad software still starts and ads still show, but they are not personalised for you: the game tells Unity that you did not consent and not to sell or share your personal information. Unity still receives the technical data it needs to deliver and count an ad and to prevent fraud, as its policy describes. The game, and all of your puppy's care, works the same whatever you choose.

Everywhere else (including Serbia and the rest of the Balkans outside the EU)

The game asks no ad question of its own. The ad software starts on its own after about 20 seconds of calm play in the park, and ads can be personalised using the data listed above, never the advertising identifier.

Changing your choice

Open Settings › Privacy and ads › Ad choices in the game, in any country. It shows the same prompt. Decline there means ads that are not personalised from then on: the game at once tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information. Accept turns personalised ads back on.

If you tapped Decline in version 1.2.2 or earlier, when Decline meant no ads at all: from version 1.2.3 that answer means ads that are not personalised. You are not asked again; the ad software starts, ads are shown but not personalised, and the game shows a one-time message: “Ads keep the game free; they don't track you.” You can change it at any time in Ad choices. If you tapped Accept earlier, that answer stays.

If you own the Golden Collar, the ad software is not started once the game knows about your purchase on this device, there are no ads of any kind, and every ▶ reward arrives instantly without an ad. No Pop-up Ads turns off pop-up ads for good; the optional ▶ ads stay available if you want them. After you reinstall the game or move to a new device, tap Restore purchases so the game knows again.

For more on how Unity uses this data and the choices you have, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in its policy: we don't hold that data.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

The game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; they are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone (Apple's privacy label lists it as "Device ID"). It is not the advertising identifier, not an identifier of your device, and it is never given to anyone, including our ad network (Unity LevelPlay).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, the first-day tasks, bond levels and the play streak, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, your answers to the ad and notification questions, rating requests, which park news opened the game, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version and build number, platform (iOS or Android), language, time-zone offset, and the install date (days since install).
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier, your puppy's name or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game recognises this by your phone's time zone, the same way as for ads: then it asks you before anything is sent, in its own Game statistics question with two equal buttons, Allow and Don't allow, shown once after you finish your first first-day task, and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow".
  • In other countries, including Serbia: our legitimate interest in improving the game; statistics are on without a question, and you can turn them off at any time.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game, open Settings › Privacy and ads and turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, if you are under 16, choose "Allow" only with a parent's consent.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional and never touch care, play or your puppy's memory: the Golden Collar, No Pop-up Ads, eight cosmetic bundles and three park looks, all one-time purchases. Clovers for the stall are earned in the game, also with the optional ▶ videos, and cannot be bought. There are no subscriptions and no random rewards.

Purchases are processed by Apple (or Google on Android). The app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase. What you own is remembered on your device. Restore purchases (in Settings › Purchases and at Stall › Bundles) asks the store to re-apply your one-time purchases, for example after you reinstall.

Park news (local notifications)

If you turn them on, Shapling can let you know when something really happens with your puppy: at most once a day, never during quiet hours at night, always about your puppy and never about offers. The first time you open the game, right after the Terms of Use (and, in the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, after the ad question), iOS asks once whether Shapling may send notifications; nothing depends on the answer. If you say no, the game may offer them again later, only after you tap to turn them on. They are scheduled on your device by the operating system; they are not push messages, and the notifications themselves send nothing to us or to any server. Game statistics (unless you have turned them off) record that the game was opened by tapping a notification, and which kind of news it was. If several go unopened, they pause on their own. Turn them off in Settings › Park news in the game or in your phone's notification settings.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your puppy: name, coat, personality, bond, learned tricks, needs, memories, journal and look.
  • Your progress: clovers, stamps, wardrobe, park decorations and the play streak.
  • Your settings, such as language, sound, park news and quiet hours. To set the seasons in the park, the game reads your phone's time zone on the device.
  • Which purchases you own, and the purchase transactions already credited, so nothing is granted twice.
  • Counters used to space out ads, such as how many ads were shown today and when the last one was shown.
  • Your ad choice, which the ad software stores so you aren't asked again every launch, the day the game last asked, and whether the one-time note about ads was shown.
  • Game statistics: your choice, the random install number (it stays when you turn statistics off; after "Delete my data" it stays only until our server confirms the deletion) and, while statistics are on, events waiting to be sent (see Game statistics).

Apart from game statistics, none of it is sent to us, and all of it is removed if you delete the app.

What there isn't

  • No account, no sign-in, no cookies.
  • No tracking across apps — not by us and not through the ad software. The game never asks iOS for permission to track you. Our own game statistics are not tracking: the install number stays with us and is never combined with other companies' data.
  • The game never asks for your location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.
  • The game plays offline. It uses the network to load ads (in the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland only after you answer the ad question), for purchases and restores through the store, and to send game statistics to our server at noqyris-ingest.noqyris.workers.dev (unless you have turned them off or, in the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, until you choose "Allow").

App stores

Apple and Google may, independently of us and under their own policies, record downloads, purchases and crash reports. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics and crash reports.

Children

Shapling is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13, and it tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone deletes them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@noqyris.com. Help with the game: Shapling support.

Shapling — politika privatnosti

Poslednja izmena: 24. novembar 2026. Važi za Shapling (Shapling: Moje štene) od verzije 1.2.3. Do verzije 1.2.2 reklamni softver se pokretao tek pošto tapneš „Prihvatam“, pitanje je stizalo na prvi dodir dugmeta ▶ i nikad na dan instalacije, „Odbijam“ je značilo bez ikakvih reklama, reklame koje iskaču nikad nisu dolazile na dan instalacije, a Srbija je bila među zemljama u kojima statistika igre prvo pita.

Shapling nema nalog ni prijavu: štene, park i orman ostaju na tvom telefonu. Igra prikazuje reklame, od kojih je besplatna. U EU, EEA, Ujedinjenom Kraljevstvu i Švajcarskoj prvo pita da li reklame smeju da koriste tvoje lične podatke; ako kažeš ne, reklame se i dalje prikazuju, ali nisu prilagođene tebi. Svuda drugde reklame kreću bez posebnog pitanja. Igra te nikad ne prati i nikad ne koristi reklamni identifikator uređaja. Igra nam šalje i statistiku o tome kako se igra, bez tvog imena, koju možeš da isključiš i obrišeš (vidi „Statistika igre“). Ovde piše šta, zašto i kako to kontrolišeš.

Reklame

Igra može da prikaže dve vrste reklama:

  • Reklame koje sam izabereš (▶). Na tri mesta — u korpici, idejama i na tezgi — dugme ▶ nudi kratak video za tačno navedeni broj detelina. Pušta se samo kad ga tapneš, a nagrada stiže samo ako je video odgledan do kraja. ▶ ponude se pojavljuju posle minuta u parku, najviše četiri videa dnevno i najmanje minut jedan od drugog.
  • Reklame koje iskaču. Igra povremeno može da prikaže kratku reklamu preko celog ekrana na prirodnoj pauzi: kad zatvoriš neki panel ili kad štene zaspi. Kartica je najavi dve sekunde pre početka. Na dan instalacije: ne pre 4 minuta igre u sesiji i najviše jedna po sesiji. Od drugog dana: ne pre 3 minuta igre u sesiji i najviše dve po sesiji. Uvek najmanje 3 minuta igre između dve reklame koje iskaču, nijedna u prvom minutu posle povratka u igru ni 5 minuta posle ▶ videa, nikad usred nege šteneta i najviše četiri dnevno. Broji se samo vreme koje stvarno provodiš u igri. Čim bilo šta kupiš u igri — bilo koji paket ili izgled parka, „Bez reklama koje iskaču“ ili Zlatnu ogrlicu — reklame koje iskaču se više nikad ne prikazuju.

Banera nema. Hranjenje, maženje, šetnja i igra sa štenetom nikad ne zavise od reklama.

Reklame prikazuje Unity LevelPlay, usluga posredovanja reklama kompanije Unity Technologies, sa reklamama iz mreže Unity Ads. Reklamni softver je deo aplikacije, ali podatke šalje direktno kompaniji Unity, a ne nama, i Unity sa njima postupa po sopstvenoj politici privatnosti.

Šta Unity i njegovi reklamni partneri mogu da prikupe

Kad se reklamni softver pokrene (kada je to, piše u „Tvoji izbori“), i kako to Unity opisuje u svojoj dokumentaciji:

  • Identifikator za prodavca (IDFV), koji iOS daje aplikacijama jednog programera na tvom uređaju.
  • Tvoju IP adresu, iz koje se može proceniti približna lokacija (na primer grad).
  • Podatke o uređaju i sistemu, kao što su model uređaja, verzija operativnog sistema, jezik i vrsta veze.
  • Podatke o reklamama, kao što su koje su reklame prikazane, da li je video odgledan do kraja i tapovi na reklamu.
  • Dijagnostiku, kao što su merenja performansi i tehničke greške pri učitavanju i prikazu reklama.

Bez praćenja

Shapling ne traži dozvolu za praćenje (iOS „App Tracking Transparency“) i ne koristi reklamni identifikator uređaja (IDFA): bez te dozvole iOS aplikaciji i reklamnom softveru daje samo prazan identifikator. Unity i njegovi reklamni partneri gore navedene podatke o uređaju koriste da bi prikazali i izmerili reklame kako piše ispod — ne da bi te pratili kroz aplikacije i sajtove drugih kompanija.

Čemu to služi

  • Izboru i prikazu reklama i ograničavanju koliko se često ista reklama ponavlja. Samo gde to tvoj odgovor dozvoljava (vidi „Tvoji izbori“): izboru reklama prilagođenih tebi.
  • Merenju učinka reklama.
  • Otkrivanju prevara i nevažećih klikova i prikaza.
  • Ispravnom radu reklamnog softvera: proveri kvaliteta reklama i otkrivanju i ispravljanju grešaka pri učitavanju i prikazu.

Tvoji izbori

Igra gde si zaključuje po vremenskoj zoni telefona, koju iOS obično podešava prema tome gde je telefon. Vremenska zona koju ne može da pročita računa se kao jedna od zemalja ispod.

U EU, EEA, Ujedinjenom Kraljevstvu i Švajcarskoj

Kad prvi put otvoriš igru, odmah pošto prihvatiš uslove korišćenja, prozor „Tvoja privatnost“ imenuje Unity LevelPlay i pita da li reklame smeju da budu prilagođene tebi. Njegova dva dugmeta, Prihvatam i Odbijam, ravnopravna su i ništa nije unapred izabrano. Dok ne odgovoriš, reklamni softver se uopšte ne pokreće: ne prikuplja ništa i reklama nema. Ako zatvoriš igru bez odgovora, pita te ponovo kad je sledeći put otvoriš, ili kad tapneš dugme ▶.

  • Ako tapneš Prihvatam, reklamni softver se pokreće i reklame mogu biti prilagođene tebi, uz gore navedene podatke. Nikakva druga dozvola se ne traži.
  • Ako tapneš Odbijam, reklamni softver se svejedno pokreće i reklame se i dalje prikazuju, ali nisu prilagođene tebi: igra kaže kompaniji Unity da nisi pristao/la i da ne prodaje i ne deli tvoje lične podatke. Unity i dalje dobija tehničke podatke koji su mu potrebni da isporuči i izbroji reklamu i spreči prevaru, kako opisuje njegova politika. Igra i sva nega šteneta rade isto, šta god izabereš.

Svuda drugde (i u Srbiji i ostatku Balkana van EU)

Igra ne postavlja posebno pitanje o reklamama. Reklamni softver se sam pokreće posle dvadesetak sekundi mirne igre u parku, i reklame mogu biti prilagođene uz gore navedene podatke, nikad uz reklamni identifikator.

Kako menjaš izbor

Otvori Podešavanja › Privatnost i reklame › Izbori za reklame u igri, u bilo kojoj zemlji. Otvara se isti prozor. „Odbijam“ tu znači reklame koje nisu prilagođene tebi od tog trenutka: igra odmah kaže reklamnoj usluzi da ne prodaje i ne deli tvoje lične podatke. „Prihvatam“ vraća reklame prilagođene tebi.

Ako si u verziji 1.2.2 ili ranije tapnuo/la „Odbijam“, kad je to značilo bez ikakvih reklama: od verzije 1.2.3 taj odgovor znači reklame koje nisu prilagođene tebi. Ne pitamo te ponovo; reklamni softver se pokreće, reklame se prikazuju, ali nisu prilagođene tebi, a igra jednom prikaže poruku: „Reklame drže igru besplatnom; ne prate te.“ To možeš kad hoćeš da promeniš u „Izbori za reklame“. Ako si ranije tapnuo/la „Prihvatam“, taj odgovor ostaje.

Ako imaš Zlatnu ogrlicu, reklamni softver se ne pokreće čim igra na ovom uređaju zna za kupovinu, nema nikakvih reklama, a svaka ▶ nagrada stiže odmah, bez reklame. Bez reklama koje iskaču zauvek gasi reklame koje iskaču; ▶ reklame po izboru ostaju ako ih želiš. Posle ponovne instalacije ili na novom telefonu tapni Vrati kupovine da bi igra ponovo znala za njih.

Više o tome kako Unity koristi ove podatke i koje izbore imaš piše u Unity politici privatnosti za igrače i korisnike aplikacija. Zahtev u vezi sa podacima o reklamama šalješ kompaniji Unity, kako piše u njenoj politici: mi te podatke nemamo.

Statistika igre (Noqyris)

Igra nam šalje statistiku o tome kako se igra, da bismo videli gde igrači zapnu ili odustanu, šta je preteško i šta ne radi. Statistiku pravimo i čitamo samo mi (Noqyris); nije reklamna i ne prodajemo je.

Koji podaci

  • Slučajni broj instalacije: nasumičan ID koji igra napravi na tvom telefonu (Apple ga u oznaci privatnosti vodi kao „Device ID“). Nije reklamni identifikator, nije oznaka tvog uređaja i ne daje se nikome, ni našoj reklamnoj mreži (Unity LevelPlay).
  • Kako igraš: početak i kraj igranja, ekrani, zadaci prvog dana, nivoi vezanosti i niz dana igranja, ponude i prikazi reklama sa zaradom po prikazu, odgovori na pitanje o reklamama i na pitanje o obaveštenjima, molbe za ocenu, koja vest iz parka je otvorila igru, greške u igri i vreme učitavanja.
  • Verzija i broj build-a igre, platforma (iOS ili Android), jezik, razlika vremenske zone i datum instalacije (broj dana od instalacije).
  • Zemlja: Cloudflare obrađuje IP adresu radi prenosa i ograničenja broja zahteva; mi je ne upisujemo i iz nje čuvamo samo zemlju.

Ne šaljemo ime, e-poštu, model telefona, reklamni identifikator, ime šteneta ni kupovine.

Pravni osnov

  • U EU, EEA, Ujedinjenom Kraljevstvu i Švajcarskoj: tvoj pristanak (GDPR čl. 6(1)(a)). Igra to prepoznaje po vremenskoj zoni telefona, isto kao za reklame: tada te pita pre nego što bilo šta pošalje, u svom pitanju „Statistika igre“ sa dva jednaka dugmeta, „Dozvoli“ i „Ne dozvoli“, koje se pojavi jednom pošto završiš prvi zadatak prvog dana, i dok ne kažeš „Dozvoli“ ništa ne čuva ni ne šalje.
  • U ostalim zemljama, i u Srbiji: naš legitimni interes da popravljamo igru; statistika je uključena bez pitanja, a možeš da je isključiš kad god hoćeš.

Ko obrađuje podatke

Podatke za nas obrađuje Cloudflare, Inc., po svom ugovoru o obradi podataka. Pojedinačni događaji sa slučajnim brojem instalacije čuvaju se kod Cloudflare-a, moguće i van EU (SAD), uz EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework i standardne ugovorne klauzule. Dnevni zbirovi bez broja instalacije čuvaju se u EU.

Koliko dugo

  • pojedinačni događaji: 3 meseca, pa se brišu sami;
  • dnevni zbirovi (bez broja instalacije, ne mogu se povezati sa tobom): dok postoji igra;
  • brojač dnevnog ograničenja (šifrovan otisak broja instalacije): 2 dana.

Kako da isključiš i obrišeš

U igri otvori Podešavanja › Privatnost i reklame i isključi „Statistika igre“: od tada igra ne šalje ništa, ni posle ponovnog pokretanja. Dugme „Obriši moje podatke“ isključuje statistiku, briše sa telefona ono što je statistika upisala i traži od servera brisanje. Broj instalacije ostaje na telefonu samo dok server ne potvrdi brisanje. Kad server primi zahtev:

  • podaci te instalacije odmah se više ne prikazuju u pregledu po igraču;
  • dnevni zbirovi poslednjih 8 dana računaju se ponovo bez nje u roku od jednog dana;
  • stariji dnevni zbirovi je i dalje sadrže, ali samo kao deo zbira (npr. jedan igrač više), bez broja instalacije;
  • pojedinačni događaji i oznaka brisanja (u njoj je broj instalacije) brišu se sami posle 3 meseca.

Ako telefon nije na mreži, igra ponovo traži brisanje pri svakom sledećem pokretanju. Pošto ne znamo ko si, statistiku možemo da pronađemo samo preko tog dugmeta u igri; e-poštom je ne možemo pronaći ni obrisati. Napredak u igri ostaje.

U EU, EEA, Ujedinjenom Kraljevstvu i Švajcarskoj, ako imaš manje od 16 godina, „Dozvoli“ izaberi samo uz saglasnost roditelja.

Kupovine u aplikaciji

Sve kupovine su opcione i nikad ne diraju negu, igru ni uspomene šteneta: Zlatna ogrlica, „Bez reklama koje iskaču“, osam kozmetičkih paketa i tri izgleda parka, sve jednokratne kupovine. Deteline za tezgu se zarađuju u igri, i uz ▶ video po izboru, i ne mogu da se kupe. Nema pretplata ni nasumičnih nagrada.

Plaćanje obrađuje Apple (ili Google na Android-u). Aplikacija nikad ne vidi tvoje podatke o plaćanju, ime ni Apple ID, a mi iz kupovine ne dobijamo nikakve lične podatke o tebi. Šta si kupio pamti se na tvom uređaju. Vrati kupovine (u Podešavanja › Kupovine i na Tezga › Paketi) traži od prodavnice da ponovo primeni jednokratne kupovine, na primer posle ponovne instalacije.

Vesti iz parka (lokalna obaveštenja)

Ako ih uključiš, Shapling može da ti javi kad se sa štenetom nešto stvarno desi: najviše jednom dnevno, nikad u tihim satima noću, uvek o štenetu i nikad o ponudama. Kad prvi put otvoriš igru, odmah posle uslova korišćenja (a u EU, EEA, Ujedinjenom Kraljevstvu i Švajcarskoj posle pitanja o reklamama), iOS jednom pita da li Shapling sme da šalje obaveštenja; ništa ne zavisi od odgovora. Ako kažeš ne, igra može kasnije ponovo da ih ponudi, i tada traži dozvolu tek pošto tapneš da ih uključiš. Zakazuje ih operativni sistem na tvom uređaju; to nisu push poruke i same vesti ništa ne šalju nama ni bilo kom serveru. Statistika igre (osim ako je isključiš) beleži da je igra otvorena dodirom na vest i koja je vrsta vesti bila. Ako ih nekoliko ostane neotvoreno, same se pauziraju. Isključuješ ih u Podešavanja › Vesti iz parka u igri ili u podešavanjima obaveštenja na telefonu.

Šta se čuva na tvom uređaju

Igra sledeće čuva samo na tvom uređaju, u prostoru same aplikacije:

  • Štene: ime, boju krzna, ličnost, vezanost, naučene trikove, potrebe, uspomene, dnevnik i izgled.
  • Napredak: deteline, pečate, orman, uređenje parka i niz dana igranja.
  • Podešavanja, kao što su jezik, zvuk, vesti iz parka i tihi sati. Za godišnja doba u parku igra na uređaju čita vremensku zonu telefona.
  • Koje kupovine imaš i već uračunate transakcije, da ništa ne bude dodeljeno dvaput.
  • Brojače za razmak između reklama, na primer koliko je reklama danas prikazano i kada poslednja.
  • Tvoj izbor o reklamama, koji čuva reklamni softver da te ne bi pitao pri svakom pokretanju, dan kad te je igra poslednji put pitala i da li je jednokratna poruka o reklamama prikazana.
  • Statistika igre: tvoj izbor, slučajni broj instalacije (ostaje i kad isključiš statistiku; posle „Obriši moje podatke“ ostaje samo dok server ne potvrdi brisanje) i, dok je statistika uključena, događaji koji čekaju slanje (vidi „Statistika igre“).

Osim statistike igre, ništa od toga se ne šalje nama, i sve se briše kad obrišeš aplikaciju.
