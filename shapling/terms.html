Shapling — Terms of Use

Last updated: November 15, 2026.

These terms apply when you play Shapling (Shapling: My Virtual Puppy), a cozy 3D puppy game developed by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). How the game, its statistics and its ad service handle data is described in the Privacy Policy.

1. Apple's licence agreement

You get Shapling from Apple's App Store, so its use is governed by Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement (the Standard EULA). These terms add to it. Where anything here differs from Apple's agreement, Apple's agreement applies.

2. What you may do with the game

You get a personal permission to play Shapling on Apple devices you own or control, under Apple's rules. The puppy, the park, pictures, sounds, texts and code of the game belong to Noqyris. You may not copy, sell or publish them outside the game.

3. Free to play, and optional purchases

The whole game, and all of your puppy's care, can be played without paying. Clovers, stamps and the items in the stall are part of the game only: they have no value outside the game and cannot be exchanged for money. Clovers are earned in the game. The optional purchases, all one-time, are:

  • Golden Collar — every ▶ reward instantly, without an ad;
  • No Pop-up Ads — no pop-up ads, for good; the optional ▶ ads stay available;
  • cosmetic bundles — looks for your puppy, its house and your park.
  • Price. Each price, in your currency, is shown in the App Store and in the stall before you confirm the purchase.
  • Payment. Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm the purchase. There are no subscriptions and no random rewards.
  • Refunds. Payments, refunds and complaints about charges are handled by Apple under its own rules, at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds in Apple's place.
  • Restoring. On a new device or after reinstalling, tap Restore purchases in Settings › Purchases or at Stall › Bundles, signed in to the same Apple account, and your one-time purchases come back. Your puppy and your progress are kept only on the device and are not restored.

4. Ads

The game shows ads only if you agree to them. Then they are shown by Unity LevelPlay: short videos you choose with a ▶ button, for a stated number of clovers, and, from the second day of play, an occasional pop-up ad at a natural pause, announced a moment before it starts. A reward is given only when the video has been watched to the end. The advertiser is responsible for an ad's content, and an ad appearing in Shapling does not mean we recommend what it advertises. You make and change your choice in Settings › Privacy and ads › Ad choices, as the Privacy Policy explains. If you notice an inappropriate ad, write to support@noqyris.com.

5. Game statistics (Noqyris)

Shapling sends us statistics about how it is played (no name, no email, no advertising identifier, no purchases), so we can improve it. Cloudflare, Inc. processes them for us as our processor. In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland and Serbia nothing is sent before you choose "Allow"; everywhere you can turn them off and delete them in Settings › Privacy and ads. The details are in the Privacy Policy, under "Game statistics (Noqyris)".

6. Acceptable use

Do not use Shapling in a way that breaks the law or these terms. In particular:

  • do not get around a purchase, or modify the game to get clovers or purchases without paying or playing;
  • do not fake views of or taps on ads, or automate them; ad networks treat that as invalid traffic;
  • do not take the game apart or extract its content, except as far as the law expressly allows.

7. Your puppy and your progress

Your puppy, progress and settings are kept only on your device. We have no copy and cannot restore them if they are lost, for example when you delete the game.

8. Changes to the game

We may change Shapling: add or change items, features and prices. We do not promise that the game will always be available in the App Store, or that it will work on every device and every version of iOS.

9. Liability

The game is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, Noqyris is not liable for indirect damage arising from use of the game. Nothing in these terms limits the rights the law gives you as a consumer.

10. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we will update this page and the date at the top. Changes do not take away a purchase you have already made.

Shapling — uslovi korišćenja

Poslednja izmena: 15. novembar 2026.

Ovi uslovi važe kada igraš Shapling (Shapling: Moje štene), toplu 3D igru sa štenetom koju razvija Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). Kako igra, njena statistika i reklamna usluga postupaju s podacima, piše u Politici privatnosti.

1. Apple-ov licencni ugovor

Shapling preuzimaš iz Apple-ovog App Store-a, pa za njegovo korišćenje važi Apple-ov standardni licencni ugovor za krajnjeg korisnika (Licensed Application End User License Agreement). Ovi uslovi ga dopunjuju. Ako se nešto u njima razlikuje od Apple-ovog ugovora, važi Apple-ov ugovor.

2. Šta smeš da radiš s igrom

Dobijaš ličnu dozvolu da Shapling igraš na Apple uređajima koje poseduješ ili koristiš, po Apple-ovim pravilima. Štene, park, slike, zvuci, tekstovi i kod igre pripadaju Noqyris-u. Ne smeš ih kopirati, prodavati ni objavljivati van igre.

3. Besplatna igra i kupovine po izboru

Cela igra i sva nega šteneta mogu se igrati bez plaćanja. Deteline, pečati i predmeti sa tezge deo su samo igre: van igre nemaju vrednost i ne mogu se zameniti za novac. Deteline se zarađuju igrom. Kupovine po izboru, sve jednokratne, su:

  • Zlatna ogrlica — svaka ▶ nagrada odmah, bez reklame;
  • Bez reklama koje iskaču — reklame koje iskaču zauvek nestaju; ▶ reklame po izboru ostaju;
  • kozmetički paketi — izgledi za štene, njegovu kućicu i tvoj park.
  • Cena. Svaka cena, u tvojoj valuti, prikazana je u App Store-u i na tezgi pre nego što potvrdiš kupovinu.
  • Naplata. Iznos se naplaćuje preko tvog Apple naloga kada potvrdiš kupovinu. Nema pretplata ni nasumičnih nagrada.
  • Povraćaj novca. Plaćanje, povraćaj novca i reklamacije vodi Apple, po svojim pravilima, na reportaproblem.apple.com. Mi ne možemo da vratimo novac umesto Apple-a.
  • Vraćanje kupovina. Na novom telefonu ili posle ponovne instalacije tapni Vrati kupovine u Podešavanja › Kupovine ili na Tezga › Paketi, sa istim Apple nalogom, i jednokratne kupovine se vraćaju. Štene i napredak čuvaju se samo na telefonu i tako se ne vraćaju.

4. Reklame

Igra prikazuje reklame samo ako pristaneš. Tada ih prikazuje Unity LevelPlay: kratke video snimke koje sam izabereš dugmetom ▶, za tačno naveden broj detelina, i, od drugog dana igre, povremeno reklamu koja iskače na prirodnoj pauzi, najavljenu trenutak pre početka. Nagrada stiže samo ako je video odgledan do kraja. Za sadržaj reklame odgovara oglašivač, i to što se reklama prikazuje u Shapling-u ne znači da preporučujemo ono što reklamira. Izbor praviš i menjaš u Podešavanja › Privatnost i reklame › Izbori za reklame, kako objašnjava Politika privatnosti. Ako primetiš neprimerenu reklamu, piši na support@noqyris.com.

5. Statistika igre (Noqyris)

Shapling nam šalje statistiku o tome kako se igra (bez imena, e-pošte, reklamnog identifikatora i kupovina), da bismo popravljali igru. Za nas je obrađuje Cloudflare, Inc. kao naš obrađivač. U EU, EEA, Ujedinjenom Kraljevstvu, Švajcarskoj i Srbiji ništa se ne šalje dok ne izabereš „Dozvoli“; svuda je možeš isključiti i obrisati u Podešavanja › Privatnost i reklame. Detalji su u Politici privatnosti, pod „Statistika igre (Noqyris)“.

6. Prihvatljivo korišćenje

Ne koristi Shapling na način koji krši zakon ili ove uslove. Posebno:

  • ne zaobilazi kupovinu i ne menjaj igru da bi dobio/la deteline ili kupovine bez plaćanja ili igre;
  • ne gledaj i ne dodiruj reklame lažno ili automatski; reklamne mreže to smatraju nevažećim saobraćajem;
  • ne rastavljaj igru i ne izvlači njen sadržaj, osim koliko ti to zakon izričito dozvoljava.

7. Tvoje štene i napredak

Štene, napredak i podešavanja čuvaju se samo na tvom telefonu. Nemamo njihovu kopiju i ne možemo da ih vratimo ako se izgube, na primer kad obrišeš igru.

9. Odgovornost

Igru pružamo takvu kakva jeste. U meri u kojoj to zakon dozvoljava, Noqyris ne odgovara za posrednu štetu nastalu korišćenjem igre. Ništa u ovim uslovima ne ograničava prava koja ti kao potrošaču daje zakon.

10. Izmene uslova

Ako promenimo ove uslove, izmenićemo ovu stranicu i datum na vrhu. Izmene ne oduzimaju kupovinu koju si već napravio/la.
