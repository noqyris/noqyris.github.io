Polyburst: Chain Reaction — Support

Polyburst is a chain reaction puzzle game for iPhone by Noqyris. Tap a cell to add a side to its shape; the sixth side makes a hexagon, which bursts and hands one side to each of its six neighbours. Build a long chain, then fire it.

Contact

Questions, problems and ideas: subotic.djo@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone you use and the version shown at the bottom of Settings.

Common questions

  • My purchase is missing. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the Apple Account that bought it. Remove Ads comes back. Spark packs are used up as you spend them and are not restored.
  • How do I change my answer about ads? Settings, then Privacy & ads. The question is asked again and your new answer applies from then on.
  • I saw an ad that should not be there. Settings, then Report an ad, or write to the address above and say what the ad showed and roughly when.
  • Do I need an internet connection? No. Every mode plays without one. Ads, purchases and ad rewards need a connection.
  • Is my progress backed up? Progress is kept on your device and is removed if you delete the game.
