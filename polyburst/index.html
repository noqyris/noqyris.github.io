Polyburst: Shape Monsters — Support

Polyburst is a puzzle battle for iPhone by Noqyris. Tap a cell to add a side to its shape; the sixth side makes a hexagon, which bursts and hands one side to each of its six neighbours. Build a long chain and fire it into the monster over the board before its clock runs out.

Contact

Questions, problems and ideas: subotic.djo@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone you use and the version shown at the bottom of Settings.

Common questions

  • My purchase is missing. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the Apple Account that bought it. Remove Ads comes back. Spark packs are used up as you spend them and are not restored.
  • How do I change my answer about ads? In the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Serbia: Settings, then Privacy & ads; the question is asked again and your new answer applies from then on. Elsewhere the game asks no question of its own: whether ads may be tailored to you is your answer to iOS, in iOS Settings, then Polyburst, then Allow Tracking.
  • How do I turn off game statistics, or delete them? Settings, then Game statistics (on or off), and Delete my data, which also asks our server to delete what was sent. Your progress stays.
  • I saw an ad that should not be there. Settings, then Report an ad, or write to the address above and say what the ad showed and roughly when.
  • Do I need an internet connection? No. Every mode plays without one. Ads, purchases and ad rewards need a connection; game statistics wait for one.
  • Is my progress backed up? Progress is kept on your device and is removed if you delete the game.

Privacy

The privacy policy explains what the ad service collects, what the game statistics send to us, and how you control both.
