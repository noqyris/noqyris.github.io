Polyburst: Chain Reaction — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026. Applies to Polyburst version 1.0 and later. Where version 1.2.0 and version 1.2.1 behave differently, the text says which version it is about.

Overview

Polyburst is a chain reaction puzzle game. There are no accounts of ours and no sign-in to us. From version 1.2.1 the game sends us game statistics about how it is played, with a random install number and nothing that names you (see Game statistics), which you can turn off and delete in the game; version 1.2.0 sends none. The game also shows ads, and the ad service it uses collects some data from your device. This policy explains what is collected, why, and how you control it.

Advertising

Unless you own Remove Ads, the game can show two kinds of ads: short videos you choose to watch for a stated reward (for example ten more taps to keep a run going, two sparks, a hint in a puzzle, or an undo), and an occasional full-screen ad when you leave the result of a finished game. A full-screen ad never interrupts a game in progress and is never shown when the app opens or closes; a reward video plays only when you tap to watch it. There is no banner.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange (both Unity Technologies). The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only while tracking is allowed for Polyburst (in iOS's question, or later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Otherwise iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data, such as performance measurements and technical error information about how ads load and display.

What it is used for

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads, only with your consent and, for the advertising identifier, your tracking permission.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity.
  • Keeping the ad software working: finding and fixing problems with how ads load, display and perform.

Your choices

What the game asks, and when, depends on your version of the game. You can see it at the bottom of Settings.

Version 1.2.0

The game asks nothing when it opens, and the ad software does not start until you have answered. The game's own question comes when you leave the result of your first finished Classic game or Daily, or of your third solved puzzle (if it is left unanswered, after a game in a later session), or earlier if you tap an offer to watch an ad for a reward. It is asked wherever your phone is. It says who shows the ads (Polyburst, by Noqyris, through Unity LevelPlay), what Unity Technologies (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange) receives and why, and what declining means. Its two answers, Accept and Decline, look the same.

  • Accept: iOS then asks whether Polyburst may track you (App Tracking Transparency), and the ad software starts once you have answered, or after about 20 seconds if iOS's question is still open; until you choose Allow, iOS gives it no advertising identifier. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown, but your advertising identifier is not shared, so ads cannot be personalised with it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Decline: the ad software is not started at all. No ads are shown, there are no rewards for watching ads, and iOS's tracking question is not shown. Sparks, hints and undos still come from playing. The game asks again only if you tap an ad offer at least 180 days later.

To change your answer, open Settings → Privacy & ads at any time: the question comes again. If you decline there after ads have started, the game stops showing and loading ads and tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information. Version 1.2.0 has no Personalised ads switch.

From version 1.2.1

The first time you open the game, a welcome screen links to our Terms of Use and to this policy; its one button, Accept Terms of Use, takes you on. Players who had an earlier version see it once too. What comes next depends on where your phone is, read from its time zone. A phone whose time zone is UTC, GMT or a similar zone that names no place, or cannot be read, is treated as being in the countries of the next section, and so are a few territories linked to them (such as the Faroe Islands and Svalbard).

In the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (from version 1.2.1)

Right after the welcome the game asks its own question. It says who shows the ads (Polyburst, by Noqyris, through Unity LevelPlay), what Unity Technologies (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange) receives and why, and what declining means. Its two answers, Accept and Decline, look the same. Nothing is sent to the ad service before you answer.

  • Accept: iOS then asks whether Polyburst may track you (App Tracking Transparency), and the ad software starts once you have answered. Ads may then be personalised. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared, so ads cannot be personalised with it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Decline: ads are still shown, but they are not personalised. The ad software is told that you did not consent and not to sell or share your personal information, and iOS's tracking question is not shown. The ad service still receives the device and ad data listed above to show ads, limit how often they repeat, measure them and detect fraud, but not to personalise them. Your advertising identifier is available to the ad software only while tracking is allowed for Polyburst in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (it is allowed there if you chose Allow in Apple's prompt, or switched it on there yourself); declining here does not turn it off.

If you leave it unanswered, no ads are shown, and the question comes again the next time you open the game, when you leave the result of a finished game, or when you tap an offer to watch an ad for a reward. To change your answer, open Settings → Privacy & ads → Change my answer at any time; your new answer applies from then on.

Everywhere else (from version 1.2.1)

The game asks no question of its own about ads. Right after the welcome, iOS asks whether Polyburst may track you across other companies' apps and websites; no screen of ours comes before it. Ads are shown whatever you answer. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared and the ad service is told not to sell or share your personal information. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Polyburst → Allow Tracking.

If you declined in version 1.2.0

In version 1.2.0 a Decline meant no ads at all. From version 1.2.1 you see ads that are not personalised: your "no" to personalisation stands, the Personalised ads switch starts off, you are not asked again, and the game shows a one-time message: "Ads keep Polyburst free. They don't track you." As above, your advertising identifier is available to the ad software only while tracking is allowed for Polyburst in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; your "no" in the game does not turn it off.

Personalised ads, everywhere (from version 1.2.1)

Settings → Privacy & ads has a Personalised ads switch. Turned off, ads are still shown, but they are not personalised: the ad service is told that you did not consent and not to sell or share your personal information. In the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, turning it on after you declined brings the question again. Outside those countries, turning it on after a "no" brings iOS's tracking question, if iOS has not asked before. The page also links to this policy, and outside those countries to the game's page in iOS Settings.

From version 1.2.1, if you have game statistics on (see below), they record which answer you gave to these questions, without any identifier of the ad service.

Report an ad: if an ad looks inappropriate or wrong for the game's age rating, tap Settings → Report an ad. It opens your mail app with a pre-written message, including the game's version; you decide what to send. From version 1.2.1 the message is addressed to support@noqyris.com; in version 1.2.0 it is addressed to an older contact address, which you can change to support@noqyris.com before you send it.

If you own Remove Ads, no ad is shown or requested from the moment of the purchase, the ad software is not started at all from the next time the game opens on this device, and the rewards that others watch an ad for are yours without watching anything. After you reinstall the game or move to a new device, tap Restore purchases in the store or in Settings so the game knows again.

Many Unity ads also show a privacy (shield) icon that you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the choices you have, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in its policy: we don't hold that data.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional: Remove Ads, and packs of sparks (the in-game currency for hints, extra undos and keeping a run going). Purchases are processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase. What you own is remembered on your device, and Restore purchases (in the store and in Settings) asks Apple to re-apply Remove Ads, for example after you reinstall. Sparks you buy are stored on your device and never expire, but like the rest of your progress they are lost if you delete the app.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your progress: best scores, stages and chains, a game in progress, each day's Daily result and your streak, puzzle stars, your sparks and play statistics.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics and reduced motion.
  • Which purchases you own, and the purchase transactions already credited, so sparks are never granted twice.
  • Counters used to space out ads, such as when the last full-screen ad was shown and how many sessions you have played.
  • Your answer to the ad question (where it is asked) and when it was last shown (in version 1.2.0, so that a refusal is not asked again for 180 days). From version 1.2.1 also: that you accepted the welcome screen, the Personalised ads switch, and the answer iOS gave to its tracking question.
  • For game statistics (from version 1.2.1): the random install number, your choice in Settings, and statistics waiting to be sent.

None of it is sent to us, except the game statistics described below, and all of it is removed if you delete the app.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

Version 1.2.0 sends no statistics; this section is about version 1.2.1 and later. The game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; they are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier, not a device ID, and it is never given to anyone, including our ad network (Unity LevelPlay).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, levels and attempts, tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, errors in the game and loading time. Also: whether the Remove Ads offer was shown, tapped or closed (never a purchase), when the game asked iOS for a rating, your Daily streak, and your answers to the ads, tracking and notification questions.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland (by the same time-zone rule as the ads question above): your consent (GDPR and UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection). The game asks you, on the main screen after your first finished game and after the questions about ads, before anything is sent, and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow".
  • In other countries: our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16, turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

Game Center

From version 1.2, Polyburst has two Game Center leaderboards: Classic (your best score) and Daily (your score on the day's board). Game Center is optional. The game never asks you to sign in on its own: if you are already signed in to Game Center on your device, it uses that, and otherwise Apple's Game Center sign-in appears only when you tap the trophy button. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits those two scores to Apple's leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center name. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy; we don't receive your Game Center details. If you are not signed in, the game works the same without leaderboards.

Sharing a result

When you tap Share, the game makes a picture of your result on your device and hands it to iOS's share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice; the game sends it nowhere itself.

Network

The game itself plays fully offline. It uses the network only to load ads (only once you have answered the questions described above where they are asked; in version 1.2.0, after Accept, also once about 20 seconds have passed with iOS's tracking question still open), for purchases and restores through Apple, for Game Center, and, from version 1.2.1, to send game statistics to our server (noqyris-ingest.noqyris.workers.dev, run by Cloudflare for us): in the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland only after you allow them, elsewhere unless you turn them off.

Children's Privacy

Polyburst is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and it tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone (from version 1.2.1) deletes them.
