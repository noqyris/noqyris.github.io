STACKO: Stack Tower Blocks — Support

STACKO is a one-tap 3D stacking game for iPhone by Noqyris. Drop each block onto your tower, chain perfect drops and see how high you can go. Play VANISH, the DAILY tower with three tries, or CLASSIC, which opens at level 3.

Contact

Questions, problems and ideas: stackogame@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone you use and the version shown at the bottom of Settings (the gear on the menu).

Common questions

  • My purchase is missing. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the Apple Account that bought it. The same button is at the bottom of the shop's OFFERS tab. Remove Ads, the Starter Pack, the Legends Pack and STACKO VIP come back. Coin packs are used up as you spend them and are not restored.
  • How do I change my answer about ads? Settings, then Privacy & ads. The question is asked again and your new answer applies from then on.
  • Where did the "watch an ad" offers go? If you declined ads, they stay hidden. You can change your answer in Settings, then Privacy & ads.
  • I saw an ad that should not be there. Settings, then Report an ad, or write to the address above and say what the ad showed and roughly when.
  • How do I turn reminders on or off? Settings, then Daily reminder. They are off until you turn them on, and there is at most one a day.
  • Do I need an internet connection? No. VANISH, DAILY and CLASSIC play without one. Ads, the shop and the Game Center rankings need a connection.
  • When does the DAILY tower change? At 00:00 UTC; the menu counts down to it. The daily gift and the missions start over at midnight where you are.
  • The game paused by itself. Leaving the app, folding the phone or opening it in Split View pauses a running game. Tap RESUME.
  • Is my progress backed up? Progress and coins are kept on your device and are removed if you delete the game. Purchases that last come back with Restore purchases.
