STACKO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026. Applies to STACKO version 1.5.3 and later.

Overview

STACKO is a casual 3D stacking game published by Noqyris. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The game shows ads, and the ad service it uses collects some data from your device. Since version 1.5.3 the game also sends us, the developer (Noqyris), statistics about how it is played, which you can turn off at any time (see Game statistics). This policy explains what is collected, why, and how you control it. The Terms of Use cover everything else about playing the game.

Advertising

Unless you own Remove Ads, the Starter Pack or STACKO VIP, the game can show two kinds of ads: short videos you choose to watch for a stated reward (for example, to continue a run or double your coins), and an occasional full-screen ad between games. Ads never appear during a run, and there is no banner.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from Unity's own ad networks: Unity Ads and, when it is switched on, ironSource Exchange. The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier (all zeros) instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data, such as performance measurements and technical error information about how ads load and display.

What it is used for

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads: only while the Personalised ads switch (below) is on and, in the consent zone described below, after you chose Accept; your advertising identifier only with your tracking permission.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity.
  • Keeping the ad software working: finding and fixing problems with how ads load, display and perform.

Your choices about ads

What the game asks depends on where your phone is, which the game reads from your phone's time zone (iOS sets it from where you are when Set Automatically is on). The consent zone is the European Economic Area (the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway, with their islands and outermost regions), the United Kingdom (with Gibraltar, the Isle of Man, Jersey and Guernsey) and Switzerland. A time zone that names no place (such as UTC or GMT) or cannot be read also counts as inside the zone. Serbia and the other European countries outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Albania, Kosovo, Moldova, Ukraine, Belarus, Russia, Turkey, Andorra, Monaco, San Marino and Vatican City) are outside the zone.

The first launch

The first time you open STACKO (or the first version 1.5.3 or later you open), right after the splash screen and before your first game, the game goes through these steps in order. Each is skipped once it has been answered, and the welcome card appears only once.

  1. Welcome. A card, Welcome to STACKO!, asks you to accept the Terms of Use and to read this Privacy Policy, with a link to each, and has one button, Accept. It is a notice, not a question about ads.
  2. In the consent zone only: our question about ads (below).
  3. Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency): outside the zone always, inside it only after Accept. There is no screen of ours in front of it.
  4. iOS's notification question, for the optional daily reminder (see Reminders).

In the consent zone (EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

The question, Ads and your data, says who shows the ads (STACKO, by Noqyris, through Unity LevelPlay), who receives which data (Unity Technologies, for Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange: information about your device and the app, which ads you see and tap and, if you allow it, your advertising identifier), why, and what each answer means. Both answers are offered the same way and nothing is chosen in advance. The ad software does not start and no ad is shown until you answer. If the question could not be shown at launch, it comes instead when you leave the results of a finished VANISH or CLASSIC game (at most once per session) or when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching an ad.

  • Accept. Ads may be personalised to you. iOS then asks for your tracking permission (it does not ask if your phone lets no apps ask), and the ad software starts. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared, so ads cannot be personalised with it; ads are still shown.
  • Decline. Ads are still shown, but they are not personalised: before the ad software starts, it is told that you do not consent (GDPR consent "no") and that your personal information must not be sold or shared ("do not sell"). Apple's tracking prompt is not shown, and your advertising identifier is not used. Ad rewards (such as a continue or double coins) stay available.

To change your answer at any time, open Settings → Privacy & ads → Change my answer in the game: it shows the same question again, and the new answer applies at once, also while the ad software is already running.

Outside the consent zone (iPhone and iPad)

The game asks no question of its own about ads. Only Apple's tracking prompt appears, at the first launch, asking whether STACKO may track you across other companies' apps and websites; if your phone is set not to let apps ask (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Allow Apps to Request to Track off), iOS shows no prompt and answers Ask App Not to Track for you. The ad software starts once the prompt has an answer, and ads are shown whichever answer it is:

  • If you choose Allow, Unity and its ad partners may use your advertising identifier to tailor ads to you.
  • If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier stays all zeros, and the game tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information.

You can change your answer at any time in your phone's Settings → STACKO → Allow Tracking (or Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking); the game picks up the change the next time you return to it. In the game, Settings → Privacy & ads has a button that opens that page of your phone's Settings.

Personalised ads, in every country

Settings → Privacy & ads has a Personalised ads switch everywhere. Turned off, the ad software is told that you do not consent and that your personal information must not be sold or shared ("do not sell"), so ads are not personalised; ads keep showing either way. In the consent zone, turning it on after a Decline first shows our question again, because personalisation there needs your Accept; outside the zone, if Apple's tracking prompt has never been answered, turning it on shows that prompt first. The same page names who shows the ads and links to this policy.

If you declined in version 1.5.0, 1.5.1 or 1.5.2

In those versions a Decline meant no ads at all. Since version 1.5.3 you see ads that are not personalised: your "no" to personalisation stands, the Personalised ads switch starts off, you are not asked again, and the game shows a one-time message: "Ads keep STACKO free. They don't track you." You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads.

Everywhere

If you own Remove Ads, the Starter Pack or STACKO VIP, the game shows you no ads and asks you nothing about them once it knows about your purchase on this device, because the ad software is never started for you; the Privacy & ads row is then not shown. After you reinstall the game or move to a new device, it knows again as soon as you open the shop or tap Restore Purchases. Until then you may see the ads question or Apple's tracking prompt at launch, and ads stop the moment your purchase is restored.

Many Unity ads also show a privacy (shield) icon that you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the choices you have, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in its policy: we don't hold that data. If you see an ad you think is inappropriate, use Report an ad in Settings to email us.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

The game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; they are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier, not a device ID, and it is never given to anyone, including our ad network (Unity LevelPlay).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, runs and attempts per mode (VANISH, CLASSIC, DAILY) with the floors reached, the time taken and whether a continue was used, the first-run tips, your daily streak, when the rating prompt or a shop offer was shown or tapped, your answers to the ads question and to Apple's tracking prompt, ad offers and ad views (full-screen ads and reward videos) with the revenue per view the ad network reports, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the European Economic Area (the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever the phone's time zone names no place (such as UTC) or cannot be read: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game asks you before anything is sent and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow". The Game statistics question comes on its own, separate from the ads question, after a finished game.
  • In other countries, Serbia included: our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time. There, statistics are on from the first launch and the game asks nothing about them.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings → Purchases & privacy, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16, turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional: Remove Ads, the Starter Pack, the Legends Pack, STACKO VIP and coin packs. Purchases are processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase; purchases are never part of the game statistics. What you own is remembered on your device, and Restore Purchases (in the shop and in Settings) asks Apple to re-apply your one-time purchases, for example after you reinstall. Coins you buy are stored on your device and never expire, but like the rest of your progress they are lost if you delete the app.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. The game never asks you to sign in on its own: if you are already signed in to Game Center on your device, it uses that, and otherwise Apple's Game Center sign-in appears only when you tap SIGN IN on the leaderboard (RANKS). If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your scores to Apple's leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center name. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy; we don't receive your Game Center details. If you are not signed in, the game works the same without leaderboards.

Reminders (local notifications)

STACKO can send one reminder a day at most, for example when the new DAILY tower or your gift is ready. At the first launch iOS asks whether STACKO may send notifications; if you allow it, the daily reminder is on, and if you don't, it stays off. You can also turn it on later in the game's Settings (iOS asks first if it has not yet). Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS; nothing about them is sent to us or to a push server. To pick a good time, the game remembers on your device the hours at which you usually play. You can turn reminders off at any time in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications → STACKO.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your progress: scores and records per mode, coins, themes, level and XP, missions, daily gift, streak, achievements and play statistics.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics and reminders, and the hours you usually play (used only to time reminders).
  • Which purchases you own, and the purchase transactions already credited, so coins are never granted twice.
  • Counters used to space out ads, such as how many games you have played since the last full-screen ad.
  • Your privacy choices: that you accepted the welcome card; your answer to the ads question, which the ad software stores so you aren't asked again every launch; the position of the Personalised ads switch; whether the one-time "They don't track you" message is behind you; when the game last showed you the ads question; the answer you gave to Apple's tracking prompt, as the game last read it; and a flag recording that the game has already cleared, once, any consent answer left over from versions before 1.5 (which used Google's ad tools; 1.5 removed them).
  • Whether the app has already asked you to rate it.
  • For the game statistics: the random install number, your statistics choice and events not sent yet. In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and with a time zone that names no place or cannot be read), none of these is stored before your choice. Delete my data removes them, keeping only your "no" (and the install number until the server has confirmed the deletion).

A backup copy of the game's own data (not the statistics) is kept in the app's own preferences storage on the same device, so your progress survives if iOS clears the game's web storage. Apart from what the game statistics send (see Game statistics), none of this is sent to us. All of it is removed if you delete the app.

Ratings and sharing

After a new record the game may ask Apple to show its standard rating prompt. Apple handles the prompt and any rating you leave. The Share button opens your device's share sheet with a short text about your score and a link to STACKO on the App Store; where you send it is up to you.

Network

The game itself plays fully offline. It uses the network only to load ads (in the consent zone once you have answered the ads question, whichever answer; elsewhere once iOS has your answer to its tracking prompt, or at once if you declined the ads question in version 1.5.0–1.5.2), to send the game statistics to our server at Cloudflare (unless you turned them off; in the consent zone only after you allowed them), for purchases and restores through Apple, for Game Center, and for Apple's rating prompt.

Children

STACKO is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone deletes them. The game also tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app; to make a request about ad data, contact Unity as described above.
