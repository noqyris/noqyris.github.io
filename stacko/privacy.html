STACKO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026. Applies to STACKO version 1.5.1 and later.

Overview

STACKO is a casual 3D stacking game. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we, the developer, run no servers: nothing you do in the game is sent to us. The game does show ads, and the ad service it uses collects some data from your device. This policy explains what is collected, why, and how you control it.

Advertising

Unless you own Remove Ads, the Starter Pack or STACKO VIP, the game can show two kinds of ads: short videos you choose to watch for a stated reward (for example, to continue a run or double your coins), and an occasional full-screen ad between games. Ads never appear during a run, and there is no banner.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from Unity's own ad networks: Unity Ads and, when it is switched on, ironSource Exchange. The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data, such as performance measurements and technical error information about how ads load and display.

What it is used for

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads, only with your consent and, for the advertising identifier, your tracking permission.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity.
  • Keeping the ad software working: finding and fixing problems with how ads load, display and perform.

Your choices

STACKO never asks about ads when you open it, and the ad software does not start until you agree. The first time you finish a game (VANISH or CLASSIC), the game asks you, as you leave the results, whether you agree to ads; it also asks if you tap a button that offers a reward for watching an ad before you have answered. If a question is left unanswered, it may come again after a game in a later session. The question says who shows the ads (STACKO, by Noqyris, through Unity LevelPlay), who receives which data (Unity Technologies, for Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange: information about your device and the app, which ads you see and tap and, if you allow it, your advertising identifier), why, and what declining means. Both answers are offered the same way:

  • Accept. iOS then asks for your tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), and the ad software starts. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared, so ads cannot be personalised with it; ads are still shown. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Decline. The ad software is not started at all, so it collects nothing, no ads are shown and there are no ad rewards (such as a continue or double coins); coins, which also buy continues, can still be earned by playing. iOS does not ask for tracking permission. The game does not ask you again on its own: only if you tap a button that offers an ad reward, and never within 180 days of your answer.

To change your answer at any time, open Settings → Privacy & ads in the game: it shows the same question again. If you withdraw consent after ads have started, the game stops showing and loading ads at once, tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information, and from the next launch does not start the ad software at all.

If you own Remove Ads, the Starter Pack or STACKO VIP, the game shows you no ads and does not ask about them once it knows about your purchase on this device. After you reinstall the game or move to a new device, it knows again as soon as you open the shop or tap Restore Purchases. Until then you may be asked after a game (you can decline), and ads stop the moment your purchase is restored.

Many Unity ads also show a privacy (shield) icon that you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the choices you have, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in its policy: we don't hold that data. If you see an ad you think is inappropriate, use Report an ad in Settings to email us.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional: Remove Ads, the Starter Pack, the Legends Pack, STACKO VIP and coin packs. Purchases are processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase. What you own is remembered on your device, and Restore Purchases (in the shop and in Settings) asks Apple to re-apply your one-time purchases, for example after you reinstall. Coins you buy are stored on your device and never expire, but like the rest of your progress they are lost if you delete the app.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. The game never asks you to sign in on its own: if you are already signed in to Game Center on your device, it uses that, and otherwise Apple's Game Center sign-in appears only when you tap SIGN IN on the leaderboard (RANKS). If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your scores to Apple's leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center name. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy; we don't receive your Game Center details. If you are not signed in, the game works the same without leaderboards.

Reminders (local notifications)

STACKO can send one reminder a day at most, for example when the new DAILY tower or your gift is ready. Reminders are off unless you turn them on in the game, and iOS asks for your permission first. They are scheduled on your device by iOS; nothing is sent to us or to a push server. To pick a good time, the game remembers on your device the hours at which you usually play. You can turn reminders off at any time in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications → STACKO.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your progress: scores and records per mode, coins, themes, level and XP, missions, daily gift, streak, achievements and play statistics.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics and reminders, and the hours you usually play (used only to time reminders).
  • Which purchases you own, and the purchase transactions already credited, so coins are never granted twice.
  • Counters used to space out ads, such as how many games you have played since the last full-screen ad.
  • Your ad consent choice, which the ad software stores so you aren't asked again every launch; when the game last asked you, so that a "no" is not asked again within 180 days; and a flag recording that the game has already cleared, once, any consent answer left over from versions before 1.5 (which used Google's ad tools; 1.5 removed them).
  • Whether the app has already asked you to rate it.

A backup copy of this data is kept in the app's own preferences storage on the same device, so your progress survives if iOS clears the game's web storage. None of it is sent to us, and all of it is removed if you delete the app.

Ratings and sharing

After a new record the game may ask Apple to show its standard rating prompt. Apple handles the prompt and any rating you leave. The Share button opens your device's share sheet with a short text about your score and a link to STACKO on the App Store; where you send it is up to you.

Network

The game itself plays fully offline. It uses the network only to load ads (after you have given consent), for purchases and restores through Apple, for Game Center, and for Apple's rating prompt.

Children's Privacy

STACKO is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and it tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected through the ads in this game, contact us at the address below.
