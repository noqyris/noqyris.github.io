STACKO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026. Applies to STACKO version 1.5.2 and later. Version 1.5.3 changes how the game asks about ads and adds game statistics; where version 1.5.2 works differently, this page says so (see If you play version 1.5.2).

Overview

STACKO is a casual 3D stacking game published by Noqyris. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The game shows ads, and the ad service it uses collects some data from your device. Since version 1.5.3 the game also sends us, the developer (Noqyris), statistics about how it is played, which you can turn off at any time (see Game statistics). This policy explains what is collected, why, and how you control it. The Terms of Use cover everything else about playing the game.

If you play version 1.5.2

The version number is shown at the bottom of the game's Settings. Version 1.5.2 differs from what the rest of this page describes in these ways:

  • No welcome card and no question at launch. The first time you finish a game (VANISH or CLASSIC), the game asks you, as you leave the results, whether you agree to ads, in every country; it also asks if you tap a button that offers a reward for watching an ad before you have answered. The question, Ads and your data, offers Accept and Decline the same way.
  • Accept: iOS then asks for your tracking permission, and the ad software starts. Decline: the ad software is not started at all, so it collects nothing, no ads are shown and there are no ad rewards; iOS does not ask for tracking permission. For 180 days after a Decline the buttons that offer an ad reward are not shown; after that they come back, and tapping one asks the question again. The game never asks again on its own.
  • Settings → Privacy & ads shows the same question again; there is no Personalised ads switch. If you withdraw consent after ads have started, the game stops loading and showing ads.
  • No game statistics. Version 1.5.2 sends us nothing; the section Game statistics applies from version 1.5.3.
  • Reminders are off until you turn them on in the game; iOS asks about notifications only then.

When you update to 1.5.3, the welcome card and the steps below appear once. If you declined in 1.5.2, see If you declined in version 1.5.0, 1.5.1 or 1.5.2.

Advertising

Unless you own Remove Ads, the Starter Pack or STACKO VIP, the game can show two kinds of ads: short videos you choose to watch for a stated reward (for example, to continue a run or double your coins), and an occasional full-screen ad between games. Ads never appear during a run, and there is no banner.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from Unity's own ad networks: Unity Ads and, when it is switched on, ironSource Exchange. The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only while tracking is allowed for STACKO on your phone (you chose Allow when iOS asked, or switched it on later in iOS Settings). Otherwise iOS gives the app a blank identifier (all zeros) instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data, such as performance measurements and technical error information about how ads load and display.

What it is used for

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads: since version 1.5.3 only while the Personalised ads switch (below) is on and, in the consent zone described below, after you chose Accept (in version 1.5.2: after you chose Accept); your advertising identifier only with your tracking permission.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity.
  • Keeping the ad software working: finding and fixing problems with how ads load, display and perform.

Your choices about ads (version 1.5.3 and later)

This section is for players who are shown ads; if you own Remove Ads, the Starter Pack or STACKO VIP, see Everywhere below.

What the game asks depends on where your phone is, which the game reads from your phone's time zone (iOS sets it from where you are when Set Automatically is on). The consent zone is the European Economic Area (the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway, with their islands and outermost regions), the United Kingdom (with Gibraltar, the Isle of Man, Jersey and Guernsey) and Switzerland. A time zone that names no place (such as UTC or GMT) or cannot be read also counts as inside the zone. Serbia and the other European countries outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Albania, Kosovo, Moldova, Ukraine, Belarus, Russia, Turkey, Andorra, Monaco, San Marino and Vatican City) are outside the zone.

The first launch

The first time you open STACKO (or the first version 1.5.3 or later you open), right after the splash screen and before your first game, the game goes through these steps in order. Each is skipped once it has been answered, and the welcome card appears only once. Our ads question comes back at a later launch only if your answer was cleared: turning Personalised ads on outside the zone clears a Decline you gave inside it.

  1. Welcome. A card, Welcome to STACKO!, asks you to accept the Terms of Use and to read this Privacy Policy, with a link to each, and has one button, Accept Terms of Use. It is a notice, not a question about ads.
  2. In the consent zone only: our question about ads (below).
  3. Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency): outside the zone, unless you declined our question before (see below); inside it only after Accept. There is no screen of ours in front of it.
  4. iOS's notification question, for the optional daily reminder (see Reminders).

In the consent zone (EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

The question, Ads and your data, says who shows the ads (STACKO, by Noqyris, through Unity LevelPlay), who receives which data (Unity Technologies, for Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange: information about your device and the app, which ads you see and tap and, if you allow it, your advertising identifier), why, and what each answer means. Both answers are offered the same way and nothing is chosen in advance. Until you answer, no ad is shown and the ad software is not started; if it already started while your phone was outside the zone, it keeps running, shows no ad and is told not to sell or share your personal information. If the question could not be shown at launch, it comes instead when you leave the results of a finished VANISH or CLASSIC game (at most once per session) or when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching an ad.

  • Accept. Ads may be personalised to you, and the Personalised ads switch (below) is turned on. iOS then asks for your tracking permission, unless it already has your answer on this install or your phone lets no apps ask, and the ad software starts. If you then choose Ask App Not to Track, your Accept still stands, but iOS gives the app a blank advertising identifier (all zeros); ads are still shown.
  • Decline. Ads are still shown, but not personalised: the ad software is told that you do not consent (GDPR consent "no") and that your personal information must not be sold or shared ("do not sell"), before it starts or, if it is already running, at once. Your Decline also turns the Personalised ads switch off, and it stays off outside the zone too, until you turn it on. Apple's tracking prompt is not shown. Your advertising identifier is available to the ad software only while tracking is allowed for STACKO in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (it is allowed there if you chose Allow in Apple's prompt, or switched it on there yourself); declining here does not turn it off. Ad rewards (such as a continue or double coins) stay available.

To change your answer at any time, open Settings → Privacy & ads → Change my answer in the game: it shows the same question again, and the new answer applies at once, also while the ad software is already running, and sets the Personalised ads switch the same way.

Outside the consent zone (iPhone and iPad)

The game asks no question of its own about ads. Only Apple's tracking prompt can appear: at the first launch, or, if it could not be shown then, after a finished game or when you tap a reward offer, and not at all if iOS already has your answer on this install. It asks whether STACKO may track you across other companies' apps and websites; if your phone is set not to let apps ask (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Allow Apps to Request to Track off), iOS shows no prompt and answers Ask App Not to Track for you. The ad software starts once the prompt has an answer, and ads are shown whichever answer it is. If you declined our question (in the consent zone, or in version 1.5.0, 1.5.1 or 1.5.2), the prompt is not shown and the ad software starts without it, with Personalised ads off. At a start where the game cannot read your earlier answer to our question, it does not show this prompt by itself, and ads are not personalised unless you turn Personalised ads on (which asks iOS first if it has no answer yet); if you had no earlier answer and iOS has none either, the prompt can come at a later start where that read works.

  • If you choose Allow, Unity and its ad partners may use your advertising identifier to tailor ads to you while Personalised ads (below) is on.
  • If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier stays all zeros, and the game tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information.

You can change your answer at any time in your phone's Settings → STACKO → Allow Tracking (or Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking); the game picks up the change the next time you return to it. In the game, Settings → Privacy & ads has a button that opens that page of your phone's Settings.

Personalised ads, in every country

Settings → Privacy & ads has a Personalised ads switch everywhere. Turned off, the ad software is told that you do not consent and that your personal information must not be sold or shared ("do not sell"), so ads are not personalised; ads keep showing either way. Your answer to our question in the consent zone sets this switch too: Accept turns it on, Decline turns it off. The switch holds in every country: a Decline, or the switch turned off, in one place still applies when your phone is somewhere else. In the consent zone, turning it on after a Decline first shows our question again, because personalisation there needs your Accept; outside the zone, if Apple's tracking prompt has never been answered, turning it on shows that prompt first. Turning it on outside the zone also clears a Decline given in the zone, so the next time your phone is in the zone our question is asked again. The same page names who shows the ads and links to this policy.

If you declined in version 1.5.0, 1.5.1 or 1.5.2

In those versions a Decline meant no ads at all. Since version 1.5.3 you see ads that are not personalised: the game keeps your Decline as a "no" to personalised ads, the Personalised ads switch starts off, you are not asked again, and the game shows a one-time message: "STACKO now shows ads. Because you said no before, personalised ads are off. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & ads."

Everywhere

If you own Remove Ads, the Starter Pack or STACKO VIP, the game shows you no ads once it knows about your purchase on this device. From the next launch after that, the ad software is not started for you, the game asks you nothing about ads, and the Privacy & ads row is not shown; in the session in which you buy, the ad software may already be running or may still start, without showing you ads, and the row stays until the game next starts. After you reinstall the game or move to a new device, it knows again as soon as you open the shop or tap Restore Purchases. Until then you may see the ads question or Apple's tracking prompt at launch (in version 1.5.2: the ads question after a game), and ads stop the moment your purchase is restored.

Many Unity ads also show a privacy (shield) icon that you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the choices you have, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in its policy: we don't hold that data. If you see an ad you think is inappropriate, use Report an ad in Settings to email us.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

Since version 1.5.3, the game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics, to improve the game, including how often and where ads appear. We do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier and not a device ID, and we never give it to our ad network (Unity LevelPlay); Cloudflare stores it for us, as described under Who processes the data.
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, runs and attempts per mode (VANISH, CLASSIC, DAILY) with the floors reached, the time taken and whether a continue was used, the first-run tips, your daily streak, when the rating prompt or a shop offer was shown or tapped, each time the game asked for our ads question (whether or not it appeared), the answer that stood after it (or that there was none), the first answer you gave on Apple's tracking prompt, ad offers and ad views (full-screen ads and reward videos) with the revenue per view the ad network reports, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the European Economic Area (the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever the phone's time zone names no place (such as UTC) or cannot be read: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game asks you before anything is sent and sends nothing until you choose "Allow". What happens in the game before you answer waits on your phone and is sent only if you choose Allow; Don't allow discards it. The Game statistics question comes on its own, separate from the ads question, after a finished game.
  • In other countries, Serbia included: our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time. There, statistics are on from the first launch and the game asks nothing about them.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings → Purchases & privacy, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16, turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional: Remove Ads, the Starter Pack, the Legends Pack, STACKO VIP and coin packs. Purchases are processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase; purchases are never part of the game statistics. What you own is remembered on your device, and Restore Purchases (in the shop and in Settings) asks Apple to re-apply your one-time purchases, for example after you reinstall. Coins you buy are stored on your device and never expire, but like the rest of your progress they are lost if you delete the app.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. The game never asks you to sign in on its own: if you are already signed in to Game Center on your device, it uses that, and otherwise Apple's Game Center sign-in appears only when you tap SIGN IN on the leaderboard (RANKS). If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your scores to Apple's leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center name. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy; we don't receive your Game Center details. If you are not signed in, the game works the same without leaderboards.

Reminders (local notifications)

STACKO can send one reminder a day at most, for example when the new DAILY tower or your gift is ready. At the first launch iOS asks whether STACKO may send notifications, unless it already has your answer; if you allow it there, the daily reminder is on, and if you don't, it stays off. You can also turn it on later in the game's Settings (iOS asks first if it has not yet). Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS; nothing about them is sent to us or to a push server. To pick a good time, the game remembers on your device the hours at which you usually play. (In version 1.5.2 iOS asks only when you turn the reminder on in the game.) You can turn reminders off at any time in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications → STACKO.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your progress: scores and records per mode, coins, themes, level and XP, missions, daily gift, streak, achievements and play statistics.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics and reminders, and the hours you usually play (used only to time reminders).
  • Which purchases you own, and the purchase transactions already credited, so coins are never granted twice.
  • Counters used to space out ads, such as how many games you have played since the last full-screen ad.
  • Your privacy choices: that you accepted the welcome card; your answer to the ads question, which the ad software stores so you aren't asked again every launch; the position of the Personalised ads switch; whether the one-time message for an earlier Decline is behind you; when the game last showed you the ads question; the answer you gave to Apple's tracking prompt, as the game last read it; and a flag recording that the game has already cleared, once, any consent answer left over from versions before 1.5 (which used Google's ad tools; 1.5 removed them).
  • Whether the app has already asked you to rate it.
  • For the game statistics: the random install number, your statistics choice and events not sent yet. In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and with a time zone that names no place or cannot be read), none of these is stored before your choice, unless the game was first opened outside them: what it stored there stays on your phone, unsent, until you choose. Delete my data removes them, keeping only your "no" (and the install number until the server has confirmed the deletion).

A backup copy of the game's own data (not the statistics) is kept in the app's own preferences storage on the same device, so your progress survives if iOS clears the game's web storage. Apart from what the game statistics send (see Game statistics), none of this is sent to us. All of it is removed if you delete the app.

Ratings and sharing

After a new record the game may ask Apple to show its standard rating prompt. Apple handles the prompt and any rating you leave. The Share button opens your device's share sheet with a short text about your score and a link to STACKO on the App Store; where you send it is up to you.

Network

The game itself plays fully offline. It uses the network for the ad software (in version 1.5.2 only after you accepted the ads question; since 1.5.3: in the consent zone once you have answered the ads question, whichever answer, or if it already started while your phone was outside the zone; elsewhere once iOS has your answer to its tracking prompt, or at once if you declined the ads question), to send the game statistics to our server at Cloudflare (since 1.5.3, unless you turned them off; in the consent zone only after you allowed them), for purchases and restores through Apple, for Game Center, for Apple's rating prompt, and to open the pages you choose to open, such as this policy.

Children

STACKO is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone deletes them. The game also tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app; to make a request about ad data, contact Unity as described above.
