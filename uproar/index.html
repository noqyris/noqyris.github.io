Uproar

2-8 players · a phone each · Bluetooth · no wifi · zero ads

A party pack for people in the same room, played phone to phone. Someone taps HOST, everyone else taps JOIN and picks that room from the list, and two to eight of you play the same round at the same moment — each on your own phone, linked nearby over Bluetooth. Plays with no wifi and no internet: no account.

Get it on the App Store

How a room works

  • Every player needs an iPhone or iPad with Uproar installed and Bluetooth on.
  • Any phone can host; up to seven more join it. Every room has its own glyph, so you can tell yours from the one at the next table.
  • The host picks a mix of games or just one, three, five or seven rounds, and taps START.
  • If a phone drops out mid-round, a bot finishes that round for it and the phone rejoins for the next one.
  • Nobody else around? Practice any game against bots at three honest difficulties, on your own.

Six games free to host

  • Reflex (2-8) — the screen turns green: tap! Everyone gets a time, fastest wins.
  • Odd One (2-8) — the same grid on every phone; spot the odd tile.
  • Tug (2) — an accuracy duel.
  • Sequence (2-8) — watch the pads, tap them back; one more each time, up to ten.
  • Stop the Clock (2-8) — stop as close to 5.00 as you can; two goes, best one counts.
  • Stack (2-8) — drop the block; a point a block, perfect drops in a row score extra, and most points wins.

Seven more with Uproar Plus

  • Sumo (2-8) — push the others out of a shrinking ring.
  • Air Hockey (2) — your goal at the bottom of your own screen.
  • Penalties (2) — one aims, one dives, both at once.
  • Count (2-8) — count the shape, tap how many; closest wins.
  • Snap (2-8) — slam when a card matches the one before it.
  • Paint (2-8) — every square you cross turns your color.
  • Dodge (2-8) — keep clear of the light; last one standing wins.

Uproar Plus is one optional in-app purchase, made once by whoever hosts: it lets you host all thirteen games for good, on all your devices, and Family Sharing covers the family. Guests never pay — everyone who joins a Plus host's room plays every game that host offers. Practice has all thirteen against the bots at no cost. Restore it from Profile.

How Bluetooth is used

Only to link the phones at your table. Uproar talks to other phones running Uproar, in Bluetooth range, and nothing else: no internet, no server, no account. Nothing leaves the room. Only buying Plus goes online, to the App Store. The permission is asked the first time you tap HOST or JOIN, never at launch. See the privacy policy.

What is not in it

No ads, no subscription, no coins, no tracking, no internet needed to play, no chat, no typed names, no online mode pretending to be people. Levels, unlocks and badges are earned by playing; nothing is random and no unlock is for sale.
