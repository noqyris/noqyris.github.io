Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Uproar ("the app") collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Nothing is sent to us or to any server.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps your progress — level and XP, unlocks and what you have equipped, badges, personal bests, your table streak, and a head-to-head record against the phones you have played — plus your sound and vibration settings, locally on your device only, using the operating system's standard storage. It is removed if you delete the app.

Bluetooth

Uproar uses Bluetooth for one thing: linking the phones at your table into one game. It only talks to other phones running Uproar, within Bluetooth range, and only while you are hosting, joining or playing in a room. The app asks for the Bluetooth permission the first time you tap HOST or JOIN, never at launch, and works without it in Practice.

What travels between the phones in a room, and nowhere else:

  • a random eight-character id your phone made up when the app was first opened (not a device identifier, and not linked to you), from which every phone draws the same generated name, such as "Brave Otter";
  • your level and the cosmetics you have equipped;
  • the game itself: your taps and moves, and the round results.

The other phones in the room keep that random id in their own on-device head-to-head record, the same way yours keeps theirs. While a phone hosts, it advertises a short random room code so nearby phones can list the room; it stops when the room closes. Nothing leaves the room: there is no internet connection and no server.

Network

The app does not connect to the internet. Every game, at a table or in Practice, works with the phone in airplane mode with Bluetooth switched on. The one exception is buying or restoring Uproar Plus, which iOS carries out with Apple's App Store.

Advertising and purchases

The app shows no ads and contains no third-party advertising software. It offers one optional in-app purchase, Uproar Plus, which Apple processes through the App Store; we never see your Apple ID or payment details. The app receives only whether Plus is owned and keeps that one fact on your device. Nothing about it is sent to us.

Player names

Players are shown as a colour, a shape and a generated name. The app never asks anyone to type a name or any other text, and there is no chat.

Rating prompt

After a few games the app may show the operating system's standard "rate this app" dialog. That dialog is provided and handled entirely by Apple; the app receives nothing from it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new date.
