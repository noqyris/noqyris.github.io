Common questions

How do I find hidden objects?
Each room lists what to find at the bottom of the screen: look for an object in the picture and tap it. Stuck? Use a Hint — it gently highlights the nearest object.

What are stars for?
Completing a room earns stars. Spend them in The Parlor to restore the house, piece by piece.

Will my progress be saved?
Yes — automatically, on your device. It stays through restarts and updates, and travels with your device when iOS backs it up or moves it to a new phone. It is not stored online, and there is no account: deleting the app deletes your progress.

I ran out of energy.
Energy refills over time on its own — come back a little later, or spend coins you've earned. If you have accepted ads, you can also watch a short video for one more.

I bought something and it is gone.
After a reinstall or on a new device, open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. It brings back Remove Ads, if you bought it. Coins, hints and energy are part of your progress on the device where you bought them, so they are not restored.

Where are the ads, and how do I turn them off?
The only ads are short videos you choose to watch for extra energy or a hint. The game asks first; you can change your answer at any time under Settings (the gear on the house screen) → Privacy & ads, and report an ad that looks wrong from the same place.
