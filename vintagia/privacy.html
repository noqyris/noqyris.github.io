Privacy Policy

Vintagia · Last updated: 25 November 2026. Applies to Vintagia 2.5 and later (the game statistics: 2.7 and later; Liftoff: 2.8 and later; the Personalised ads switch and the ad question counting together: 2.8.2 and later); the last section covers version 2.4 and earlier.

Vintagia is a single-player hidden-object game by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). This policy explains what leaves your device, who receives it, why, and how you control it.

Your progress

Your progress and settings — energy, coins, hints, stars, finished rooms, collected curios, renovations and your daily-reward streak — are saved on your device, in the game's own storage, and nowhere else. The game has no account and no login, and it does not copy your progress to any server of ours (from version 2.7 it sends the game statistics described below, which are not your progress). Deleting the app deletes it. (It is part of the app's data, so it is included when iOS backs up or transfers your device.)

Advertising

Vintagia shows two kinds of ad; when the ad software starts is described under Your choices. A short video you choose to watch for a stated reward — extra energy, or a hint — plays only when you tap the offer. From version 2.6, a short full-screen ad can also come between rooms: only when you leave a room you have finished, never during a room, never in your first rooms, at most every second room and a few times a day, and never if you have bought anything in the game. There are no banners.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange (both Unity Technologies) and, from version 2.8, from Liftoff (Liftoff Monetize), which bids in the same auction for each ad. The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to these companies, not to us, and each of them handles that data under its own privacy policy (linked below).

What these ad companies may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (when iOS asks, or later in the iOS Settings app). If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data about how ads load and display.

What it is used for:

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads, as far as the game lets them: what it tells the ad software about your choices is described under Your choices.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity, and keeping the ad software working.

Your choices

When you open the game for the first time, a welcome screen links the Terms of Use and this policy. Then the game asks its questions, one after the other, and skips each one that already has an answer: about ads (which question depends on where your device is, read from its time zone, as described below) and, through iOS, whether it may send you reminders (when your energy is full or your daily gift is ready). Where the game asks its own question about ads, the ad software does not start before that question has an answer. A question left unanswered can come again: the ad question after a room you finish, when you tap an offer to watch an ad for a reward, or at a later launch. The game does not bring up the ad question on its own while it knows that you own Remove Ads.

In the European Economic Area (the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, where the law asks for your consent before ad software uses your data to personalise ads, and on a device whose time zone names no place or cannot be read, the game asks its own question first (below: "where the game asks its own question"). It says who shows the ads (Vintagia, by Noqyris, through Unity LevelPlay), what the ad networks — Unity Technologies (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange) and Liftoff — receive and use and why, and what declining means. Its two answers, Accept and Decline, look the same. If you accepted before version 2.8, when the question named Unity Technologies only, the game asks again and starts no ads until you answer.

  • Accept: iOS asks whether Vintagia may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency), unless it already has your answer; then the ad software starts and is told that you consent (GDPR consent true). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your consent stands, but the app gets no advertising identifier. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Decline: the ad software starts and is told that you do not consent (GDPR consent false) and that your personal information must not be sold or shared. The game does not bring up iOS's tracking question. (Until version 2.8, declining meant no ads at all; if you declined then, the game tells you once that ads are now shown.)

To change your answer, open Settings (in the bar at the bottom of the house screen) → Privacy & ads at any time. If the ad software is already running, the game tells it your new answer at once (an ad already loaded before your change can still be shown). The game statistics (below), if they are on, record each time the game asks this question and the answer that stands after it.

Everywhere else (since version 2.8 also Serbia and the rest of the Western Balkans), the game asks no question of its own; the only question is iOS's own (App Tracking Transparency): whether Vintagia may use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. iOS shows it unless it already has your answer. If you declined the game's own question earlier (where that question is asked, or before version 2.8), the ad software starts without iOS's question, as after a decline; otherwise it starts after you answer, whichever answer you give. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the app gets no advertising identifier and the game tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information. You can change your answer at any time in the iOS Settings app (Vintagia → Allow Tracking). In the game, Settings → Privacy & ads explains this, opens that page and has a Personalised ads switch: turned off, the game tells the ad software that you do not consent to personalised ads (GDPR consent false) and that your personal information must not be sold or shared, at once if the ad software is already running (an ad already loaded before your change can still be shown). Turning the switch on drops an earlier Decline of the game's own question, so the question comes again at your next launch where it is asked. Remove Ads takes away the ads between rooms, and the video rewards stay your choice.

From version 2.8.2, your answer to the game's own question and the Personalised ads switch count together, wherever you are. An Accept you give on screen turns the switch on, and a Decline turns it off; if the game could not read your earlier answer just before the question, an Accept leaves the switch as it was, and while the switch is off the ad software is not told that you consent (Accept again in Settings → Privacy & ads). Where the game asks its own question, a stored Accept counts only while the switch is on: if you turned the switch off elsewhere, the ad software is told that you do not consent when you are back, and the game does not bring up iOS's tracking question; Accept again in Settings → Privacy & ads to change that. As long as the game cannot read your stored answer or the switch, it treats them as a no: the ad software is not told that you consent, and the game does not bring up iOS's tracking question.

Report an ad: if an ad looks inappropriate or wrong for the game's age rating, tap Settings → Report an ad. It opens your mail app with a pre-written message to us; you decide what to send.

Many ads also show a privacy icon you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how each company uses this data and the choices you have, read its policy: Unity (Game Player and App User Privacy Policy) https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy; Liftoff https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact the company as described in its policy: we don't hold that data.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional. Payments are processed by Apple; the app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID. From version 2.6 the app asks Apple directly what you have bought (and brings it back on a new device or after a reinstall: Shop → Restore Purchases); no other company receives your purchases. Up to version 2.5 the app used RevenueCat for this, which received the App Store transaction records together with a random, anonymous app user id (RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

Remove Ads (from version 2.6 on sale again) takes away the ads between rooms and gives you the video rewards without an ad. If you bought it earlier, it stays yours, and Restore Purchases brings it back.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

From version 2.7 the game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics, and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier and not a device ID, and the app never gives it to Unity LevelPlay or any other ad company.
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, rooms and attempts, the first room's tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, each time the game asks its own ad question and the answer that stands after it (or that it was left unanswered), iOS's tracking answer when its question comes up, iOS's notification answer each time the game asks, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • Where the game asks its own question (the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and a device whose time zone names no place or cannot be read): your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game asks you, and while you are there it sends nothing until you choose "Allow". What happens in the game before you answer waits on your phone and is sent only if you choose "Allow"; "Don't allow" discards it.
  • In other countries (since version 2.8 also Serbia): our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings (in the bar at the bottom of the house screen), turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16 (15 in Serbia), turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

Data stored on your device

Besides your progress and settings, the game keeps on your device your answer to its ad question (where it asks one) and the date the question was last shown, the Personalised ads switch, a note of your answer to iOS's tracking question, and how many ads were shown today and when (so the limits above hold). The game statistics (above), if they are on, record the answers listed there; they keep their own random install number and their on/off choice on the device too.

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number or contacts. The game has no login; the only thing it sends to a server of ours is the game statistics described above, which you can turn off. We never sell data, and we do not combine the game's data with data from other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Vintagia is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. The game tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app, and ads are limited to content suitable for the game's age rating. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone deletes them.

Deleting your data

Your progress exists only on your device: deleting the app deletes it. The game statistics are deleted with Settings → "Delete my data" (see Game statistics above). Data held by Unity, Liftoff or RevenueCat is handled under their policies, linked above. For copies of progress stored by version 2.4 and earlier, see the next section.

Version 2.4 and earlier

If you have not updated yet, two things are different.

Copy of your progress. Versions up to 2.4 tried to keep a copy of your progress with a backend provider (Supabase), under an anonymous account: a random identifier that is not tied to your name, email, Apple ID or any other personal information. That backend is offline, so nothing is being sent to it or stored there now, and version 2.5 does not use it at all. Copies stored before it went offline hold only the game progress listed above, each under its random identifier, which we cannot link to you; if you would like them erased, write to us and we will delete the stored copies.

Advertising. Versions up to 2.4 were built with Google's advertising software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, "AdMob"). Since August 2026 it no longer delivers ads to the game, so their "watch an ad" buttons do nothing — but the software is still part of those versions and starts when the game opens. It may show Apple's question about tracking, in the EEA and the United Kingdom Google's consent form, and it contacts Google, which may receive your IP address, device and system information, diagnostic data and, only if you allowed tracking, your device's advertising identifier. Google handles that data under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. Updating to 2.5 removes Google's software from the game.
