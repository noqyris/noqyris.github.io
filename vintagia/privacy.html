Vintagia — Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026 · applies to Vintagia 2.4 and earlier

Vintagia: Cozy Hidden Objects is a single-player hidden-object game made by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). This policy explains what leaves your device, who receives it, why, and how you control it.

Your progress

Your progress and settings — energy, coins, hints, stars, finished rooms, collected curios, renovations and your daily-reward streak — are saved on your device, in the game's own storage. They are deleted when you delete the app.

This version also tries to keep a copy of that progress with our backend provider (Supabase), under an anonymous account: a random identifier that is not tied to your name, email, Apple ID or any other personal information. That backend is offline at the moment, so nothing is being sent to it or stored there now. Copies stored before it went offline hold only the game progress listed above, each under its random identifier.

Advertising

Vintagia does not show any ads at the moment. This version was built with Google's advertising software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, "AdMob"), and since August 2026 that software no longer delivers ads to the game, so its "watch an ad" buttons do nothing.

The software is still part of this version, and it starts when the game opens. It may then:

  • show Apple's question about whether Vintagia may track you (App Tracking Transparency);
  • in Europe and the United Kingdom, show Google's consent form;
  • contact Google, which may receive your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and system information, diagnostic data, and your device's advertising identifier — the last only if you allowed tracking.

Google receives that data directly, not through us, and handles it under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can refuse or withdraw tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

An update that removes Google's advertising software from the game is in preparation. This page will describe that version when it is released.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional. Payments are processed by Apple; the app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID. To confirm what you have bought, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives the App Store transaction records together with a random, anonymous app user id, and tells the game what you own. RevenueCat privacy policy

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number or contacts. The game has no login. We do not track you across apps or websites ourselves, and we never sell data.

Children

Vintagia is rated 4+ and is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children, and it tells the advertising software that it is not a child-directed app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe a child's information has been collected through this game, contact us below.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device. A copy of your progress kept with our backend before it went offline is held under a random identifier that we cannot link to you; if you would like it erased, write to us and we will delete the stored copies. Data held by Google or RevenueCat is handled under their policies, linked above.
