Privacy Policy

Vintagia · Last updated: 10 November 2026. Applies to Vintagia 2.5 and later (the game statistics: 2.7 and later; Liftoff: 2.8 and later); the last section covers version 2.4 and earlier.

Vintagia is a single-player hidden-object game by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). This policy explains what leaves your device, who receives it, why, and how you control it.

Your progress

Your progress and settings — energy, coins, hints, stars, finished rooms, collected curios, renovations and your daily-reward streak — are saved on your device, in the game's own storage, and nowhere else. The game has no account and no login, and it does not copy your progress to any server of ours (from version 2.7 it sends the game statistics described below, which are not your progress). Deleting the app deletes it. (It is part of the app's data, so it is included when iOS backs up or transfers your device.)

Advertising

Vintagia shows two kinds of ad, and the ad software starts only after the question described under Your choices. A short video you choose to watch for a stated reward — extra energy, or a hint — plays only when you tap the offer. From version 2.6, a short full-screen ad can also come between rooms: only when you leave a room you have finished, never during a room, never in your first rooms, at most every second room and a few times a day, and never if you have bought anything in the game. There are no banners.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange (both Unity Technologies) and, from version 2.8, from Liftoff (Liftoff Monetize), which bids in the same auction for each ad. The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to these companies, not to us, and each of them handles that data under its own privacy policy (linked below).

What these ad companies may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data about how ads load and display.

What it is used for:

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads, only with your tracking permission for the advertising identifier, in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland only with your consent, and never while the Personalised ads switch is off.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity, and keeping the ad software working.

Your choices

When you open the game for the first time, a welcome screen links the Terms of Use and this policy. Then the game asks its questions, one after the other: about ads (which question depends on where your device is, read from its time zone, as described below) and, through iOS, whether it may send you reminders (when your energy is full or your daily gift is ready). The ad software does not start before the ad question has an answer. If you leave that question unanswered, it comes again after a room you finish, or when you tap an offer to watch an ad for a reward. If you own Remove Ads, the game asks nothing about ads.

In the European Economic Area (the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, where the law asks for your consent before ad software uses your data to personalise ads, the game asks its own question first. It says who shows the ads (Vintagia, by Noqyris, through Unity LevelPlay), what the ad networks — Unity Technologies (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange) and Liftoff — use and why, and what declining means. Its two answers, Accept and Decline, look the same. If you accepted before version 2.8, when the question named Unity Technologies only, the game asks once more, after the next room you finish, and starts no ads until you answer.

  • Accept: iOS then asks whether Vintagia may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency), and then the ad software starts and ads may be personalised. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown, but your advertising identifier is not shared, so ads cannot be personalised with it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Decline: the ad software starts and shows ads, but it is told that you did not consent (GDPR consent false) and not to sell or share your personal information, so the ads are not personalised. iOS's tracking question is not shown. (Until version 2.8, declining meant no ads at all; if you declined then, the game tells you once that ads are now shown, without tracking.)

To change your answer, open Settings (in the bar at the bottom of the house screen) → Privacy & ads at any time; the change reaches the ad software at once. Your answer is not sent to us, except as part of the game statistics (below), if they are on.

Everywhere else (since version 2.8 also Serbia and the rest of the Western Balkans), the game asks no question of its own; the only question is iOS's own (App Tracking Transparency): whether Vintagia may use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. The ad software starts after you answer, whichever answer you give. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared and the game tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information, so ads are not personalised with it. You can change your answer at any time in the iOS Settings app (Vintagia → Allow Tracking). In the game, Settings → Privacy & ads explains this, opens that page and has a Personalised ads switch: turned off, the ad software is told that you do not consent to personalised ads (GDPR consent false) and not to sell or share your personal information, so the ads you see are not personalised. Remove Ads takes away the ads between rooms, and the video rewards stay your choice.

Report an ad: if an ad looks inappropriate or wrong for the game's age rating, tap Settings → Report an ad. It opens your mail app with a pre-written message to us; you decide what to send.

Many ads also show a privacy icon you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how each company uses this data and the choices you have, read its policy: Unity (Game Player and App User Privacy Policy) https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy; Liftoff https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact the company as described in its policy: we don't hold that data.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional. Payments are processed by Apple; the app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID. From version 2.6 the app asks Apple directly what you have bought (and brings it back on a new device or after a reinstall: Shop → Restore Purchases); no other company receives your purchases. Up to version 2.5 the app used RevenueCat for this, which received the App Store transaction records together with a random, anonymous app user id (RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

Remove Ads (from version 2.6 on sale again) takes away the ads between rooms and gives you the video rewards without an ad. If you bought it earlier, it stays yours, and Restore Purchases brings it back.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

From version 2.7 the game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; they are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier, not a device ID, and it is never given to anyone, including our ad network (Unity LevelPlay).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, rooms and attempts, the first room's tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, your answers to the questions about ads, tracking and notifications, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game asks you before anything is sent and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow".
  • In other countries (since version 2.8 also Serbia): our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings (in the bar at the bottom of the house screen), turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16 (15 in Serbia), turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

Data stored on your device

Besides your progress and settings, the game keeps on your device your answer to its ad question (in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) and the date the question was last shown, the Personalised ads switch (elsewhere), a note of your answer to iOS's tracking question, and how many ads were shown today and when (so the limits above hold). None of it is sent to us, except your answers as part of the game statistics (above), if they are on; the game statistics keep their own random install number and their on/off choice on the device too.

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number or contacts. The game has no login; the only thing it sends to a server of ours is the game statistics described above, which you can turn off. We do not track you across apps or websites ourselves, and we never sell data.

Children

Vintagia is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. The game tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app, and ads are limited to content suitable for the game's age rating. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone deletes them.

Deleting your data

Your progress exists only on your device: deleting the app deletes it. The game statistics are deleted with Settings → "Delete my data" (see Game statistics above). Data held by Unity, Liftoff or RevenueCat is handled under their policies, linked above. For copies of progress stored by version 2.4 and earlier, see the next section.

Version 2.4 and earlier

If you have not updated yet, two things are different.

Copy of your progress. Versions up to 2.4 tried to keep a copy of your progress with a backend provider (Supabase), under an anonymous account: a random identifier that is not tied to your name, email, Apple ID or any other personal information. That backend is offline, so nothing is being sent to it or stored there now, and version 2.5 does not use it at all. Copies stored before it went offline hold only the game progress listed above, each under its random identifier, which we cannot link to you; if you would like them erased, write to us and we will delete the stored copies.

Advertising. Versions up to 2.4 were built with Google's advertising software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, "AdMob"). Since August 2026 it no longer delivers ads to the game, so their "watch an ad" buttons do nothing — but the software is still part of those versions and starts when the game opens. It may show Apple's question about tracking, in Europe and the United Kingdom Google's consent form, and it contacts Google, which may receive your IP address, device and system information, diagnostic data and, only if you allowed tracking, your device's advertising identifier. Google handles that data under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. Updating to 2.5 removes Google's software from the game.
