Privacy Policy

Don't Wake Otto · Last updated: 28 November 2026. Applies to version 1.0 and later.

Don't Wake Otto is a single-player game by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). This policy explains what leaves your device, who receives it, why, and how you control it.

Your progress

Your best score and your two switches (sound and vibration) are saved on your device, in the game's own storage, and nowhere else. The game has no account and no login. What it sends us about how the game is played is described under Game statistics (Noqyris). Deleting the app deletes your progress. (It is part of the app's data, so it is included when iOS backs up or transfers your device.)

Advertising

Don't Wake Otto shows three kinds of ad, and the ad software starts only after the questions described under Your choices are done. A short video you choose to watch plays only when you tap "Watch an ad · Catch him" after a fall: it repairs the plank and puts Otto back on his feet, once in a run. A short full-screen ad can also come between two runs, never during one: not in the first 3 runs after you install the game, then at most after every 3rd run, at least 75 seconds after the last full-screen ad, at most 12 a day, and never if you own Remove Ads. A one-second notice comes before each. A small banner can sit at the bottom of the screen after a fall, never on the title screen, never during a run and never if you own Remove Ads.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from Unity Ads and the ironSource Exchange (both Unity Technologies). The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data about how ads load and display.

What it is used for:

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, only with your consent to the game's question and the "Personalised ads" switch on; elsewhere, only with the switch on. In both, iOS's tracking question decides whether the ad software receives your advertising identifier.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity, and keeping the ad software working.

Your choices

The first time you open the game it shows a welcome with the Terms of Use and this policy, and then asks its questions, before the first run. Which questions you see depends on where your device is, read from its time zone. Once you have answered, nothing is asked again unless you ask for it in Settings.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, where the law asks for your consent before ad software reads your device, the game asks its own question first. It says who shows the ads (Don't Wake Otto, by Noqyris, through Unity LevelPlay), what Unity Technologies (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange) receives and why, and what declining means. Its two answers, Accept and Decline, look the same.

  • Accept: iOS then asks whether Don't Wake Otto may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency), unless it has asked before, and the ad software starts with your consent. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead. You can change that in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Decline: the ad software starts without your consent: it is told you declined, ads are not tailored to you, and iOS's tracking question is not shown. You still see ads, and "Catch him" is still offered.

To change your answer, open Settings (the sliders at the top right of the title screen) → Privacy and ads → Change my answer, at any time; the new answer also sets the "Personalised ads" switch (below). Your answer is kept on your device.

Everywhere else, the only question is iOS's own (App Tracking Transparency), with no screen of ours in front of it: whether Don't Wake Otto may use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Ads are shown whichever answer you give. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, iOS gives the app a blank advertising identifier. You can change your answer at any time in the iOS Settings app (Don't Wake Otto → Allow Tracking).

Personalised ads, everywhere: Settings → Privacy and ads has a "Personalised ads" switch. Turned off, the game tells the ad service that you do not consent to personalised ads; you still see ads, but they are not tailored to you. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland your answer to the game's question also sets the switch: Accept turns it on, Decline turns it off, and it then stays off in other countries too until you turn it on yourself. In that area, turning it on after Decline asks the question again.

Many Unity ads also show a privacy icon you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the choices you have, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in that policy: we don't hold that data.

In-app purchase

The one purchase, Remove Ads, is optional. Payment is processed by Apple; the app never sees your payment details, name or Apple Account, and it asks Apple directly whether you own it. Remove Ads is yours for good and comes back on a new device or after a reinstall (Settings → Restore purchase).

Game statistics (Noqyris)

The game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. These statistics are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier, not a device ID, and it is never given to anyone, including our ad network (Unity LevelPlay).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, levels and attempts, tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it; we keep only the country derived from it, and whether it belongs to Google's or Apple's own network (to leave out store review devices).

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the European Economic Area (the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway), the United Kingdom and Switzerland: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game asks you before anything is sent and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow".
  • In other countries, Serbia included: our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA). Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • today's and the previous 7 days' totals are recalculated without it, usually within a day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

In Don't Wake Otto both are in Settings (the sliders at the top right of the title screen) → Privacy and ads.

If you are under 16, turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

Data stored on your device

Besides your best score and your two switches, the game keeps on your device: that you accepted the welcome, your answer to its ad question (in the area above), your "Personalised ads" switch, how many runs and ad breaks there were today and when the last full-screen ad was shown (so the limits above hold), and that you own Remove Ads. None of it is sent to us. The game statistics keep their own state there too (the random install number, your answer about statistics, a count of your runs and the events not sent yet), as described under Game statistics.

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number or contacts. The game has no login, and the only thing it sends to a server of ours is the game statistics described above, under a random install number. We do not track you across apps or websites ourselves, and we never sell data.

Children

Don't Wake Otto is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone starts the deletion described under Game statistics.

Deleting your data

What the game keeps on your device is deleted with the app. The game statistics it has already sent are deleted with the "Delete my data" button, as described under Game statistics; deleting the app alone does not reach our server, and what was sent is then deleted automatically after 3 months. Data held by Unity is handled under its policy, linked above.
