Don't Wake Otto

Support · a one-tap game by Noqyris

Otto walks in his sleep along a row of planks high over the city, and he never stops. Every plank ahead of him spins. One tap freezes the next one. Questions or problems: email support@noqyris.com and say which iPhone and iOS version you have.

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to freeze the NEXT spinning plank, the glowing one. Freeze it flat and it holds; freeze it crooked and it drops away, and Otto walks into the hole. You can freeze planks ahead of him, as far as the screen shows. A plank frozen almost exactly flat is a PERFECT and scores more, and a row of perfects quickens his step.

What is "Catch him"?

A second chance, once in a run, on a run that got somewhere (10 points or more, or close to your best). You watch a short video, the plank is repaired and Otto is put back on his feet; nothing moves again until you tap. If you own Remove Ads, "Catch him" is free and plays no video.

I bought Remove Ads and still see ads, or it is missing

Open Settings (the sliders at the top right of the title screen) and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the Apple Account you bought with. Remove Ads takes away the banner and the ad breaks between runs and makes "Catch him" free. For refunds, Apple handles purchases: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy

Ads never interrupt a run: a break can come only between two runs, a video only when you tap "Catch him", and the small banner at the bottom shows only on the screen after a fall, never on the title screen and never during a run. In Settings → Privacy and ads you can turn "Personalised ads" off at any time (you still see ads, but they are not tailored to you), and in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland change your answer to the ads question. The same page has "Game statistics" (the studio's own statistics about how the game is played: On or Off) and "Delete my data". Full details: Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Sound and vibration

Both have a switch in Settings. The game's sounds follow the iPhone's volume.

Does my best score sync between devices?

No. It is saved on your device only (there is no account). It is included when iOS backs up or transfers your device.
