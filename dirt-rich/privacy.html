Privacy Policy

Dirt Rich: Metal Detecting · Last updated: 9 November 2026. Applies to version 1.0 and later.

Dirt Rich is a single-player metal-detecting game by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). This policy explains what leaves your device, who receives it, why, and how you control it.

Your progress

Your progress and settings — your finds, the journal and museum, your coins and kit, the hunt, the battery, Grandad's tin and the field notes — are saved on your device, in the game's own storage, and nowhere else. The game has no account and no login, and it does not copy your progress to any server of ours. Deleting the app deletes it. (Separately, the game sends us statistics about how it is played, under a random number and never with your progress; see Game statistics (Noqyris) below.) (It is part of the app's data, so it is included when iOS backs up or transfers your device.)

Advertising

Dirt Rich shows two kinds of ad, and the ad software starts only after the question described under Your choices. A short video you choose to watch for a stated reward — more battery bars, doubling the day's takings, a second opinion on a reading, doubling Grandad's tin, or one more chance in an endless run — plays only when you tap the offer, and each offer says what you get before you tap it. A short full-screen ad can also come between games: only when you start a new outing after a finished day (and only if the battery has charge for it), or when you start another endless run after one has ended — never during a dig or a run, never on the day you install the game, never before the second step of Grandad's hunt, at least four minutes apart, at most three a day, and never if you have bought anything in the game. There are no banners.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from Unity Ads and the ironSource Exchange (both Unity Technologies). The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data about how ads load and display.

What it is used for:

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads, only with your tracking permission for the advertising identifier and, in Europe, your consent.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity, and keeping the ad software working.

Your choices

The ad software does not start when the game opens, and the game asks nothing when it opens. The first question comes when you start your first new outing after a finished day, or when you tap an offer to watch an ad for a reward. Which question you see depends on where your device is, read from its time zone.

In Europe (the EU and EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Serbia), where the law asks for your consent before ad software reads your device, the game asks its own question first. It says who shows the ads (Dirt Rich, by Noqyris, through Unity LevelPlay), what Unity Technologies (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange) receives and why, and what declining means. Its two answers, Accept and Decline, look the same.

  • Accept: iOS then asks whether Dirt Rich may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency), and only then does the ad software start. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown, but your advertising identifier is not shared, so ads cannot be personalised with it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Decline: the ad software is not started at all and collects nothing. No ads are shown, there are no rewards for watching ads, and iOS's tracking question is not shown. The battery still charges over time and everything in the game can be earned by playing. The game asks again only if you tap an ad offer at least 180 days later.

To change your answer, open ☰ at the top left of the county → Settings → Privacy & ads at any time. If you withdraw consent after ads have started, the game stops loading and showing ads and tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information. Your answer is not sent to us.

Everywhere else, the only question is iOS's own (App Tracking Transparency): whether Dirt Rich may use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. The ad software starts after you answer, whichever answer you give. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared and the game tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information, so ads are not personalised with it. You can change your answer at any time in the iOS Settings app (Dirt Rich → Allow Tracking); Settings → Privacy & ads in the game explains this and opens that page.

Report an ad: if an ad looks inappropriate or wrong for the game's age rating, tap Settings → Report an ad. It opens your mail app with a pre-written message to support@noqyris.com; you decide what to send.

Many Unity ads also show a privacy icon you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the choices you have, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in that policy: we don't hold that data.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional. Payments are processed by Apple; the app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and it asks Apple directly what you have bought — no other company receives your purchases. No Ad Breaks, Grandad's Field Kit, the Big Battery and the Detectorist's Club are yours for good and come back on a new device or after a reinstall (Shop → Restore Purchases, or Settings → Restore purchases). The Flask of Tea is used up the moment it is bought. Coins cannot be bought: they are earned by playing.

Sharing your finds

"Share" on the day's end draws a picture of the day's finds on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet, with a link to the game on the App Store. You choose where it goes; nothing is sent to us.

Reminders

If you say yes when the game asks (and then to iOS), it schedules at most one local reminder a day on your device — when your battery is charged or Grandad's tin is ready — never at night. Reminders are made on the phone itself: nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Turn them off in Settings → Reminders or in the iOS Settings app.

Data stored on your device

Besides your progress and settings, the game keeps on your device your answer to its ad question (in Europe) and the date the question was last shown (so a refusal is not asked again for 180 days), a note of your answer to iOS's tracking question, how many ads were shown today and when (so the limits above hold), and a record of purchases already credited (so nothing is paid twice). None of it is sent to us. The game statistics described below also keep a few things on your device: the random install number, your answer about statistics and events waiting to be sent.

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number or contacts. The game has no login. The only thing it sends to a server of ours is the game statistics described below, under a random install number — never your name, your progress, your purchases or the advertising identifier. We do not track you across apps or websites ourselves, and we never sell data.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

The game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; they are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier, not a device ID, and it is never given to anyone, including our ad network (Unity LevelPlay).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, levels and attempts, tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland and Serbia: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), Serbian Personal Data Protection Act Art. 12, Electronic Communications Act Art. 160). The game asks you before anything is sent and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow".
  • In other countries: our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16 (15 in Serbia), turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

In Europe the game asks about statistics with its own two-button question (Allow / Don't allow), separate from the ads question and after it, on the way into a new outing; nothing in the game depends on the answer. Settings (☰ at the top left of the county → Settings → Game statistics, and Delete my data) are there in every country.

Children

Dirt Rich is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children, and it tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app. Ads are limited to content suitable for the game's age rating. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe a child's information has been collected through the ads in this game, contact us below.

Dirt Rich is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone deletes them.

Deleting your data

Your progress exists only on your device: deleting the app deletes it. Game statistics are deleted with Settings → Delete my data in the game (see Game statistics (Noqyris)): deleting the app alone does not reach our server. Data held by Unity is handled under its policy, linked above.
