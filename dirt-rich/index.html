Dirt Rich: Metal Detecting

Support · a cozy metal-detecting game by Noqyris

You inherit your grandfather's detector and the field book he left behind. Walk the county, listen to the machine, decide what is worth a hole, and follow his notes to the end of the story. Questions or problems: email subotic.djo@gmail.com — say which iPhone and iOS version you have.

How do I play?

Drag your thumb on the ground to walk. The detector sings louder as you get closer to something buried; when the ring appears on the ground, tap DIG, then tap again when the pointer is in the gold for a clean plug. The reading names every metal the signal could be — "FOIL or GOLD" really means either. The gold sign at the top of the screen points to the place Grandad's current clue names; tap it to read the clue, and open ☰ (top left) for the whole list.

The battery ran out. What now?

The battery charges by itself: one bar every five minutes, and a new day starts full. The time to the next bar is inside the battery at the top right; tap the battery to see when it will be full. You can keep walking and listening while it charges. Grandad's tin and the field notes can add bars, and so can a short video you choose to watch, or the Flask of Tea in the shop.

What are Grandad's tin and the field notes?

The tin is a small gift once a day, a little better each day in a row; missing a day only pauses it. The field notes are three small jobs a day (☰ → Today's notes; a red dot on ☰ means one is ready), each paying coins, with a chest when all three are done.

Can it remind me when the battery is full?

Yes: ☰ → Settings → Reminders. At most one reminder a day, never at night, made on your phone — nothing is sent anywhere.

I bought something and it's missing

Open the Shop (tap the coins at the top) or ☰ → Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. No Ad Breaks, Grandad's Field Kit, the Big Battery and the Detectorist's Club come back. The Flask of Tea is used up when it is bought. For refunds, Apple handles purchases: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy

Ads never interrupt a dig. You can change your ad choices at any time in ☰ → Settings → Privacy & ads, and report an ad with Settings → Report an ad. Full details: Privacy Policy.

Does my progress sync between devices?

No. Progress is saved on your device only (there is no account). It is included when iOS backs up or transfers your device.

Accessibility

Settings has Bigger text, Reduce motion, and Skip plug timing (dig straight away with no dial — same finds, same pay). Rarity is always shown with a word and a shape as well as a colour.
