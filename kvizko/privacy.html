Politika privatnosti — KVIZKO

Poslednja izmena: 27. septembar 2026. (verzija 1.3.0)

English version below

KVIZKO je kviz igra koju razvija Noqyris. Ova politika objašnjava koje se informacije obrađuju kada koristiš aplikaciju, ko ih obrađuje, koliko dugo se čuvaju i kako ih brišeš.

  • Kviz, „Preživi“, dnevni izazov, zadaci, niz, statistika, žetoni i kupovine rade na tvom uređaju. Ti podaci ostaju na uređaju.
  • Samo Duel (igra uživo protiv drugog igrača) šalje podatke našem serveru. Šalje anonimni ID igrača, nadimak koji izabereš i tok tvojih duela. Ne šalje ime, e-mail ni broj telefona.
  • Reklame prikazuje Unity LevelPlay, i to samo ako na njih pristaneš.

1. Šta ostaje samo na tvom uređaju

Na uređaju se čuvaju rezultati, statistika, napredak, nivoi, medalje, niz, zadaci, žetoni, kasica, podešavanja i kupovine. Podsetnici se zakazuju lokalno na telefonu i ne prolaze kroz naš server. Mi ove podatke ne vidimo. Ako obrišeš aplikaciju, oni nestaju s njom.

2. Duel — šta se šalje našem serveru

Duel radi preko servera koji za nas vodi Supabase (Supabase, Inc.). Server je u centru podataka u Frankfurtu, u Nemačkoj (EU). Duel koristi internet, a ostatak igre radi i bez njega. Kad otvoriš Duel, obrađuje se sledeće:

  • Anonimni nalog. Server pravi nasumični identifikator igrača. Ne tražimo e-mail, telefon ni lozinku, i nema prijave. Ključ sesije čuva se na uređaju, da bi te server prepoznao sledeći put.
  • Nadimak. Biraš ga jednom i posle ne može da se menja. Dok ga ne izabereš, server ti dodeljuje privremeni nadimak oblika „Igrač 1234“. Nadimak koji izabereš prolazi kroz filter neprimerenih reči i mora biti jedinstven.
  • Tok duela. Čuva se sledeće:
    • koja su pitanja bila u meču (iz kataloga koji je već u aplikaciji);
    • tvoj odgovor na svako pitanje ili „Ne znam“, i da li je tačan;
    • vreme odgovora koje meri server;
    • bodovi, ishod meča, predaja ili prekid veze;
    • zahtevi za revanš i koliko si reakcija poslao/la;
    • tvoj ukupan broj „mozgića“ i zapis o svakoj dodeli.
  • Tehnički podaci. Aplikacija šalje verziju kataloga pitanja i verziju protokola duela. Kao i kod svakog internet zahteva, server vidi IP adresu i oznaku pregledača (user agent) tvog uređaja. Supabase čuva IP adresu i user agent uz sesiju anonimnog naloga. Vodi i tehničke zapise o zahtevima radi rada i bezbednosti servisa; nama su ti zapisi dostupni samo za poslednji dan.
  • Prijave nadimka. Ako prijaviš nečiji nadimak (sa rang liste ili posle meča), čuvamo ko je prijavio, koji nadimak i kada. Isto važi ako neko prijavi tebe.
  • Blokiranja. Ako blokiraš igrača, čuvamo ko je koga blokirao (samo dva nasumična ID-ja profila) i kada. Vas dvoje posle toga više ne igrate jedan protiv drugog, a taj nadimak nestaje sa tvoje rang liste. Isto važi ako neko blokira tebe.

Duel ne šalje ime, e-mail, broj telefona, kontakte ni lokaciju. Ne šalje ni reklamni identifikator, podatke o kupovinama ni stanje žetona. Ulaz u duel naplaćuje se na tvom uređaju. Šifru sobe za igru sa prijateljem deliš sam/a, preko sistema za deljenje na telefonu.

Šta vide drugi igrači

Drugi igrači vide tvoj nadimak, broj mozgića i mesto na rang listi (prvih 100). Tokom meča protivnik vidi i tvoje bodove, da li si odgovorio/la tačno, tvoje vreme odgovora, da li si povezan/a i reakcije koje pošalješ. Duel nema čet, nego samo pet unapred zadatih emodžija.

Zašto

Ove podatke koristimo za sledeće:

  • da te spojimo sa protivnikom;
  • da meč bude pošten: server odlučuje ko je prvi tačno odgovorio;
  • da vodimo rang listu;
  • da sprečimo zloupotrebe.

Zaštita od zloupotreba obuhvata sledeće:

  • ograničenje broja zahteva;
  • ograničenje neuspelih pokušaja ulaska u sobu, za šta kratko čuvamo IP adresu;
  • kratku pauzu traženja protivnika posle više napuštenih mečeva;
  • automatsku proveru neuobičajeno brzih odgovora. Takvi profili igraju samo međusobno i drugi igrači ih ne vide na rang listi.

Pravni osnov je pružanje usluge koju tražiš kad otvoriš Duel, i naš legitimni interes da igra bude poštena i bezbedna. Podatke iz Duela ne koristimo za reklame i ne prodajemo ih.

Koliko dugo

  • Mečevi i odgovori u njima čuvaju se 30 dana od kraja meča.
  • Profil (nadimak, mozgići) briše se kad 180 dana ne otvoriš Duel.
  • Zapisi o dodeli mozgića čuvaju se 180 dana.
  • Prijave nadimka čuvaju se 180 dana, ili kraće ako se jedan od dva profila obriše ranije.
  • Blokiranje se čuva dok ga ne poništiš (u Duelu: „⋯“ → „Blokirani igrači“) ili dok se jedan od dva profila ne obriše.
  • IP adrese neuspelih pokušaja ulaska u sobu brišu se u dnevnom čišćenju, najkasnije za oko dva dana.
  • Anonimni nalog i podaci njegove sesije (IP adresa, user agent) brišu se kad obrišeš profil za duel, a inače 180 dana posle poslednjeg otvaranja Duela, u prvom sledećem dnevnom čišćenju.

Brisanje

Profil za duel brišeš u aplikaciji: u Duelu otvori „⋯“ (gore desno), izaberi „Obriši moj profil za duel“ i potvrdi. Brisanje nije moguće dok je meč u toku i ne može se opozvati. Odmah se briše sledeće:

  • tvoj nadimak: oslobađa se (drugi igrač ga može uzeti), a umesto njega piše „Obrisan igrač“;
  • mozgići, zapisi o svakoj dodeli i mesto na rang listi;
  • istorija duela, odnosno svi završeni mečevi sa odgovorima u njima. Meč završen u poslednjih 5 minuta briše se u prvom sledećem dnevnom čišćenju, jer protivnik možda još gleda rezultat;
  • prijave koje su drugi podneli protiv tvog nadimka;
  • sva blokiranja: ona koja si ti postavio/la i ona koja su drugi postavili protiv tebe;
  • anonimni nalog, zajedno sa IP adresom i user agentom sesije;
  • na uređaju: ključ sesije, sačuvani nadimak i lokalna istorija duela.

Prazan zapis profila (samo nasumični ID i oznaka da je obrisan) ostaje još jedan dan. Tako stari ključ sesije ne može da vrati profil. Posle toga ga briše dnevno čišćenje, a s njim i prijave koje si ti podneo/la. Žetoni i napredak u ostalim igrama ostaju na uređaju.

Brisanje možeš tražiti i e-mailom. U poruci navedi svoj nadimak.

Privacy Policy — KVIZKO

Last updated: 27 September 2026 (version 1.3.0)

Srpska verzija gore

KVIZKO is a quiz game developed by Noqyris. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app, who processes it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

  • The quiz, "Survive", the daily challenge, quests, the streak, statistics, žetoni (coins) and purchases work on your device. That data stays on the device.
  • Only Duel (a live match against another player) sends data to our server. It sends an anonymous player ID, the nickname you choose and the course of your duels. It never sends a name, e-mail or phone number.
  • Ads are shown by Unity LevelPlay, and only if you consent to them.

1. What stays on your device only

Scores, statistics, progress, levels, medals, the streak, quests, žetoni, the piggy bank, settings and purchases are stored only on your device. Reminders are scheduled locally on the phone and never pass through our server. We cannot see this data. If you delete the app, it goes with it.

2. Duel: what is sent to our server

Duel runs on a server operated for us by Supabase (Supabase, Inc.). The server is in a data centre in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Duel needs an internet connection; the rest of the game works without one. When you open Duel, the following is processed:

  • Anonymous account. The server creates a random player identifier. We do not ask for an e-mail, phone number or password, and there is no login. A session key is stored on your device so the server recognises you next time.
  • Nickname. You choose it once, and it cannot be changed afterwards. Until you choose one, the server gives you a temporary nickname such as "Igrač 1234". The nickname you choose passes a filter for inappropriate words and must be unique.
  • The course of each duel. The server keeps:
    • which questions were played (from the catalogue already inside the app);
    • your answer to each question, or "I don't know", and whether it was correct;
    • the response time, measured by the server;
    • points, the result, a forfeit or a lost connection;
    • rematch requests and how many reactions you sent;
    • your total of "mozgići" (brain points) and a record of each award.
  • Technical data. The app sends the version of its question catalogue and of the duel protocol. As with any internet request, the server sees your device's IP address and browser identifier (user agent). Supabase stores the IP address and user agent with the anonymous account's session. It also keeps technical request logs to run and secure the service; we can see those logs for the last day only.
  • Nickname reports. If you report someone's nickname (from the ranking or after a match), we keep who reported, which nickname and when. The same applies if someone reports you.
  • Blocks. If you block a player, we keep who blocked whom (the two random profile IDs only) and when. From then on the two of you never play each other, and that nickname disappears from your ranking. The same applies if someone blocks you.

Duel does not send your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts or location. It does not send your advertising identifier, purchase data or žeton balance either: the entry fee is charged on your device. You share a friend-room code yourself, through the phone's share sheet.

What other players see

Other players see your nickname, your mozgići total and your place on the ranking (top 100). During a match your opponent also sees your points, whether you answered correctly, your response times, whether you are connected and the reactions you send. There is no chat in Duel, only five preset emoji.

Why

We use this data:

  • to pair you with an opponent;
  • to keep the match fair: the server decides who answered correctly first;
  • to keep the ranking;
  • to prevent abuse.

Abuse prevention covers:

  • request limits;
  • a limit on failed attempts to join a room, for which we keep the IP address briefly;
  • a short matchmaking pause after several abandoned matches;
  • an automatic check for unusually fast answers. Profiles it flags only meet each other and do not appear on the ranking other players see.

The legal basis is providing the service you ask for when you open Duel, and our legitimate interest in a fair and secure game. Duel data is never used for advertising and never sold.

How long

  • Matches and the answers in them: 30 days after the match ends.
  • Your profile (nickname, mozgići): deleted after 180 days in which you do not open Duel.
  • Mozgići award records: 180 days.
  • Nickname reports: 180 days, or less if either of the two profiles is deleted earlier.
  • Blocks: until you lift the block (in Duel: "⋯" → "Blokirani igrači", blocked players) or either of the two profiles is deleted.
  • IP addresses of failed room-join attempts: removed by the daily clean-up, within about two days.
  • The anonymous account and its session data (IP address, user agent): deleted when you delete your duel profile, and otherwise 180 days after you last opened Duel, in the next daily clean-up.

Deletion

You delete your duel profile in the app: in Duel, open "⋯" (top right), choose "Obriši moj profil za duel" (delete my duel profile) and confirm. Deletion is not possible while a match is in progress, and it cannot be undone. The following is deleted at once:

  • your nickname: it is released (another player can take it), and "Obrisan igrač" (deleted player) is shown in its place;
  • your mozgići, every award record and your place on the ranking;
  • your duel history, that is every finished match with the answers in it. A match that ended in the last 5 minutes goes in the next daily clean-up instead, because your opponent may still be looking at the result;
  • reports other players filed against your nickname;
  • every block: the ones you placed and the ones other players placed on you;
  • the anonymous account, together with the session's IP address and user agent;
  • on your device: the session key, the saved nickname and the local duel history.

An empty profile record (only the random ID and a "deleted" mark) stays for one more day, so that an old session key cannot bring the profile back. The daily clean-up then removes it, together with any reports you filed. Your žetoni and your progress in the rest of the game stay on your device.

You can also ask for deletion by e-mail. Include your nickname.

3. Third-party services

The app uses these services:

  • Supabase is the server for Duel (section 2). It processes data on our behalf, only to run Duel. Supabase privacy policy
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) shows ads and mediates between several ad networks. It collects technical data about the device and the ad shown ("Services Data"): the advertising identifier, device type, OS version, approximate location (country), ad impression and interaction data, and diagnostic data about the app. Unity processes it as an independent controller: to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its services. Unity privacy policy
  • Apple Game Center (iOS) keeps your score on the leaderboards if you are signed in. Leaderboards are optional, and the game works normally without signing in.
  • Google Play Games (Android) does the same on Android: if you are signed in, it keeps your score on the leaderboards and the achievements you earn.
  • Apple App Store (iOS) and Google Play billing (Android) process in-app purchases. We never see or store your payment details.

4. Ads, tracking and your choice

On first launch on iOS, Apple's "App Tracking Transparency" prompt appears. If you do not allow tracking, ads are requested as non-personalised, and the game works exactly the same. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Before the first ad, on both platforms, a consent prompt run by Unity LevelPlay appears. If you do not consent, the ad system never starts. The game then works the same, but without ads and without the bonuses earned by watching one. You can change your decision in the app's settings, on the "Privacy policy" row.

No ad of any kind is shown during a duel. A between-games ad can appear only after it.

The "No ads" purchase permanently removes the banner and the between-games ads. The "Welcome pack" includes the same. After buying either, only optional videos that you start yourself remain: "Double winnings", "Continue", "Restore a lifeline", a bonus hint, a question explanation, free žetoni and "Repair streak". They play only when you ask, and Unity LevelPlay then processes data as for any ad shown.

5. Reporting a wrong question

"Report a mistake in this question" opens your own mail app with a pre-written message. The message contains the question, its answer, the question's id and the app version. Nothing is sent until you send the e-mail yourself. We then see your e-mail address and what you wrote, and use them only to fix the question and reply to you.

6. Children

KVIZKO is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. The app contains ads, as shown in its App Store rating. Duel has no chat. Other players see only a nickname, which is filtered. Any nickname can be reported and any player blocked.

7. Your rights

You can ask for access to the Duel data about you. You can also ask for it to be corrected (for example, a nickname change, which we make on request), deleted or restricted, and you can object to its processing. Write to the address below and include your nickname: there is no other detail by which we could recognise you. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Serbian Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection, or with the supervisory authority of your own country.
