Magnet Pop: Relaxing Puzzle — Support

Magnet Pop is a relaxing physics puzzle for iPhone and iPad by Noqyris. Drop a magnet and it pulls in every ball within its reach; when four or more balls of the same colour touch, they pop. Meet each level's goal before your magnets run out. 300 levels, a new Today's Calm board every day, and Zen when you just want to play.

Contact

Questions, problems and ideas: support@noqyris.com. Please say which iPhone or iPad you use and the version shown at the bottom of Settings (the gear on the main menu).

Common questions

  • My purchase is missing. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the Apple Account that bought it. The same button is at the bottom of the Shop. No Ads and the Calm Bundle come back. The Welcome Pack and the power packs are used up as you spend them and are not restored.
  • How do I change my answer about ads? Settings, then Privacy & ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the question is asked again and your new answer applies from then on. Everywhere else it has a Personalised ads switch; your answer to Apple's tracking question is in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Ads are shown either way; they are tailored to you only if you allow it.
  • How do I turn off the game statistics? Settings, then Game statistics. Delete my data, just under it, also asks us to delete what was sent. Both are there in every country.
  • I saw an ad that should not be there. Settings, then Report an ad, or write to the address above and say what the ad showed and roughly when.
  • How do I stop the reminders? Settings, then Reminders, or the iOS Settings app, then Notifications. They are off until you say yes, and there is at most one a day.
  • Why did I only get two stars? A board finished with a power tops out at two stars. Finish it with magnets alone for three.
  • I am stuck on a level. After five tries in a row a free Skip level button appears.
  • When does Today's Calm change? At midnight where you are; everyone gets the same board on the same date. Only your first clear of the day goes to Game Center, and a clear that used a power posts no score.
  • Where are the leaderboards? Settings, then Leaderboards (Game Center).
  • Do I need an internet connection? No. Every level, Today's Calm and Zen play without one. Ads, the Shop, Game Center and sending the game statistics need a connection.
  • Is my progress backed up? Progress is kept on your device and is removed if you delete the game. No Ads and the Calm Bundle come back with Restore purchases.
