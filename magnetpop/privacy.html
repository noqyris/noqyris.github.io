Privacy Policy

Magnet Pop · Last updated: November 25, 2026 · Applies to Magnet Pop 1.4 and later; Version 1.3 and earlier says what is different before 1.4 · Terms of Use

Magnet Pop ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). There are no Magnet Pop accounts. The game sends us statistics about how it is played, without your name, so we can improve it (see Game statistics); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland only if you allow it (from the game's first start there), and everywhere you can turn it off in the game. The app also shows ads, and the ad service it uses collects some data from your device. If you use Apple's Game Center, your scores go to Apple's leaderboards. This policy explains what, why, and how you control it.

Advertising

The app shows a banner ad on the menu screens (never while a board is in play), an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and short videos you can choose to watch for a reward, such as extra magnets, more powers, or skipping a level you are stuck on. Levels 1 to 5 have no banner and no full-screen ads. With No Ads or the Calm Bundle no banner or full-screen ad is shown and the bonuses need no video; wherever this policy says that ads are shown, it means players without them.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies (LevelPlay was formerly part of ironSource, now owned by Unity), with ads from Unity's own ad networks, Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange. The ad software is built into the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, when iOS asks or later in iOS Settings. If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives one developer's apps on your device, and an installation identifier Unity creates for its ad software in this app.
  • Your IP address, and a coarse location (country or region) derived from it.
  • Device and usage information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, connection type, and which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Purchase history: the ad software can see that an in-app purchase was made in the app (not your payment details, which only Apple handles), which Unity uses for advertising and to measure ads.
  • Diagnostic data about the ad software, such as crashes and performance, used to check that ads display correctly.
  • If you report an ad as inappropriate, the details of that report.

What it is used for

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads. The app lets the ad software personalise ads only while your last choice is yes: where the game asks its own question about ads (see Your choices), your Accept with the Personalised ads switch on; elsewhere the switch on and tracking allowed. Otherwise it tells the ad software not to sell or share your personal information, and, after a Decline or with the switch off, that you do not consent (GDPR consent false). The app can read your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity, and keeping the ad software working.

We do not sell your personal information. The ad software sends the data listed above to Unity, not to us. With Game statistics on, the game itself sends us what that section describes, including which ad offers were shown and tapped, which ads were shown or failed to load, the revenue reported for each ad and the rewards earned, the system version and language, and the country derived from your IP address, with the random install number and never your advertising identifier.

Your choices

When you open the game for the first time, right after its launch screen and before the first level, a welcome screen links the Terms of Use and this policy, with one button, Accept Terms of Use. It is a notice, not a question about ads. Then the game asks its questions, one after the other: about ads (which question depends on where your phone is, read from its time zone, as described below) and, through iOS, whether it may send you reminders. The ad software does not start before the question about ads has an answer. If you update from an earlier version, you see the welcome screen once, and only the questions you have not answered yet. If you own No Ads or the Calm Bundle when the game starts, it asks nothing about ads.

In the European Economic Area (the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, where the law asks for your consent before ad software uses your data to personalise ads, and on a phone whose time zone cannot be read or names no place (such as UTC), the game asks its own question first, "Ads and your data". It says that Magnet Pop (by Noqyris) shows ads through Unity LevelPlay; that Unity Technologies (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange) receives information about your device and this app, and about the ads you see and tap, whatever you answer; that if you accept, it may also use it and, if you allow it, your advertising identifier to tailor the ads to you; and that if you decline, ads still show (unless you bought No Ads), and Unity is told that you do not consent to personalised ads. Its two answers, Accept and Decline, look the same, and it links to this policy. Your answer also sets the Personalised ads switch described below: Decline always turns it off, and Accept turns it on (in the rare case that the game could not read its stored answer just before the question at launch, an Accept there leaves the switch as it was).

  • If you tap Accept, iOS then asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency), unless you have answered that before or your phone lets no apps ask, and then the ad software starts and ads may be personalised. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your consent stands and ads are still shown, but the app gets no advertising identifier (iOS gives it a blank one). You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • If you tap Decline, the ad software starts and shows ads, but it is told that you did not consent (GDPR consent false) and not to sell or share your personal information, and iOS does not ask you about tracking. The optional reward videos stay available.

There, to change your answer at any time, open Settings › Privacy & ads in the app. It opens the same question again; your new answer reaches the ad software at once (or when it starts) and sets the switch the same way. A question you leave unanswered changes nothing; if you never answered it, it is asked again later. If the switch was turned off in another country, an earlier Accept here is applied with the switch off: the ad software is told that you do not consent, until you accept again.

Everywhere else (since version 1.4 also Serbia and the rest of the Western Balkans), the game asks no question of its own about ads. Right after the welcome screen, unless it already has your answer or your phone lets no apps ask, iOS asks whether Magnet Pop may use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). The ad software starts after you answer, whichever answer you give, and ads are shown. At a start where the game cannot read its stored answer about ads, iOS is not asked, the ad software starts without that and ads are not personalised; at a later start where that read works, what this paragraph says applies again. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the app gets no advertising identifier and tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the game, Settings › Privacy & ads explains this and has a Personalised ads switch: turned off, the ad software is told that you do not consent to personalised ads (GDPR consent false) and not to sell or share your personal information, at once if it is running (and in any case before its next start). The switch counts in every country, and your answer to the game's own question about ads (where it is asked, see above) sets it as described there. If you declined that question, your Decline stands here too: while it stands, iOS is not asked, the ad software starts with that Decline and the switch shows off. Turning the switch on removes that Decline (if the game cannot remove it, the switch shows off again the next time you open the page, and you can turn it on again), so where the game asks its own question, it is asked again.

If your phone's time zone changes while the game is open, the game applies the rule of the new place when you return to the game, open Settings › Privacy & ads or are asked a question. If the ad software is already running, it keeps showing ads and is told the new place's choice at that moment; where the game asks its own question and it has no answer yet, the ad software is told that you do not consent and not to sell or share your personal information until you answer it. If the ad software is not running, it does not start there before that answer.

If you declined ads in an earlier version (when declining meant no ads at all), version 1.4 does not ask you again and, while that Decline stands, iOS does not ask you about tracking: it shows ads, with the ad software told that you did not consent and not to sell or share your personal information, and it tells you so once. Your Personalised ads switch starts off. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & ads.

Many Unity ads also show a privacy (shield) icon you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the rights you have (including access and deletion requests under laws such as the GDPR and CCPA), read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity's Cookie & Advertising Policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in its policy: we don't hold that data.

Reporting an ad

If an ad looks inappropriate, open Settings › Report an ad. It opens your mail app with a message to us, addressed and titled, with the app's version and build already filled in. From version 1.4 the message is addressed to support@noqyris.com; in version 1.3 and earlier it is addressed to the developer's own mailbox. Either way, you add what the ad showed and when you saw it. We receive only what you choose to send, which includes your email address, and use it only to look into that ad and to reply. Many ads also carry a privacy (shield) icon you can use to report them to Unity directly.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional. Every power in the game can also be earned by playing.

  • Power packs (Small, Big and Mega Pack) add Super Magnet and Color Pop powers to your inventory. They can be bought more than once.
  • The Welcome Pack is a one-time power pack, offered once per player. On a new install it may be offered again.
  • No Ads, a one-time purchase, removes the banner and the full-screen ads between levels.
  • The Calm Bundle, a one-time purchase, includes everything No Ads does, plus a one-time set of Super Magnet and Color Pop powers.

No Ads perks. With No Ads or the Calm Bundle, the optional bonuses that other players unlock by watching a short video (such as extra magnets, more powers, or skipping a level) become instant: no video is shown and the ad software is not asked for one. The same daily limits apply to these bonuses as to everyone else's. If you own either one when the game starts, the ad software is not started and Settings › Privacy & ads is not shown. If you buy one during a session, no more ads are shown and from the next launch the ad software is not started; in that session Settings › Privacy & ads stays available, and answering the question there can still start it.

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase. Your power inventory is stored on your device, so it is lost if you delete the app. Power packs and the Welcome Pack are used up and cannot be restored. Restoring purchases asks Apple to re-apply No Ads and the Calm Bundle, for example after you reinstall. Because the powers themselves live only on your device, restoring the Calm Bundle on a new install adds its one-time powers again.

Reminders

If you allow notifications (iOS asks when you first open the game, after the question about ads, unless it already has your answer; you can also allow them later in iOS Settings), the app can remind you, now and then, about the daily puzzle, your calm days, your daily gift or the level you were on: at most one a day, and never at night. These are local notifications: the app schedules them on your device and iOS shows them. There is no notification server, and nothing about them is sent to anyone else; if Game statistics are on, they record only that a session began from a tapped reminder, and which kind. Reminders are off until you allow them, and you can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings › Reminders or in iOS Settings › Notifications.

To send a reminder around the time you usually play, the app keeps a short play-time history on your device: the start times (hour and minute) of your last 10 play sessions. It never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device, using the operating system's standard app storage: your level progress and stars; your settings (sound, music, vibration and reminders); your power inventory and a tally of where your powers came from and how many you used (it helps balance the game when we test it, and is never sent anywhere); your daily progress (calm days, the daily gift, the star jar, chapters and the ball finishes you have earned); which one-time purchases you own and the identifiers of delivered purchases, so a purchase is never credited twice; counters used to space out full-screen ads (including how many were shown today), the daily limits on bonuses and the rating prompt; your reminder schedule and play-time history (above); that you have seen the welcome screen; your answer to the question about ads (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, stored by the ad software too, so you aren't asked at every launch) and when it was asked; the Personalised ads switch (set by that answer, or by you in Settings › Privacy & ads elsewhere); your answer to Apple's tracking prompt; and how many magnets you had to spare on your first clear of Today’s Calm on each of the last three days, so the Game Center leaderboard gets the score of the right day. None of this is sent to us, except what Game statistics describes. It is removed if you delete the app.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

From version 1.4 the game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics, and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier and not a device ID, and the app never gives it to Unity LevelPlay (it reaches Cloudflare with the statistics, see below).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, levels and attempts, tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view; each time the game asks the question about ads, the answer that stands after it (or that it was left unanswered); Apple's tracking answer when its prompt appeared; whether a session began from a tapped reminder or the game's link, rating requests, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and wherever the time zone cannot be read or names no place): your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game asks you with its own "Game statistics" card on the main menu, after the first-launch questions, with two equal answers and nothing chosen for you, and sends nothing until you choose "Allow". What happens in the game before you answer waits on your phone and is sent only if you choose "Allow"; "Don't allow" discards it. If your phone's time zone changes while the game is open, the statistics follow the new place from the next start of the game. On a launch that showed the welcome screen the card waits one visit: on a new install it comes the second time you return to the main menu, and after an update from version 1.3 the first time you return to it after playing. If you close the game before that, it comes when the main menu opens at your next start.
  • In other countries (including Serbia): our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. Both are in Settings in every country. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16 (15 in Serbia), turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

Ratings

After you clear some levels, the app may ask Apple to show its standard rating prompt. Apple handles the prompt and any rating you leave.

Game Center

From version 1.3, Magnet Pop has four Game Center leaderboards: Stars (the stars you have earned in the levels, not counting a level you skipped), Longest Chain (your longest chain of pops), Zen (your best Zen score) and Today’s Calm (how many magnets you had to spare on your first clear of the day’s puzzle). Game Center is optional. The game never asks you to sign in on its own: if you are already signed in to Game Center on your device, it uses that, and otherwise Apple’s Game Center sign-in appears only when you tap a board in Settings › Leaderboards. If you are signed in, the game sends those scores to Apple’s leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center name. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy; we don’t receive your Game Center details. If you are not signed in, the game works the same without leaderboards, and you can turn Game Center off at any time in iOS Settings › Game Center.

Network

The puzzles work fully offline. The app uses the network for the ad software once it has started (to load and show ads and report on them, as described above), to send the game statistics (to our own server at noqyris-ingest.noqyris.workers.dev, over HTTPS), to make or restore purchases through Apple, for Apple's rating prompt, and for Game Center if you are signed in to it. Links to this policy and the terms open in your browser. Reminders are scheduled on your device and need no network. An ad report goes through your own mail app, never through the app.

Children

Magnet Pop is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, or believes a child's information has been collected through the ads in this app, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone deletes them.

Version 1.3 and earlier

If you have not updated to 1.4 yet, these things are different.

  • No game statistics. Versions before 1.4 send us nothing at all, and have no Game statistics or Delete my data rows.
  • No welcome screen and no question of ours at launch. The app's own question about ads never comes when you open the app (iOS may ask about tracking then, if you accepted earlier and iOS has no answer yet). Until you have answered the app's question, "Ads and your data" (with equal answers; there, a Decline means no ads), the ad software does not start. The question comes, in every country, at one of these moments only: when you move on from a finished level (normally your third), in place of a break between levels; when you leave Zen mode after playing it for two minutes in that session; when you tap an offer to watch a short video for a bonus (the video then plays if you accept); and whenever you open Settings › Privacy & ads. After Accept, iOS asks about tracking (unless it already has your answer or your phone lets no apps ask) and the ad software starts. Once you have accepted, the ad software starts every time you open the app (unless you own No Ads or the Calm Bundle), right after its launch screen, with your stored answer and without asking you again.   • A Decline means no ads. The ad software is not started, no ad is requested and no ads are shown, including the optional reward videos, and iOS does not ask you about tracking. Your refusal is kept on your device, and the ad software is not started. After a Decline the app asks again only when you tap an offer to watch a video yourself (at most once every 180 days) or open Settings › Privacy & ads. If you withdraw consent in Settings › Privacy & ads after ads have started, the app stops showing ads and, from the next launch, does not start the ad software.
  • Reminders are offered in the game's own card after an early win, not at the first launch.
  • Report an ad addresses its message to the developer's own mailbox, not to support@noqyris.com (see Reporting an ad).
