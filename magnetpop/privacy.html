Privacy Policy

Magnet Pop · Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Applies to Magnet Pop 1.4 and later; Version 1.3 and earlier says what is different before 1.4 · Terms of Use

Magnet Pop ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). There are no Magnet Pop accounts. The game sends us statistics about how it is played, without your name, so we can improve it (see Game statistics); in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland only if you allow it, and everywhere you can turn it off in the game. The app also shows ads, and the ad service it uses collects some data from your device. If you use Apple's Game Center, your scores go to Apple's leaderboards. This policy explains what, why, and how you control it.

Advertising

The app shows a banner ad on the menu screens (never while a board is in play), an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and short videos you can choose to watch for a reward, such as extra magnets, more powers, or skipping a level you are stuck on. Levels 1 to 5 have no banner and no full-screen ads.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies (LevelPlay was formerly part of ironSource, now owned by Unity), with ads from Unity's own ad networks, Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange. The ad software is built into the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives one developer's apps on your device, and an installation identifier Unity creates for its ad software in this app.
  • Your IP address, and a coarse location (country or region) derived from it.
  • Device and usage information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, connection type, and which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Purchase history: the ad software can see that an in-app purchase was made in the app (not your payment details, which only Apple handles), which Unity uses for advertising and to measure ads.
  • Diagnostic data about the ad software, such as crashes and performance, used to check that ads display correctly.
  • If you report an ad as inappropriate, the details of that report.

What it is used for

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads: only with your tracking permission for the advertising identifier; in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland only with your consent; and never while the Personalised ads switch is off.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity, and keeping the ad software working.

We do not sell your personal information, and we receive none of the data listed above, except what Game statistics describes: which ad offers were shown and tapped, which ads were shown or failed to load, the revenue reported for each ad shown and the rewards earned, with the random install number and never your advertising identifier.

Your choices

When you open the game for the first time, right after its launch screen and before the first level, a welcome screen links the Terms of Use and this policy, with one button, Accept. It is a notice, not a question about ads. Then the game asks its questions, one after the other: about ads (which question depends on where your phone is, read from its time zone, as described below) and, through iOS, whether it may send you reminders. The ad software does not start before the question about ads has an answer. If you update from an earlier version, you see the welcome screen once, and only the questions you have not answered yet.

In the European Economic Area (the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway), the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and wherever the time zone cannot be read), where the law asks for your consent before ad software uses your data to personalise ads, the game asks its own question first, "Ads and your data". It says that Magnet Pop (by Noqyris) shows ads through Unity LevelPlay; that Unity Technologies (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange) receives information about your device and this app and which ads you see and tap, to show the ads; that if you accept, it may also use this and, if you allow it, your advertising identifier to tailor the ads to you; and that if you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. Its two answers, Accept and Decline, look the same, and it links to this policy.

  • If you tap Accept, iOS then asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency), and then the ad software starts and ads may be personalised. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown, but your advertising identifier is not shared, so ads cannot be personalised with it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • If you tap Decline, the ad software starts and shows ads, but it is told that you did not consent (GDPR consent false) and not to sell or share your personal information, so the ads you see are not personalised, and iOS does not ask you about tracking. The optional reward videos stay available.

There, to change your answer at any time, open Settings › Privacy & ads in the app. It opens the same question again, and your new answer reaches the ad software at once.

Everywhere else (since version 1.4 also Serbia and the rest of the Western Balkans), the game asks no question of its own about ads. Right after the welcome screen, iOS asks whether Magnet Pop may use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). The ad software starts after you answer, whichever answer you give, and ads are shown. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared and the game tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information, so ads are not personalised with it. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the game, Settings › Privacy & ads explains this and has a Personalised ads switch: turned off, the ad software is told that you do not consent to personalised ads (GDPR consent false) and not to sell or share your personal information, so the ads you see are not personalised.

If you declined ads in an earlier version (when declining meant no ads at all), version 1.4 does not ask you again: it shows ads that do not track you, with the ad software told that you did not consent and not to sell or share your personal information, and it tells you so once. Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your Personalised ads switch starts off. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & ads.

Many Unity ads also show a privacy (shield) icon you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the rights you have (including access and deletion requests under laws such as the GDPR and CCPA), read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity's Cookie & Advertising Policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in its policy: we don't hold that data.

Reporting an ad

If an ad looks inappropriate, open Settings › Report an ad. It opens your mail app with a message to us at support@noqyris.com, addressed and titled, with the app's version and build already filled in; you add what the ad showed and when you saw it. We receive only what you choose to send, which includes your email address, and use it only to look into that ad and to reply. Many ads also carry a privacy (shield) icon you can use to report them to Unity directly.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional. Every power in the game can also be earned by playing.

  • Power packs (Small, Big and Mega Pack) add Super Magnet and Color Pop powers to your inventory. They can be bought more than once.
  • The Welcome Pack is a one-time power pack, offered once per player. On a new install it may be offered again.
  • No Ads, a one-time purchase, removes the banner and the full-screen ads between levels.
  • The Calm Bundle, a one-time purchase, includes everything No Ads does, plus a one-time set of Super Magnet and Color Pop powers.

No Ads perks. With No Ads or the Calm Bundle, the optional bonuses that other players unlock by watching a short video (such as extra magnets, more powers, or skipping a level) become instant: no video is shown and the ad software is not asked for one. The same daily limits apply to these bonuses as to everyone else's. If you buy either one before the ad software has started, it is not started at all. If it was already running, no more ads are shown, and from the next launch it is not started. If the ad software has ever run on your device, Settings › Privacy & ads stays available to change your choice.

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase. Your power inventory is stored on your device, so it is lost if you delete the app. Power packs and the Welcome Pack are used up and cannot be restored. Restoring purchases asks Apple to re-apply No Ads and the Calm Bundle, for example after you reinstall. Because the powers themselves live only on your device, restoring the Calm Bundle on a new install adds its one-time powers again.

Reminders

If you allow notifications (iOS asks when you first open the game, after the question about ads), the app can remind you, now and then, about the daily puzzle, your calm days, your daily gift or the level you were on: at most one a day, and never at night. These are local notifications: the app schedules them on your device and iOS shows them. There is no notification server, and nothing about them is sent to anyone else; if Game statistics are on, they record only that a session began from a tapped reminder, and which kind. Reminders are off until you allow them, and you can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings › Reminders or in iOS Settings › Notifications.

To send a reminder around the time you usually play, the app keeps a short play-time history on your device: the start times (hour and minute) of your last 10 play sessions. It never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device, using the operating system's standard app storage: your level progress and stars; your settings (sound, music, vibration and reminders); your power inventory and a tally of where your powers came from and how many you used (it helps balance the game when we test it, and is never sent anywhere); your daily progress (calm days, the daily gift, the star jar, chapters and the ball finishes you have earned); which one-time purchases you own and the identifiers of delivered purchases, so a purchase is never credited twice; counters used to space out full-screen ads (including how many were shown today), the daily limits on bonuses and the rating prompt; your reminder schedule and play-time history (above); that you have seen the welcome screen; your answer to the question about ads (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, stored by the ad software too, so you aren't asked at every launch) and when it was asked; the Personalised ads switch (elsewhere); your answer to Apple's tracking prompt; and how many magnets you had to spare on your first clear of Today’s Calm on each of the last three days, so the Game Center leaderboard gets the score of the right day. None of this is sent to us, except what Game statistics describes. It is removed if you delete the app.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

From version 1.4 the game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; they are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier, not a device ID, and it is never given to anyone, including our ad network (Unity LevelPlay).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, levels and attempts, tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, your answers to the ads question and to iOS's tracking prompt, whether a session began from a tapped reminder or the game's link, rating requests, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and wherever the time zone cannot be read): your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game asks you with its own "Game statistics" card, the first time you come back to the main menu after playing, with two equal answers and nothing chosen for you, and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow".
  • In other countries (including Serbia): our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. Both are in Settings in every country. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16 (15 in Serbia), turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

Ratings

After you clear some levels, the app may ask Apple to show its standard rating prompt. Apple handles the prompt and any rating you leave.

Game Center

From version 1.3, Magnet Pop has four Game Center leaderboards: Stars (the stars you have earned in the levels, not counting a level you skipped), Longest Chain (your longest chain of pops), Zen (your best Zen score) and Today’s Calm (how many magnets you had to spare on your first clear of the day’s puzzle). Game Center is optional. The game never asks you to sign in on its own: if you are already signed in to Game Center on your device, it uses that, and otherwise Apple’s Game Center sign-in appears only when you tap a board in Settings › Leaderboards. If you are signed in, the game sends those scores to Apple’s leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center name, and your friends can challenge you on Today’s Calm in Apple’s Games app. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy; we don’t receive your Game Center details. If you are not signed in, the game works the same without leaderboards, and you can turn Game Center off at any time in iOS Settings › Game Center.

Network

The puzzles work fully offline. The app uses the network only to load ads (once the ad software has started, as described above), to send the game statistics (to our own server at noqyris-ingest.noqyris.workers.dev, over HTTPS), to make or restore purchases through Apple, for Apple's rating prompt, and for Game Center if you are signed in to it. Reminders are scheduled on your device and need no network. An ad report goes through your own mail app, never through the app.

Children

Magnet Pop is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, or believes a child's information has been collected through the ads in this app, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone deletes them.

Version 1.3 and earlier

If you have not updated to 1.4 yet, three things are different.

  • No game statistics. Versions before 1.4 send us nothing at all.
  • No welcome screen and no questions at launch. In every country, the ad software does not start when you open the app, and nothing asks you about ads at launch. The app asks its question, "Ads and your data" (with equal answers; there, a Decline means no ads), at one of these moments only: when you move on from a finished level (normally your third), in place of a break between levels; when you leave Zen mode after playing it for two minutes in that session; when you tap an offer to watch a short video for a bonus (the video then plays if you accept); and whenever you open Settings › Privacy & ads. After Accept, iOS asks about tracking and the ad software starts.
  • A Decline means no ads. The ad software is not started, no ad is requested and no ads are shown, including the optional reward videos, and iOS does not ask you about tracking. Your refusal is kept on your device; because the ad software is never started, nothing about you is passed to it. The app asks again only if you tap an offer to watch a video yourself, at most once every 180 days. If you withdraw consent in Settings › Privacy & ads after ads have started, the app stops showing ads and, from the next launch, does not start the ad software. Reminders are offered in the game's own card after an early win.
