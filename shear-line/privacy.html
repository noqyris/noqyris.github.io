Shear Line: Lockpicking Game — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026. Applies to Shear Line version 1.0.2 and later, and to 1.0.1 where this page says how 1.0.1 differs. Version 1.0 sends us no game statistics, asks its own Accept/Decline ad question in every country, and has Privacy & ads and Report an ad directly in Settings.

Overview

Shear Line is a lockpicking puzzle game. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The game sends us (Noqyris) statistics about how it is played, without your name, so we can improve it (see Game statistics (Noqyris) below). The game also shows ads, and the ad service it uses collects some data from your device. This policy explains what is collected, why, and how you control it.

Advertising

Unless you own Remove Ads, the game can show two kinds of ads: short videos you choose to watch for a stated reward (for example, to see every gate of a lock for a few seconds, to put a jammed pin back, to double the loot from a lock, or to keep your Night Shift streak), and an occasional full-screen ad when you leave the result of a lock you opened. A full-screen ad never interrupts a lock you are picking and never follows a failed attempt; a reward video plays only when you tap to watch it. There is no banner.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange (both Unity Technologies). The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data, such as performance measurements and technical error information about how ads load and display.

What it is used for

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads, only with your consent and, for the advertising identifier, your tracking permission.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity.
  • Keeping the ad software working: finding and fixing problems with how ads load, display and perform.

Your choices

When you first open the game, a welcome card links to the Terms of Use and this policy and has one button, Accept Terms of Use. Right after it you are asked about ads, in a way that depends on where your phone is, read from its time zone. If that moment passes without an answer (for example, iOS does not show its question then), you are asked at once when you tap an offer to watch an ad for a reward, and only in a later session when you leave a level you won. Version 1.0.1 has no welcome card and does not ask when it opens: you are first asked after you finish your first level, or when you tap an ad offer.

In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

No ad is shown until you answer. The game's own question says who shows the ads (Shear Line, by Noqyris, through Unity LevelPlay), what Unity Technologies (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange) receives and why, and what declining means. Its two answers, Accept and Decline, look the same. The same applies when the phone's time zone cannot be read. Up to version 1.0.1 this question was also asked in the rest of Europe (for example Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Ukraine and Turkey); since 1.0.2 these countries follow "Everywhere else" below.

  • Accept: iOS then asks whether Shear Line may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency), and then the ad software starts. Unity may personalise ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown, but your advertising identifier is not shared, so ads cannot be personalised with it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Decline: the ad software still starts and ads are still shown, including the optional reward videos, but they are not personalised: the game tells the ad service that you did not consent and not to sell or share your personal information, and iOS's tracking question is not shown. Up to version 1.0.1, Decline meant that the ad software was not started at all: no ads and no ad offers for 180 days; after that, tapping an ad offer led to the question again.

To change your answer, open Settings → Privacy & data → Privacy & ads at any time; your new answer applies from then on. Up to version 1.0.1, withdrawing consent after ads had started stopped the ads. Your answer reaches us only as part of the game statistics, if they are on (see below).

Everywhere else

The game asks no question of its own about ads. iOS asks its tracking question (App Tracking Transparency), and the ad software starts once you have answered it, whichever answer you give. With Allow, Unity may use your advertising identifier to personalise ads. With Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown, your advertising identifier is not shared, and the game tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information. If iOS does not show its question at the first moment, a short notice, "One question first", comes up for a moment before it, with no buttons: at once when you tap an offer to watch an ad, and in a later session when you leave a level you won. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (or iOS Settings → Shear Line); the game reads it again each time you come back to it. Settings → Privacy & data → Privacy & ads in the game explains this and leads to iOS Settings. Up to version 1.0.1, a short screen with one button, Continue, came before iOS's question; and a player who had declined the game's own question in an earlier version got no ads unless they later tapped an ad offer and went on through iOS's question.

If you declined in version 1.0.1 or earlier: from version 1.0.2 the game shows you ads that are not personalised, as after a Decline, and asks you nothing new. A short message tells you so once: "Ads keep Shear Line free; they don't track you." The same message is shown once to anyone who declines in version 1.0.2 or later.

Report an ad: if an ad looks inappropriate or wrong for the game's age rating, tap Settings → Privacy & data → Report an ad. It opens your mail app with a pre-written message to support@noqyris.com, including the game's version; you decide what to send.

If you own Remove Ads, the ad software is not started once the game knows about your purchase on this device, and the optional reward perks are yours without watching anything. After you reinstall the game or move to a new device, tap Restore in the store or in Settings so the game knows again.

Many Unity ads also show a privacy (shield) icon that you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the choices you have, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in its policy: we don't hold that data.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

The game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; they are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier, not a device ID, and it is never given to anyone, including our ad network (Unity LevelPlay).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, levels and attempts, tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS or Android), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

  • In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The game asks you on its own "Game statistics" card, after the ad question, and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow".
  • In other countries: our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.
  • Up to version 1.0.1 the game also asked first in the rest of Europe (for example Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Ukraine and Turkey), on the basis of consent (in Serbia: Personal Data Protection Act Art. 12, Electronic Communications Act Art. 160); since 1.0.2 these countries are among the other countries.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "DELETE MY DATA" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16 (15 in Serbia), turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional: Remove Ads, the Pro Pick Set, the Starter Kit, the Locksmith Bundle, loot packs, the Rescue Kit and the Loot Stash. Purchases are processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase. What you own is remembered on your device, and Restore (in the store and in Settings) asks Apple to re-apply your one-time purchases, for example after you reinstall. Loot you buy is stored on your device and never expires, but like the rest of your progress it is lost if you delete the app.

Reminders (local notifications)

Shear Line can send at most one reminder a day, for example when tonight's Night Shift lock is ready or your streak is about to end. iOS asks once, when you first open the game, right after the ad question, whether Shear Line may send notifications; nothing is sent unless you allow them. Up to version 1.0.1 the game asked iOS for permission only after you tapped to turn reminders on. Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS; nothing is sent to us or to a push server. To pick a good time, the game remembers on your device the hours at which you usually play. You can turn reminders off at any time in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Shear Line.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your progress: locks opened, stars and best times, jobs and answers, loot, cosmetics, the Night Shift streak, daily missions, the Vault Run and play statistics.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, visual cues and reminders, and the hours you usually play (used only to time reminders).
  • Which purchases you own, and the purchase transactions already credited, so loot is never granted twice.
  • Counters used to space out ads, such as when the last full-screen ad was shown and how many sessions you have played.
  • Your ad consent answer and when the question was last shown (up to version 1.0.1, so that a refusal was not asked again for 180 days), whether the one-time message about ads was shown, and your first answer to iOS's tracking question.
  • The game statistics' own state: the random install number, your answer about the statistics, and events not yet sent.

None of this is sent to us as it is stored; what the game statistics send is described above. All of it is removed if you delete the app.

Network

The game itself plays fully offline. It uses the network only to load ads (as described above), for purchases and restores through Apple, and to send the game statistics (unless you have turned them off, or, in the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, and, up to version 1.0.1, in the rest of Europe, have not allowed them).

Children's Privacy

Shear Line is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13, and it tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "DELETE MY DATA" button in the game's Settings on the child's phone deletes them.
