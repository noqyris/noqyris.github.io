A spooky 3D adventure in an English village where the dawn won't come.

Need a hand?

For any question, bug report or feedback, email subotic.djo@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. If something went wrong, tell us your device, the chapter and what you were doing.

Common questions

How do I play?
Move with the joystick on the left and drag anywhere else to look around. The round buttons on the right are your torch, your phone's camera and, from chapter 2, poppy seeds. The big button that appears near something you can use (talk, pick up, open) does the action.

I'm stuck.
Tap Sausage the cat at the top right. He gives a gentle hint first, a clearer one if you ask again, and then an arrow to where you need to go.

The bats keep catching me.
Bats see you inside their light on the ground. Hide (bushes, barrels, sheds), wait for the light to pass, then move on. Turning your torch off makes you harder to spot, and a handful of poppy seeds makes a bat stop to count them.

Will my progress be saved?
Yes, automatically at every checkpoint, on your device. It stays through restarts and updates and moves with your device backups. There is no account and nothing is stored online, so deleting the game deletes your progress.

What does the purchase unlock?
Chapter 1 is free. One purchase unlocks chapters 2 to 5 for good, with no ads and nothing else to buy. With Family Sharing it works for your family too.

I bought the full game and it is locked again.
After a reinstall or on a new device, open the main menu, tap Unlock full game, then Restore purchases, while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Which languages are there?
English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Polish. The game follows your device's language; you can change it in Settings.
