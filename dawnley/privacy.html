Privacy Policy

Dawnley: Vampire Mystery · Last updated: 16 October 2026.

Dawnley: Vampire Mystery ("the game") is a single-player adventure game by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

We don't collect any personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It works offline.

What stays on your device

Your progress (chapter, checkpoints, collectibles and the photos you take in the game) and your settings are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. We never receive them. They travel with your device when iOS backs it up or moves it to a new phone. Deleting the game deletes them.

Photos taken in the game are small pictures of the game world, saved inside the game. They are not saved to your photo library and are never uploaded.

Purchases

Chapter 1 is free. Chapters 2–5 are unlocked with one in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple (the App Store). We don't see or store your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. The game only asks the App Store whether the full game is unlocked on your account.

Children

The game is made for players aged 12 and over. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. If you email us, we use your email address only to answer you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
