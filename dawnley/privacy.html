Privacy Policy

Dawnley: Vampire Mystery · Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Dawnley: Vampire Mystery ("the game") is a single-player adventure game by Noqyris (Djordje Subotic). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

The game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it works offline. If you allow it, the game sends us statistics about how it is played, under a random install number (see "Game statistics" below). The game asks you first; nothing is stored or sent before you choose "Allow", and you can turn it off at any time in the game's Settings. We never collect your name, email, advertising identifier or purchases.

What stays on your device

Your progress (chapter, checkpoints, collectibles and the photos you take in the game) and your settings are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. We never receive them. They travel with your device when iOS backs it up or moves it to a new phone. Deleting the game deletes them. Game statistics, if you allow them, are described in the next section.

Photos taken in the game are small pictures of the game world, saved inside the game. They are not saved to your photo library and are never uploaded.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

If you allow it, the game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics; they are not used for advertising and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier, not a device ID, and it is never given to anyone.
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, chapter steps and attempts (how often you were caught), tutorial steps, whether the unlock screen was shown, tapped or closed (never whether you bought), the review prompt, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS), system version, language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases.

Legal basis

Your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), Serbian Personal Data Protection Act Art. 12, Electronic Communications Act Art. 160). The game asks you in every country before anything is sent, and stores or sends nothing until you choose "Allow". "Don't allow" changes nothing in the game.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings → Privacy, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16 (15 in Serbia), turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

Purchases

Chapter 1 is free. Chapters 2–5 are unlocked with one in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple (the App Store). We don't see or store your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. The game only asks the App Store whether the full game is unlocked on your account. Purchases are never part of the game statistics.

Children

Dawnley: Vampire Mystery is made for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings (Privacy) on the child's phone deletes them.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. Its statistics are our own (Noqyris), processed for us by Cloudflare as described above. If you email us, we use your email address only to answer you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
