Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Warpkey ("the game") is in testing and is not yet on the App Store. This page describes the test version. It collects no personal data: there are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising software, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Nothing is sent to us, and the game has no server.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps which levels you have finished and the fewest portal trips each one took, locally on your device only, using the operating system's standard storage. It is removed if you delete the game.

Network

The game does not connect to the internet. It plays the same in airplane mode. The links to this page and to the Terms of Use open in a browser window.

Advertising and purchases

The test version contains no advertising software and contacts no ad network. Where a test build shows an ad or a purchase, it is a placeholder drawn by the game itself and labelled as fake: no ad is requested, nothing is bought and no money is taken.

A test build with placeholder ads also asks the questions the released game will ask: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland a question about personalised ads, and on iPhone and iPad Apple's own "Allow tracking?" prompt. In the test version nothing uses your answers: the game reads no advertising identifier and sends nothing anywhere.

Before the game is released with real ads or a real purchase, this page will be replaced by one that names every company that receives data, what it receives and the choices you have.

Children

The game asks for no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new date.

Contact
