Warpkey

Take the key. Now get back.

A puzzle game with one whole room on the screen. Walk through a portal, take the key and bring it to the gate. Taking the key changes the room, and every pair of portals works a set number of times, so the way back is a different puzzle from the way in. Nothing hurts you and nothing is timed: you plan, you walk, and if you end in a dead end you start the room again.

The game is in testing and is not on the App Store yet.

How to play

  • ◀ ▶ under your left thumb walk, ▲ under your right thumb jumps.
  • A portal takes you to the portal of the same colour.
  • IN and OUT: a one-way pair. IN takes you in; OUT never takes you back.
  • A number on a pair is how many trips it has left. At zero it is dead and you can walk through it.
  • When you take the key, red cracked blocks break and dashed gold blocks turn solid.
  • The big gate opens only with the key. ↺ starts the room again.
