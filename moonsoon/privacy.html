Privacy Policy

Moonsoon is designed to be private by default.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Moonsoon ("we", "the app") helps you track your sleep and understand the sounds you make at night. We designed Moonsoon to be private by default.

The short version

  • Moonsoon never uploads your audio recordings or your sleep data. Night-sound clips are recorded and classified on your device, and Moonsoon never uploads a clip. They stay in the app's local storage for your 7 most recent nights and are then deleted automatically — except a clip you choose to keep, which stays until you stop keeping it or delete your data; if clips take up a lot of space, older nights' clips may be removed sooner, but never last night's or a kept one. The clips you choose to keep are part of your device's own backup (iCloud or a computer, if you use one), so a device restored from it still plays them; every other audio clip is kept out of it. You can delete them yourself at any time: one recording, one night, or everything.
  • "Show what you said" is optional and on-device. Only if you ask for it, sleep-talk clips are turned into text on your device with Apple's on-device speech recognition — never an online service. The text is stored with its night on your device — and, like the rest of your sleep data, in your device's own backup if you use one — stays after that night's audio is deleted automatically, and is removed with its recording or its night when you delete either, with all your data, or when you turn the feature off.
  • Ask Moonsoon is optional and answered on your device. Only when you ask it something, Apple Intelligence — Apple's language model, running on your device — works out the answer from what Moonsoon looks up in your nights: never your notes or your answers in Things to mention to your doctor, and the words of your sleep talk only when you ask what you said. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and the conversation is never saved. If you ask by voice, what you say is turned into text on your device with Apple's on-device speech recognition — the sound is never kept or sent — and answers you choose to hear are read aloud by your device.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We use a small number of third parties (below) for purchases and optional Apple Health / Health Connect sync (writing the nights you track and, on iPhone, reading the sleep stages your Apple Watch or another wearable recorded and your Apple Watch's overnight vitals for them) — and, only if you turn them on, tag suggestions from what you log in Apple Health, which Moonsoon reads and never stores.
  • A backup you make in Settings → Advanced → Your nights is a file only you hold: your nights' numbers, notes and tags, which clips you kept, and your answers in Things to mention to your doctor — no audio (not even a kept clip's), nothing read from Apple Health, and the words from your sleep talk only if you choose to include them (a tag you added from an Apple Health suggestion is your own tag and goes in like any other, never the workout, caffeine or drink behind it). Moonsoon never sees or stores it anywhere else. Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer, if you use one) includes Moonsoon's local database and settings, as it does any app's data — your nights, which clips you kept, any words from "Show what you said", your answers in Things to mention to your doctor and what Moonsoon read from Apple Health for them, and which Apple Health tag suggestions you added or dismissed (never what they were read from) — and the clips you choose to keep, compressed, so a device restored from it still plays them. Every other audio clip is kept out of it. Moonsoon never uploads a clip: audio leaves your device only if you share a clip or a video of your night yourself or, for a clip you keep, in your device's own backup.
  • A spreadsheet you export there (CSV) is the same kind of file: one row per night with its times, scores, snoring, breathing pauses, tags and how rested you felt — your notes only if you switch them on for that file. It never carries audio, the words from your sleep talk, your answers in Things to mention to your doctor, or anything read from Apple Health (a tag you added from an Apple Health suggestion is your own tag, like any other). Moonsoon never sees it or keeps a copy.
  • Moonsoon shows no ads (from version 1.2.0), and it has no advertising or tracking software in it. Version 1.1.x could show an optional ad you chose to watch: see Version 1.1.x below.

We do not collect your name, email or account — Moonsoon has no login.

If you contact support from Settings → Help & support → Contact support, your email app sends us your message and your email address. Only if you switch on Include diagnostics does it also carry the app and iOS version, iPhone model, your plan, the number of tracked nights, notification status, recent error messages and, on iPhone and iPad, the battery and performance figures iOS measured for Moonsoon over the last week, one line a day: CPU time, time running in the background with audio (which shows roughly how long Moonsoon listened, or played a sleep sound, that day), disk writes, launch time, hangs and how often iOS closed it in the background. Never audio, recordings or what a night held — its sounds, words, scores, stages or notes. You see all of it before you send. We use it only to answer you. Those figures are kept on your device for two weeks and Moonsoon sends them nowhere else.

Reminders and summaries (bedtime nudges, the weekly and monthly recaps) are scheduled on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to a server, and you can turn each one off in Settings → Sleep → Reminders & summaries. The Sleep Focus reminder is off until you turn it on in Settings → Advanced → Sleep Focus reminder and add Moonsoon's filter to your Sleep Focus; then its one reminder to start your night is made on your device too, when that Focus turns on in the evening. Turn it off there, or remove the filter in iOS's Settings → Focus.

Microphone

Moonsoon only listens while a sleep session is active (you start it manually) — and, in Ask Moonsoon, while you speak a question after tapping its microphone: then only until you stop speaking, for a minute at most, and never while a night or a nap is tracked (see Ask Moonsoon). That sound is never kept. Audio is analyzed on-device, and Moonsoon never uploads a clip: audio leaves your device only if you share a clip or a video of your night yourself or, for a clip you keep, in your device's own backup. You can delete one recording (touch and hold it on its report → Delete this recording), one night (••• at the top of its report → Delete this night), or all recordings and sessions any time from Settings → Privacy → Delete all sleep data.

Deleting one night removes its numbers, every clip from it (kept ones too) and any words from it; deleting one recording removes its audio and keeps only the night's numbers. A night Moonsoon already wrote to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

What you said (speech recognition)

If you tap Show what you said on a report, Moonsoon turns that night's sleep-talk clips into text — only clips you can play, and only after iOS has asked you for permission to use speech recognition. The text is made on your device: Moonsoon uses Apple's on-device speech recognition only, so the audio is not sent to Apple or anyone else. (iOS's own permission prompt describes speech recognition in general, including its online kind, which Moonsoon never uses.) If your device can't recognise your language on the device, Moonsoon says so and makes no text. Ask Moonsoon uses the same on-device speech recognition for a question you speak (see Ask Moonsoon); iOS asks you about it the first time you use either.

The text is stored with its night in the app's local database. It is kept after the clip's audio is deleted automatically — a short line of text is the part of an old night worth keeping — and it is deleted with its recording (Delete this recording), with its night (Delete this night), with Delete all sleep data, or all at once when you turn off Settings → Privacy → Show what you said. Moonsoon never sends it anywhere: it leaves your device only in your device's own backup, like the rest of your nights, in a picture you share — of a night, of one quote or of your month — if you add it, or in a video of a night you share if you leave it in, each after a confirmation that says so, or in a backup file you make if you switch the words on for it.

Your partner's night

If you choose My partner under Listening to on the Tonight screen before Start Sleep, Moonsoon listens to your partner's night instead of yours, with your device on their side of the bed. A partner's night is recorded and analysed on your device like any night: its clips and numbers are kept with your nights (so, like them, in your device's own backup), and Moonsoon never uploads them. Moonsoon doesn't transcribe it — "Show what you said" is never offered for it, and none of its clips is ever turned into text — and it never writes it to Apple Health, never adds your Apple Watch's sleep stages or vitals to it, and keeps it out of your own trends, experiments, monthly recap, Siri answers and doctor PDF. Moonsoon never makes a video of it: sharing their night means a picture of their snoring, or one of its clips. A recording of someone is about them: ask the person first, and before you share a picture or a clip of their night.

Ask Moonsoon (Apple Intelligence)

Ask Moonsoon (Premium and Lifetime) answers questions about your own nights with Apple Intelligence — Apple's language model, running on your device (an iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or an iPad with an M1 or A17 Pro chip or newer, on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on). When you ask, Moonsoon looks up only what the question needs and hands it to that model: the numbers your reports show (scores, snoring, sounds, breathing pauses — and, if you connected Apple Health, the sleep stages from your Apple Watch or another wearable whose app writes them there, and your Apple Watch's overnight vitals), your tags, your experiments, your snoring plan, your sleep goal and bedtimes. It never hands over your notes, your answers in Things to mention to your doctor or to your snoring plan's question about your nose, any audio, or anything else from Apple Health; the words Moonsoon read from your sleep talk only when you ask what you said, with Show what you said on, and only from your own nights. Of a night you tracked for your partner it hands over only what that night's report says about their snoring.

Asking by voice, and hearing the answers (optional). If you tap the microphone beside the question field, Moonsoon turns what you say into the question's text with Apple's on-device speech recognition only — never an online service; where your device can't recognise the conversation's language on the device, it says so and listens to nothing. The first time, Moonsoon says this in one sentence before iOS asks you to allow the microphone and speech recognition. The sound is never kept, written to storage or sent anywhere: it goes from the microphone to the recognizer on your device and is gone. The text appears in the question field for you to check and send, and then lives like a typed question. The microphone closes when you stop speaking, after a minute at most, or when you leave the chat or the app, and it never opens while a night or a nap is being tracked, during a wind-down or while an alarm rings (one that starts to ring closes it). If you turn on Read answers aloud, your device reads each answer aloud with its own voice — nothing is recorded or sent; while VoiceOver is on, VoiceOver reads them instead.

Nothing of this is sent to us or to any third party: the model runs on your device, and Moonsoon has no server. The conversation is kept only in the app's memory — never written to storage, so never in a backup of any kind — and it is gone when Moonsoon is closed (swiped away in the app switcher, or closed by iOS), when you start a new conversation, delete a night or a recording it quoted, delete all your data, turn off Show what you said, or when Premium ends. Ask Moonsoon only offers changes (an alarm time, an experiment, a tag on a night); nothing changes until you tap to confirm. Its answers are not medical advice: Moonsoon can't diagnose sleep apnea — a doctor can.

No ads

From version 1.2.0, Moonsoon shows no ads, on any plan, and has no advertising or tracking software in it: no ad network, no ad partners, and nothing that tracks you across other companies' apps or websites. Your recordings and sleep data are never shared with anyone, and Moonsoon never sends them anywhere: they leave your device only if you share them yourself, or — your sleep data and the clips you keep — in your device's own backup. The same goes for the text of what you said.

Version 1.1.x (rewarded ads)

This section is only about Moonsoon 1.1.x on iPhone and iPad, which some devices still run until they update. From version 1.2.0 there are no ads and none of this applies (see No ads above). Moonsoon for Android never showed ads.

  • What it offered: on the free plan, a short rewarded ad you choose to watch — for example to hear one more night-sound clip, use a Premium sleep sound for one night, or open detailed Trends until tomorrow. No banners and no ad you didn't ask for; Premium and Lifetime see none.
  • Your consent first: the first time you tap such an offer, Moonsoon asks whether you allow ads (Continue or No thanks). No ad software starts before you choose Continue. Only after that, iOS may show Apple's own "Allow tracking" prompt (App Tracking Transparency); you can say no to it and still watch the ad.
  • Who shows the ads, and what they collect: Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads. They use what they collect to pick, deliver, measure and protect ads and to improve their software; Unity links some of it to your device. According to their answers to Apple's privacy questions:
    • ironSource (LevelPlay): your device's advertising identifier, product interaction, advertising data and performance data.
    • Unity Ads: coarse location (your country), a user ID, your device's advertising identifier, purchase history, product interaction, advertising data, other usage data, performance data (such as how fast the ad software starts) and other data such as your device's language, make, model, screen size and connection type.
    The advertising identifier is used for tracking only if you allow it in that prompt. None of this includes your recordings, your sleep data or anything from Apple Health. See Unity's privacy policy.
  • How to withdraw: in 1.1.x, turn ads off in Settings → Privacy → Ads & privacy — that withdraws your consent from the ad partners and stops ad requests; change your answer to "Allow tracking" in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; or update to 1.2.0, which has no ad software at all.
  • Report an ad: in 1.1.x, Settings → Privacy → Report an ad (also Help → Contact support) opens an email to us in your own mail app; nothing is sent until you send it. Or write to subotic.djo@gmail.com with what the ad showed and roughly when. We use it only to check the ad, have it blocked and answer you.

Third-party services

  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process purchases and manage your subscription / lifetime entitlement (in-app purchase via StoreKit / Play Billing).
  • Apple Intelligence — the on-device model behind Ask Moonsoon. It runs on your device and is part of iOS; Moonsoon hands it what a question needs and nothing leaves your device through it.
  • Apple HealthKit / Android Health Connect — only if you grant permission; we write the sleep we track (never a night you track for your partner) and, on iPhone, read the sleep stages your Apple Watch — or an Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, Withings or Ultrahuman device, through its app — recorded for the nights you tracked, and the overnight vitals (blood oxygen, respiratory rate, heart rate and Apple's breathing disturbances) your Apple Watch recorded for them. Only if you turn on Suggest tags from Apple Health (Settings → Advanced → Your tags) — which is when iOS asks you — we also read the workouts, caffeine and alcoholic drinks logged around those nights, to suggest their tags. We read no other health data, and Moonsoon never sends what it reads anywhere — not to our servers, not to anyone. The stages and vitals are kept with your nights in the app's local database (so, like them, in your device's own backup), never in a Moonsoon backup file or a shared picture; the vitals go into a doctor PDF only if you leave them in and share it yourself. What tag suggestions read is never stored at all: only a tag you choose to add is kept, as your own tag.

Your choices

  • Decline microphone, speech recognition or Health permissions at any time in system settings.
  • Turn Show what you said off in Settings → Privacy; that deletes every transcript Moonsoon made (your clips stay). If it is already off and some nights still keep words, Delete what you said there does the same. Neither needs speech recognition, or a device that can still do it.
  • Don't use Ask Moonsoon, or end a conversation with New conversation: nothing of it is kept either way.
  • Turn Suggest tags from Apple Health off in Settings → Advanced → Your tags: Moonsoon stops reading for it and forgets every suggestion and every answer (the tags you added stay, as your own).
  • Clear your answers in Things to mention to your doctor (from Your snoring plan, or the Share with your doctor card in Trends) with Clear my answers; Delete all sleep data clears them too.
  • Delete one recording or one night from its report, or everything in Settings → Privacy → Delete all sleep data; uninstalling removes all local data. A night already written to Apple Health / Health Connect stays there until you delete it in that app.
