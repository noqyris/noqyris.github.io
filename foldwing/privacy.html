Foldwing — Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026 (version 1.4.1)

Foldwing is a puzzle game published by Djordje Subotic. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and who else is involved.

What Foldwing itself collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no login and no server of its own. Your progress, your settings and the drawings you save are stored only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app. Nothing you draw is uploaded anywhere. If you tap Share, the image is handed to your device's own share sheet and you choose where it goes — Foldwing does not send it anywhere itself. The daily reminder, if you allow it, is scheduled on your device; no server is involved.

Advertising

Foldwing is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, a service of Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource) that runs an auction between ad networks, including Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange. Foldwing (Noqyris) decides when ads may appear; to show ads, tailor them, measure them and prevent fraud, Unity and the ad networks it works with may collect and process, as independent controllers:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA, only if you allow tracking)
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • approximate location, such as the country, derived from your IP address
  • ad data, such as which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them
  • diagnostic data about how the ad software is running

This data is handled under Unity's privacy policy.

Your consent comes first

Foldwing does not ask about ads when you open it, and no advertising software runs until you say yes. The question appears after you finish your first fold, when you tap an offer to watch an ad, or whenever you open Settings → Privacy & ads. It says who shows the ads, who receives which data and why, and offers two equal answers: Accept and Decline.

If you decline, the advertising software is never started: Foldwing shows no ads and no rewards for watching them; reveals still come from playing — the daily reveal, missions, chapters and streaks — only more slowly. We ask again only if you tap an offer to watch an ad yourself, and not more than once every six months. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads; withdrawing takes effect at once and stops the banner and every further ad request.

On iOS, only after you accept does Foldwing show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline that, your advertising identifier is not shared; ads you have agreed to may still be shown, but they are less relevant to you.

Reporting an ad

If you see an ad that is inappropriate or not suitable for the game's audience, tap Settings → Report an ad. It opens an email to us with the app's version filled in; tell us what the ad showed and roughly when, so we can have it blocked. Nothing is sent until you send it.

Purchases

Foldwing offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase and packs of reveals. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the app never sees your payment details.

Remove Ads stops every ad for good: no banners, no interstitials, and the game no longer offers reward videos — reveals become unlimited and skips are free. If you agreed to ads, the advertising software may still start with the app after the purchase, and Unity LevelPlay processes data as described above whenever it does.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your Daily Fold time and the achievements you earn are sent to Game Center under Apple's privacy terms. Game Center is optional; the game works fully without it.

Children

Foldwing is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Change your ad consent in the game: Settings → Privacy & ads.
  • Report an ad: Settings → Report an ad.
  • Turn off tracking at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop every ad in the game.
  • Delete the app to erase everything it has stored on your device.
