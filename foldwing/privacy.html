Foldwing — Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026 (every version; the Changes section at the end says what versions before 1.6 do differently)

Foldwing is a puzzle game published by Djordje Subotic. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and who else is involved.

What Foldwing itself collects

Foldwing has no account and no login. Your progress, your settings and the drawings you save are stored only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app. Nothing you draw is uploaded anywhere. If you tap Share, the image is handed to your device's own share sheet and you choose where it goes — Foldwing does not send it anywhere itself. The daily reminder, if you allow it, is scheduled on your device; no server is involved.

The one thing Foldwing itself sends, from version 1.6, is the game statistics described in the next section, to our own server. You can turn them off, and delete them, in the game: Settings → Privacy & data (Settings is the ••• button at the top right of the home screen).

Game statistics (Noqyris)

From version 1.6, the game sends us statistics about how it is played, so we can see where players get stuck or stop, what is too hard and what does not work. Only we (Noqyris) make and read these statistics, to improve the game, and we do not sell them.

What data

  • A random install number: a random ID the game creates on your phone. It is not the advertising identifier and not a device ID. The game never gives it to our ad network (Unity LevelPlay); it goes only to our own server, which Cloudflare runs for us (below).
  • How you play: start and end of play, screens, each maze you start and finish or leave (with the number of strokes and stars), tutorial steps, ad offers and ad views with revenue per view, each time our question about ads is asked the answer that stands after it (or that none was given) and a change of that answer, your answer to Apple's tracking prompt when the prompt appears, your answer to iOS's notification question each time the game asks for it and where it was asked, rating prompts, store offers shown or tapped, streaks, whether a reminder opened the game, errors in the game and loading time.
  • Game version, platform (iOS), language and time-zone offset.
  • Country: Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and limit their number; we do not store it and keep only the country derived from it.

We do not send your name, email, phone model, advertising identifier or purchases: whether or what you buy is never part of the statistics.

Legal basis

  • In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland — and wherever your phone's time zone names no place at all: your consent (GDPR and UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), Swiss FADP). When the game starts there, it sends nothing until you choose "Allow". What happens in the game before you answer waits on your phone and is sent only if you choose Allow; Don't allow discards it. The question, "Game statistics", comes with two equal answers, Allow and Don't allow, after the questions of the first launch (below), the next time you come back to the home screen.
  • In other countries: our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can turn statistics off at any time.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the data for us under its data processing agreement. Individual events with the random install number are stored at Cloudflare, possibly outside the EU (USA), under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Daily totals without the install number are stored in the EU.

How long

  • individual events: 3 months, then deleted automatically;
  • daily totals (without the install number, cannot be linked to you): as long as the game exists;
  • the daily limit counter (a hashed form of the install number): 2 days.

How to turn it off and delete

In the game's Settings, under Privacy & data, turn off "Game statistics": from then on the game sends nothing, also after a restart. "Delete my data" turns statistics off, deletes what statistics stored on your phone and asks our server to delete your data. The install number stays on your phone only until the server confirms the deletion. Once the server receives the request:

  • this install's data is no longer shown in the per-player view, right away;
  • the daily totals of the last 8 days are recalculated without it within one day;
  • older daily totals still include it, but only as part of a total (for example, one more player), without the install number;
  • individual events and the deletion mark (which contains the install number) are deleted automatically after 3 months.

If your phone is offline, the game asks again at every later start. Because we do not know who you are, we can find your statistics only through that button in the game; we cannot find or delete them by email. Your game progress stays.

If you are under 16, turn statistics on only with a parent's consent.

Advertising

Foldwing is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, a service of Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource) that runs an auction between ad networks, including Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange. Foldwing (Noqyris) decides when ads may appear; to show ads, tailor them, measure them and prevent fraud, Unity and the ad networks it works with may collect and process, as independent controllers:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA, only if you allow tracking)
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • approximate location, such as the country, derived from your IP address
  • ad data, such as which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them
  • diagnostic data about how the ad software is running

This data is handled under Unity's privacy policy.

When you first open Foldwing

This section and the three below describe version 1.6 and later; versions before 1.6 ask differently (see Changes at the end of this page).

Right after the start screen, once: a welcome card links our Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy, with one button, Accept Terms of Use. It is a notice, not consent to ads. Then come the questions below that apply where you are (read from your phone's time zone) and still have no answer, and then iOS's own question about notifications if iOS has not asked it before, because Foldwing can remind you of the Daily Fold and your streak. When the game starts where our question about ads is asked, no advertising software starts before you have answered it.

In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Here, and wherever your phone's time zone names no place at all, our question about ads says who shows the ads, who receives which data and why, and offers two equal answers: Accept and Decline. Only if you accept does Foldwing show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you accept and then choose "Ask App Not to Track" there, your acceptance stands and ads may still be tailored to you, but the ad software gets no advertising identifier from your phone.

If you decline, you still see ads (unless you bought Remove Ads), but they are not personalised: the advertising software starts with your refusal, Unity and its networks are told you did not consent and not to sell your data, and Apple's prompt is not shown. Your answer also sets the "Personalised ads" switch (below): Decline turns it off and Accept turns it on, and the switch keeps that position when you are in another country. Your advertising identifier is available to the ad software only while tracking is allowed for Foldwing in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (it is allowed there if you chose Allow in Apple's prompt, or switched it on there yourself); declining here does not turn it off. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & data → Privacy & ads ("Change my answer"); a change takes effect at once, and if you leave that question unanswered your earlier answer stays. If the first question is left unanswered, no advertising software starts; if it is already running (it started while your phone's time zone was in another country), it is told you did not consent and not to sell your data, and no ads are shown, from the moment the game sees the new time zone (at the latest before its next ad) until you answer. The game asks again at the next start and, unless you bought Remove Ads, when you finish a fold or tap an offer to watch an ad.

Everywhere else, on iPhone

Foldwing asks no question of its own: Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt comes directly, with no screen of ours in front of it, if iOS has not yet asked on this install and your phone lets apps ask to track. If it could not be shown, it comes at the next start and, unless you bought Remove Ads, when you finish a fold or tap an offer to watch an ad. The advertising software starts once iOS has an answer, whichever it is. (If you declined our question about ads, in a version before 1.6 or in the countries above, Apple's prompt is not shown at launch and the advertising software starts without it; see Personalised ads.) At a start where the game cannot read your earlier answer the same happens, and ads are not personalised; if you had no earlier answer, Apple's prompt comes as above at a later start where that read works. If you allow tracking, the ad software can read your advertising identifier, and ads may be tailored to you unless "Personalised ads" is off; if you do not, the ad software gets no advertising identifier from your phone, and Unity is told not to sell your data. You can change that answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Personalised ads, everywhere

Settings → Privacy & data → Privacy & ads has a "Personalised ads" switch. Turned off, Unity LevelPlay is told you do not consent to personalised ads and not to sell your data; ads still come (unless you bought Remove Ads), but they are not personalised. Turning it on asks first where an answer is missing: where our question about ads is asked (above) it opens, unless you have already accepted it, and elsewhere on iPhone Apple's prompt is shown if iOS has not asked you yet and your phone lets apps ask to track. Your answer to our question about ads (in the countries above) sets this switch too: Accept turns it on, Decline turns it off. So after a Decline, ads are not personalised in any country until you turn the switch on here. If you declined ads in a version of Foldwing before 1.6 (and so had no ads), the update asks you nothing new: your ads (unless you bought Remove Ads) are not personalised and this switch is off until you turn it on, and the game tells you so once.

Reporting an ad

If you see an ad that is inappropriate or not suitable for the game's audience, tap Settings → Privacy & data → Report an ad (in 1.5.0: Settings → Report an ad). It opens an email to us with the app's version filled in; tell us what the ad showed and roughly when, so we can have it blocked. Nothing is sent until you send it.

Purchases

Foldwing offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase and packs of reveals. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the app never sees your payment details, and the game statistics never say whether or what you bought.

Remove Ads stops every ad for good: no banners, no interstitials, and the game no longer offers reward videos — reveals become unlimited and skips are free. If you answered the question about ads (in versions before 1.6: only if you accepted it) or, outside the countries above, Apple's tracking prompt, the advertising software may still start with the app after the purchase, and Unity LevelPlay processes data as described above whenever it does.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your Daily Fold time and the achievements you earn are sent to Game Center under Apple's privacy terms. Game Center is optional; the game works fully without it.

Children

Foldwing is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The 4+ App Store rating describes the game's content, not the audience it is made for. If a parent finds that a child has used the game, please write to us at support@noqyris.com and we will delete whatever we can find. We cannot find game statistics by email; the "Delete my data" button in the game's Settings (Settings → Privacy & data) on the child's phone deletes them.

Your choices

  • Turn game statistics on or off (1.6 and later; 1.5.0 sends none): Settings → Privacy & data → Game statistics.
  • Delete your game statistics (1.6 and later): Settings → Privacy & data → Delete my data.
  • Turn personalised ads off, and (EU, EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland) change your answer about ads: Settings → Privacy & data → Privacy & ads (in 1.5.0: Settings → Privacy & ads, which asks the question about ads again and has no "Personalised ads" switch).
  • Report an ad: Settings → Privacy & data → Report an ad (in 1.5.0: Settings → Report an ad).
  • Turn off tracking at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop every ad in the game.
  • Delete the app to erase everything it has stored on your device. Deleting the app does not delete game statistics already sent: use "Delete my data" first (individual events are deleted automatically after 3 months in any case).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

Versions of Foldwing before 1.6 (up to 1.5.0) differ in these ways. They show no welcome card and send no game statistics. They ask nothing when the game opens, except Apple's prompt for a player who accepted the question about ads but never answered that prompt. The question about ads, with Accept and Decline, comes in every country, when you leave a solved level (once per session while it has no answer, never after Remove Ads) or when you tap an offer to watch an ad, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt comes only after you accept it, in every country. After a Decline they show no ads at all, and ask again only when you tap an offer to watch an ad, at most once in 180 days. They ask about notifications only when you turn reminders on ("Remind me each day" on the streak card, "Remind me" on the card after a solved level, or the Reminders switch in Settings), not when the game first opens. They have no "Personalised ads" switch, and may show a banner ad on the menus. In those versions Privacy & ads (where you change your answer about ads) and Report an ad are rows directly in the game's Settings, not on a Privacy & data page.
