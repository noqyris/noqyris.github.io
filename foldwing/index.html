Foldwing: Mirror Maze Puzzle — Support

Foldwing is a mirror maze puzzle for iPhone by Noqyris. You draw one line from the dot to the ring, and it is mirrored into a second maze with different walls, so one stroke has to clear both. 300 mazes in 15 chapters, and a new Daily Fold every day. You can also play today's fold in your browser.

Contact

Questions, problems and ideas: support@noqyris.com. Please say which iPhone you use and the version shown at the bottom of Settings (the ••• button on the home screen).

Common questions

  • My purchase is missing. Tap Restore purchases, signed in to the Apple Account that bought it. In version 1.6 and later it is at the bottom of the Store (Settings, then Store, or the Store tile on the home screen); in 1.5.0 it is directly in Settings. Remove Ads comes back. Reveal packs are used up as you spend them and are not restored.
  • I bought reveals and still see ads. Any purchase stops the full-screen breaks between folds; videos you choose to watch for a reward stay. Version 1.5.0 may also show a banner on the menus, which only Remove Ads takes away. From 1.6 there is no banner.
  • How do I turn off personalised ads? In 1.6 and later: Settings, then Privacy & data, then Privacy & ads, and turn "Personalised ads" off. Ads still come (unless you bought Remove Ads), but they are not personalised. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the same page has "Change my answer", which asks the question about ads again; a Decline there also turns "Personalised ads" off, and an Accept turns it on. In 1.5.0: Settings, then Privacy & ads, which asks the question about ads again; there a "no" means no ads.
  • I declined ads in an earlier version. Why do I see ads now? Since 1.6 a "no" means ads that are not personalised, as in most games. "Personalised ads" starts off, and stays off until you turn it on. Your advertising identifier is available to the ad software only while tracking is allowed for Foldwing in iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking; a "no" in the game does not turn that off.
  • I saw an ad that should not be there. In 1.6 and later: Settings, then Privacy & data, then Report an ad. In 1.5.0: Settings, then Report an ad. Or write to the address above and say what the ad showed and roughly when.
  • How do I stop the reminders? Settings, then Reminders. There is at most one a day.
  • How do I turn off game statistics or delete them? Settings, then Privacy & data: "Game statistics" turns them on or off, and "Delete my data" turns them off and asks our server to delete what was sent. Version 1.5.0 sends no statistics.
  • Do I need an internet connection? No. Every maze and the Daily Fold play without one. Ads, purchases and Game Center need a connection.
  • When does the Daily Fold change? At midnight where you are. Only your first finish of the day goes on the Game Center leaderboard.
  • Is my progress backed up? Progress is kept on your device and is removed if you delete the game.

Game statistics (Noqyris)

From version 1.6, Foldwing sends us statistics about how it is played (no name, no email, no advertising identifier, no purchases), so we can improve it. Cloudflare, Inc. processes them for us as our processor. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland nothing is sent before you choose "Allow". The details are in the privacy policy.
