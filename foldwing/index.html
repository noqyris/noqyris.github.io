Foldwing: Mirror Maze Puzzle — Support

Foldwing is a mirror maze puzzle for iPhone by Noqyris. You draw one line from the dot to the ring, and it is mirrored into a second maze with different walls, so one stroke has to clear both. 300 mazes in 15 chapters, and a new Daily Fold every day. You can also play today's fold in your browser.

Contact

Questions, problems and ideas: subotic.djo@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone you use and the version shown at the bottom of Settings (the ••• button on the home screen).

Common questions

  • My purchase is missing. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the Apple Account that bought it. Remove Ads comes back. Reveal packs are used up as you spend them and are not restored.
  • I bought reveals and still see a banner. Any purchase stops the breaks between folds. Only Remove Ads takes the banner away too.
  • How do I change my answer about ads? Settings, then Privacy & ads. The question is asked again and your new answer applies from then on.
  • I saw an ad that should not be there. Settings, then Report an ad, or write to the address above and say what the ad showed and roughly when.
  • How do I stop the reminders? Settings, then Reminders. There is at most one a day.
  • Do I need an internet connection? No. Every maze and the Daily Fold play without one. Ads, purchases and Game Center need a connection.
  • When does the Daily Fold change? At midnight where you are. Only your first finish of the day goes on the Game Center leaderboard.
  • Is my progress backed up? Progress is kept on your device and is removed if you delete the game.
